Questions aspirants ask

What is YESPYQ?

YESPYQ is a practice platform for previous year questions (PYQs) across UPSC, JEE, NEET, SSC CGL, Defence exams and school Boards. Questions are organised subject-wise and year-wise with instant answer keys and detailed explanations.

Which exams does YESPYQ cover?

YESPYQ covers UPSC Civil Services Prelims, JEE (Main & Advanced), NEET, SSC CGL, Defence exams and school Board exams (Class 11 & 12), with more exams being added.

Is YESPYQ free to use?

You can practise free with limited access. PYQ Pass unlocks the complete set of 12,000+ PYQs across every exam with every answer and explanation for ₹149 for one year.

Can I practice PYQs subject-wise and year-wise?

Yes. You can filter questions by subject or by exam year, and attempt focused sets with instant feedback.

Is YESPYQ affiliated with UPSC, NTA or any exam body?

No. YESPYQ is an independent study tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by UPSC, NTA, SSC, IBPS or any other examination authority.
