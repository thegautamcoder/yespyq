Questions, answered

What does PYQ Pass include?

Every PYQ across every exam on YESPYQ: UPSC, JEE, NEET, SSC CGL, Defence and Boards, with the correct answer and a full explanation for each question.

Can I try before I pay?

Yes. Every question and its options are free to see, no account needed. About 10% of the questions in every chapter also include the correct answer and explanation for free — Pass unlocks the answer and explanation for the remaining ~90%.

How long does it last?

12 months from the date of payment. Renewing before expiry extends your existing access rather than restarting it.

Do I need an account?

Yes. PYQ Pass requires signing in with Google so your access is tied to your account, not your device.

Is payment secure?

Yes. Payments are processed by Razorpay. YESPYQ never sees or stores your card, UPI or bank details.

Is this a subscription?

No. It is a one-time payment for one year of access, not a recurring plan. See the Terms for full details.
