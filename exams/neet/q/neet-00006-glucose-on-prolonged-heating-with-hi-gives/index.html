Glucose on prolonged heating with HI gives
a-hexane
b1-hexene
cHexanoic acid
d6-iodohexanal
✓ Correct answer: a) -hexane
ExplanationGlucose on prolonged heating with HI (hydroiodic acid) undergoes a complete reduction reaction.HI is a strong reducing agent that removes all the oxygen atoms (from the aldehyde and hydroxyl groups) and replaces them with hydrogen atoms.This results in the formation of a straight-chain alkane with six carbon atoms, which is n- hexane (C₆H₁₄).
