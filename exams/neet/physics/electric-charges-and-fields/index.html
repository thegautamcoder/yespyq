Q1 FREE PREVIEW

A metal cube of side 5 cm is charged with 6μC. The surface charge density on the cube is :

[Re-NEET 2024]

a

0.125×10-3Cm-2

b

0.25×10-3Cm-2

c

4×10-3Cm-2

d

0.4×10-3Cm-2

✓ Correct answer: d)

0.4×10-3Cm-2

Explanation

In metal all the charge is present on surface.


\(\mathrm{Q}=6 \mu C\)


Total surface area \(S=6 a^2\)


\(\begin{aligned}
& =6 \times\left(5 \times 10^{-2}\right)^2 \\
& =6 \times 25 \times 10^{-4} \\
& =150 \times 10^{-4} \mathrm{~m}^2
\end{aligned}\)


Surface charge density \(\sigma=\frac{Q}{S}\)


\(\begin{aligned}
& =\frac{6 \times 10^{-6}}{150 \times 10^{-4}} \\
& =0.4 \times 10^{-3} \mathrm{Cm}^{-2}
\end{aligned}\)
