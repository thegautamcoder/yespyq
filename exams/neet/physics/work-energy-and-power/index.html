Q1

An object falls from a height of 10 m above the ground. After striking the ground it loses 50% of its kinetic energy. The height upto which the object can rebounce from the ground is:

[Re-NEET 2024]

a

7.5 m

b

10 m

c

2.5 m

d

5 m

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

5 m

Explanation

K.E. just before striking the ground \(K_1=m g h_1=m g(10)\)
K.E. just after striking the ground \(=K_1-\frac{50}{100} K_1=\frac{50}{100} K_1=\frac{K_1}{2}=K_2\)

Now, \(K_2=m g h_2\)

⇒K12=mgh2⇒mg(10)2=mgh2⇒h2=5m

Q3

A particle of mass M moves along a horizontal x-axis from x=0 to x=L. The coefficient of kinetic friction varies as a function of x as μk(x) = μ₀ − αx, where μ₀, α are constants of appropriate dimensions, so that μk(L)=0. The total work done by the frictional force during the motion is nμ₀MgL, where g is the acceleration due to gravity. The value of n is:

[Re-NEET 2026]

a

3

b

1

c

1/3

d

1/2

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

1/2

Explanation

\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
&\begin{aligned}
& \mu_k=\mu_0-\alpha x \\
& \mu_0-\alpha L=0 \Rightarrow \alpha=\frac{\mu_0}{L}
\end{aligned}\\
&\begin{aligned}
& \text { W.D. by kinetic friction }=\int f_k d x \cos 180^{\circ} \\
& =\int_0^L \mu_k \mathrm{mgdx}(-1) \\
& =-\int_0^L\left(\mu_0-\alpha x\right) \mathrm{mgdx} \\
& =-\left[\mu_0 \mathrm{mgL}-\alpha \mathrm{mg} \frac{\mathrm{~L}^2}{2}\right] \\
& =-\left[\mu_0 \mathrm{mgL}-\frac{\mu_0}{\mathrm{~L}} \mathrm{mg} \frac{\mathrm{~L}^2}{2}\right] \\
& =-\left[\frac{\mu_0 \mathrm{mgL}}{2}\right]
\end{aligned}\\
&\text { on comparing magnitude with } \mathrm{n} \mu_0 \mathrm{mgL}\\
&\mathrm{n}=\frac{1}{2}
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}

Q6

An object moving along horizontal x-direction with kinetic energy 10 J is displaced through x=3i^ m by the force F→=(-2i^+3j^)N. The kinetic energy of the object at the end of the displacement x is :

a

10 J

b

16 J

c

4 J

d

6 J

✓ Correct answer: c)

4 J

Explanation

Work energy theorem,

Wall =ΔK⋅E (Wall = work done by all foces)

 ⇒Kf−Ki=F→⋅Δx→

 ⇒Kf−10=−2i^+3j^⋅3i^

Kf−10=−6

Kf=4 J

Q7

An object moving along horizontal x-direction with kinetic energy 10 J is displaced through x=3i^ m by the force F→=(-2i^+3j^)N. The kinetic energy of the object at the end of the displacement x is :

[Re-NEET 2024]

a

10 J

b

16 J

c

4 J

d

6 J

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

4 J

Explanation
  • Force F = (-2î + 3ĵ) N
  • Displacement x = (3î) m
  • Work W = F ⋅ x = (–2î + 3ĵ) ⋅ (3î) = (–2)(3) + (3)(0) = –6 J.
  • Initial KE (Ki) = 10 J.
  • W = Kf – Ki ⇒ –6 J = Kf – 10 J ⇒ Kf = 10 J – 6 J = 4 J.

(NEW NCERT 11th Page No. 73)
