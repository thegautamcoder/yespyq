Q3

Two slits in Young's double slit experiment are 1.5 mm apart and the screen is placed at a distance of 1 m from the slits. If the wavelength of light used is 600×10-9 m then the fringe separation is :

[Re-NEET 2024]

a

4×10-5 m

b

9×10-8 m

c

4×10-7 m

d

4×10-4 m

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

4×10-4 m

Explanation
  • Fringe separation (β) in YDSE = λD / d.
  • β = (600 × 10⁻⁹ m) × (1 m) / (1.5 × 10⁻³ m) = (600 / 1.5) × 10⁻⁶ m = 400 × 10⁻⁶ m = 4 × 10⁻⁴ m.

Q4

An unpolarised light beam strikes a glass surface at Brewster's angle. Then

[NEET 2024]

a

the reflected light will be partially polarised.

b

the reflected light will be completely polarised.

c

both the reflected and refracted light will be completely polarised.

d

the reflected light will be completely polarised but the refracted light will be partially polarised.

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

the reflected light will be completely polarised but the refracted light will be partially polarised.

Explanation

At Brewster’s angle the parallel (p-) component of the electric field is not reflected at all, leaving only the perpendicular (s-) component in the reflected beam. Hence the reflected light is completely plane-polarised, while the transmitted (refracted) beam still contains both components and is therefore only partially polarised.

Q5

In interference and diffraction, the light energy is redistributed. If it reduces in one region, producing a dark fringe, it increases in another region, producing a bright fringe.
A. As there is no gain or loss of energy, these phenomena are consistent with the principle of conservation of energy.
B. Diffraction and interference are characteristics exhibited only by light waves.
Choose the correct answer from the options given below

a

A is true, but B is false

b

A is true and B is also true

c

A is false, but B is true

d

Both A and B are false

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

A is true, but B is false

Explanation

A is true: In interference/diffraction, energy is only redistributed. Dark fringes have less energy and bright fringes have more energy, so conservation of energy is obeyed.

B is false: Interference and diffraction are wave phenomena, not exclusive to light. Sound waves, water waves, etc. also show them.

[NEET 2024]

interference pattern will disappear.

b

there will be a central dark fringe surrounded by a few coloured fringes.

c

there will be a central bright white fringe surrounded by a few coloured fringes.

all bright fringes will be of equal width.

Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

there will be a central bright white fringe surrounded by a few coloured fringes.

Explanation
  • In YDSE with white light, the central fringe (path difference = 0) is white because all colours interfere constructively. Fringes on either side are coloured due to different path differences for different wavelengths.
  • Central bright white fringe surrounded by coloured fringes.

Q10

The intensity of transmitted light when a polaroid sheet, placed between two crossed polaroids at 22.5° from the polarization axis of one of the polaroid is ( I0 is the intensity of polarised light, after passing through the first polaroid):

[NEET 2025]

a

I08

b

I016

c

I02

d

I04

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

I08

Explanation

When light passes through a polaroid at an angle \(\theta\) from the original polarization direction, the transmitted intensity is given by:

\(I=I_0 \times \cos ^2 \theta\)

Since there are two polaroids with one in between at \(22.5^{\circ}\), the intensity after the first polaroid becomes \(I _0\).
After the second polaroid (placed at \(22.5^{\circ}\) to the first):

\(I _1= I _0 \times \cos ^2\left(22.5^{\circ}\right)\)

After the third polaroid (crossed with the first, i.e., \(90^{\circ}\) apart, and thus \(67.5^{\circ}\) from the middle one):

\(I_2=I_1 \times \cos ^2\left(67.5^{\circ}\right)\)

So final intensity \(= I _0 \times \cos ^2\left(22.5^{\circ}\right) \times \cos ^2\left(67.5^{\circ}\right)\)

\(\begin{array}{l}\cos \left(22.5^{\circ}\right) \approx 0.924, \cos ^2\left(22.5^{\circ}\right) \approx 0.853 \\ \cos \left(67.5^{\circ}\right) \approx 0.383, \cos ^2\left(67.5^{\circ}\right) \approx 0.146\end{array}\)

Final intensity \(\approx I _0 \times 0.853 \times 0.146 \approx I _0 \times 0.124 \approx I _0 / 8\)
