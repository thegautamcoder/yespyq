athe magnetic field at all points inside the pipe is the same, but not zero

Q5

An electron (mass 9×10-31 kg and charge 1.6×10-19C ) moving with speed c/100 (c = speed of light) is injected into a magnetic field B of magnitude 9×10-4 T perpendicular to its direction of motion. We wish to apply an uniform electric field E→ together with the magnetic field so that the electron does not deflect from its path. Then (speed of light c=3×108 ms-1 )

[NEET 2025]

a

E→ is parallel to B→ and its magnitude is 27×102 V m-1

b

E→ is parallel to B→ and its magnitude is 27×104 V m-1

c

E→ is perpendicular to B→ and its magnitude is 27×104 V m-1

d

E→ is perpendicular to B→ and its magnitude is 27×102 V m-1

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

E→ is perpendicular to B→ and its magnitude is 27×102 V m-1

Explanation

The magnetic force on a moving charge is given by:

Fmagnetic =qv×B

The electric force is given by:

Felectric =qE

For no deflection, the forces must balance:

Felectric =Fmagnetic 

qE=qv×B

Therefore, E = v × B which means E should be perpendicular to both v and B.
Now, the velocity (v) of the electron is:

v=c/100=3×108/100=3×106 m/s

Substitute the values for B and v :

E=3×106×9×10−4=27×102 V/m

A. hold the sheet there if it is magnetic.

B. hold the sheet there if it is non-magnetic.

C. move the sheet away from the pole with uniform velocity if it is conducting.

D. move the sheet away from the pole with uniform velocity if it is both, non-conducting and non-polar.

Choose the correct statement(s) from the options given below:

[NEET 2024]

a

B and D only

b

A and C only

c

A, C and D only

d

C only

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

A and C only

Explanation

A. A magnetic pole will repel or attract magnetic sheet so force is needed.
B. If sheet is non-magnetic, no force needed.
C. If it is conducting, then there will be addy current in sheet, which opposes the motion. So forces is needed move sheet with uniform speed.
D. The non-conducting and non-polar sheet do not interact with magnetic field of magnet.

Q7

A tightly wound 100 turns coil of radius 10 cm carries a current of 7 A. The magnitude of the magnetic field at the centre of the coil is (Take permeability of free space as 4π×10-7SI units):

a

44 mT

b

4.4 T

c

4.4 mT

d

44 T

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

4.4 mT

Explanation

For nn tightly wound turns of radius RR

B=μ0nI2R.B=\frac{\mu_{0} n I}{2R}. μ0=4π×10−7 Tm A−1,  n=100,  I=7 A,  R=0.10 m B=4π×10−7 (100)(7)2(0.10)=4.4×10−3 T=4.4 mT.B=\frac{4\pi\times10^{-7}\,(100)(7)}{2(0.10)} =4.4\times10^{-3}\,\text{T}=4.4\ \text{mT}.
