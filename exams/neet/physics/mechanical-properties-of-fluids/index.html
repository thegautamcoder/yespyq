PYQ

A submarine is designed to withstand an absolute pressure of 100 atm. How deep can it go below the water surface?

(Consider the density of water = 1000 kg m–3, 1 atm = 1 × 105 Pa and gravitational acceleration g = 10 m/s2)

a

9900 m

b

99 m

c

9000 m

d

990 m

✓ Correct answer: d)

990 m

Explanation

At depth h,

\({P}_{\text{absolute}}={P}_{\text{atm}}+\rho gh\)

Given:

\(100\text{atm}=1\text{atm}+\rho gh\)

\(\rho gh=99\text{atm}=99\times {10}^{5}\text{Pa}\)

\(h=\frac{99\times {10}^{5}}{1000\times 10}=990\text{m}\)

So, maximum depth = \(990\text{m}\).
