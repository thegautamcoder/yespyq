Q1 FREE PREVIEW

A box of mass 5 kg is pulled by a cord, up along a frictionless plane inclined at 30° with the horizontal. The tension in the cord is 30 N. The acceleration of the box is (Takeg=10 ms-2 ):

a

2 m s-2

b

zero

c

0.1 m s-2

d

1 m s-2

✓ Correct answer: d)

1 m s-2

Explanation

Force acting up the incline (Tension, T ): T = 30  N Force acting down the incline: m g sin ⁡ θ = 5 × 10 × sin ⁡ ( 30 ∘ ) = 50 × 1 2 = 25  N Net force equation ( F net = m a ): T − m g sin ⁡ θ = m a 30 − 25 = 5 × a 5 = 5 a a = 1  m  s − 2
