Q3

A room heater is rated 400 W, 220 V. If the supply voltage drops to 200 V, what will be the power consumed (approximately)?

a

121 W

b

331 W

c

200 W

d

400 W

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

331 W

Explanation

Resistance of heater \(R_H=\frac{(220)^2}{400}\)
So Actual power gained

$$
\begin{aligned}
P^{\prime} & =\frac{V_s^2}{R_H}=\frac{(200)^2}{(220)^2}(400) \\
& =330.57 \mathrm{~W} \\
& \simeq 331 \mathrm{~W}
\end{aligned}
$$

Q4

Two heaters A and B have power rating of 1 kW and 2 kW, respectively. Those two are first connected in series and then in parallel to a fixed power source. The ratio of power outputs for these two cases is:

[NEET 2024]

a

1 : 1

b

2 : 9

c

1 : 2

d

2 : 3

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

2 : 9

Explanation

Given:

  • Heater A: PA=1 kWP_A = 1\,\text{kW}

  • Heater B: PB=2 kWP_B = 2\,\text{kW}

  • Connected to same power supply

  • We are to find the ratio of total power output in series : parallel connection

Step 1: Find resistance of each heater using P=V2R⇒R=V2PP = \frac{V^2}{R} \Rightarrow R = \frac{V^2}{P}

Let voltage of the source be VV (same for both heaters)

  • RA=V21000R_A = \frac{V^2}{1000}

  • RB=V22000R_B = \frac{V^2}{2000}

Step 2: Power when in series

Total resistance:

Rseries=RA+RB=V21000+V22000=3V22000R_{\text{series}} = R_A + R_B = \frac{V^2}{1000} + \frac{V^2}{2000} = \frac{3V^2}{2000}

Total power:

Pseries=V2Rseries=V23V22000=20003 WP_{\text{series}} = \frac{V^2}{R_{\text{series}}} = \frac{V^2}{\frac{3V^2}{2000}} = \frac{2000}{3}\ \text{W}

Step 3: Power when in parallel

In parallel, total power:

Pparallel=PA+PB=1000+2000=3000 WP_{\text{parallel}} = P_A + P_B = 1000 + 2000 = 3000\ \text{W}

Step 4: Ratio of total power

PseriesPparallel=2000/33000=29\frac{P_{\text{series}}}{P_{\text{parallel}}} = \frac{2000/3}{3000} = \frac{2}{9}

1.2 V
