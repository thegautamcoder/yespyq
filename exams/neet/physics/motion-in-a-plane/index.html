Q1 FREE PREVIEW

The trajectory of projectile, projected from the ground is given by \(y=x-\frac{{x}^{2}}{20}\). Where x and y are measured in meter. The maximum height attained by the projectile will be:

a

\(5\mathrm{m}\)

b

\(10\sqrt{2}\mathrm{m}\)

c

\(200\mathrm{m}\)

d

\(10\mathrm{m}\)

✓ Correct answer: a)

\(5\mathrm{m}\)

Explanation

\(y=x-\frac{{x}^{2}}{20}\)

For maximum height,
\(\frac{dy}{dx}=0\Rightarrow \frac{d}{dx}\left(x-\frac{{x}^{2}}{20}\right)=0\\ 1-\frac{2x}{20}=0\\ x=10\)

So, \({y}_{\max }=10-\frac{100}{20}=5m\)
