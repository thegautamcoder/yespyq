PYQ

The amplitude of \(15\sin (1000\pi t)\) is modulated by \(10\sin (4\pi t)\) signal. The amplitude modulated signal contains frequency(ies) of
A. \(500\mathrm{Hz}\)
B. \(2\mathrm{Hz}\)
C. \(250\mathrm{Hz}\)
D. \(498\mathrm{Hz}\)
E. \(502\mathrm{Hz}\)

Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

[JEE Main 2023, 31 Jan (Shift 1)]

a

A only

b

A, D and E only

c

B only

d

A and B only

✓ Correct answer: b)

A, D and E only

Explanation

Frequency of carrier wave
\({v}_{C}=\frac{1000\pi }{2\pi }=500\mathrm{Hz}\)
modulating wave
\({v}_{m}=\frac{4\pi }{2\pi }=2\mathrm{Hz}\)

Frequencies of the modelated wave
\(∴{v}_{C}-{v}_{m},{v}_{C},{v}_{C}+{v}_{m}\\ =498\mathrm{Hz},500\mathrm{Hz},502\mathrm{Hz}\)
