Q1 FREE PREVIEW

The amplitude of 15sin(1000πt) is modulated by 10sin(4πt) signal. The amplitude modulated signal contains frequency(ies) of
A. 500 Hz
B. 2 Hz
C. 250 Hz
D. 498 Hz
E. 502 Hz

Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

[JEE Main 2023, 31 Jan (Shift 1)]

a

A only

b

A, D and E only

c

B only

d

A and B only

✓ Correct answer: b)

A, D and E only

Explanation

Frequency of carrier wave
vC=1000π2π=500 Hz
modulating wave
vm=4π2π=2 Hz

Frequencies of the modelated wave
 ∴vC-vm,vC,vC+vm =498 Hz,500 Hz,502 Hz
