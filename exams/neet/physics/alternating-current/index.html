Q11

To an ac power supply of 220 V at 50 Hz , a resistor of 20Ω, a capacitor of reactance 25Ω and an inductor of reactance 45Ω are connected in series. The corresponding current in the circuit and the phase angle between the current and the voltage is, respectively -

[NEET 2025]

a

15.6 A and 30°

b

15.6 A and 45°

c

7.5 A and 30°

d

7.8 A and 45°

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

7.8 A and 45°

Explanation

\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
& X_L=45 \mathrm{~W}, X_C=25 \mathrm{~W}, R=20 \mathrm{~W} \\
& I=\frac{220}{\sqrt{\left(X_L-X_C\right)^2+R^2}}=\frac{220}{\sqrt{(45-25)^2+20^2}} \\
& =\frac{220}{20 \sqrt{2}}=\frac{11}{\sqrt{2}}=7.779 \mathrm{~A} \\
& \tan \phi=\frac{X_L-X_C}{R}=\frac{45-25}{20}=1 \\
& \phi=45^{\circ}
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}

The peak value of an alternating current is 5 A and frequency is 60 Hz. How long will the current, starting from zero, take to reach the peak value?

a

1240s

b

130s

c

1120s

d

160s

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

1240s

Explanation

∵ I = I₀ sin ωt

I = I₀, when ωt = π/2

⇒ (2πf)t = π/2

⇒ 2π × 60 × t = π/2

⇒ t = 1/240 sec
