[NEET 2024]

a

Statement I is false but Statement II is true.

b

Both Statement I and Statement II are true.

Both Statement I and Statement II are true.

Explanation

  • Both [ Co(NH3)6]3+[\,\mathrm{Co}(\mathrm{NH}_3)_6]^{3+} and [ CoF6]3−[\,\mathrm{CoF}_6]^{3-} are octahedral but differ in their magnetic properties.

Q5

The correct order of the wavelength of light absorbed by the following complexes is,
A. CoNH363+
B. Co(CN)63-
C. CuH2O42+
D.TiH2O63+

Choose the correct answer from the options given below

[NEET 2025]

a

C \(<\) D \(<\) A \(<\) B.

b

C \(<\) A \(<\) D \(<\) B

c

B \(<\) D \(<\) A \(<\) C

d

B \(<\) A \(<\) D \(<\) C

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

B \(<\) A \(<\) D \(<\) C

Explanation

The higher the crystal field splitting energy (Δ₀) in the complex, the higher will be the energy of light absorbed and the lower will be the wavelength of the absorbed light.
Ligand strength order:
CN⁻ > NH₃ > H₂O

Thus, the order of splitting energy will be:
[Co(CN)₆]³⁻ > [Co(NH₃)₆]³⁺ > [Ti(H₂O)₆]³⁺ > [Cu(H₂O)₄]²⁺

Since wavelength is inversely proportional to energy, the increasing order of wavelength will be:

[Co(CN)₆]³⁻ < [Co(NH₃)₆]³⁺ < [Ti(H₂O)₆]³⁺ < [Cu(H₂O)₄]²⁺

i.e., B < A < D < C

CN-

✓ Correct answer: a)

C2O42-

Explanation

  • An ambidentate ligand can coordinate through two different atoms (e.g., SCN⁻ via S or N, NO₂⁻ via N or O, CN⁻ via C or N).

  • C2O42−\mathrm{C_2O_4^{2-}} (oxalate) is bidentate (binds through two oxygens) but not ambidentate (it does not offer distinct donor atoms for alternative attachment).
