Q1 FREE PREVIEW
Which one of the following statements is not true?.
aAmpicillin is a natural antibiotic.
bAspirin is both analgesic and antipyretic.
cSulphadiazine is a synthetic antibacterial drug.
dSome disinfectants can be used as antiseptics
✓ Correct answer: a) Ampicillin is a natural antibiotic.
ExplanationAmpicillin is a semi-synthetic antibiotic, meaning it is derived from a natural substance but has been chemically modified to improve its properties.
