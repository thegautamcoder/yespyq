Q4

Rate constants of a reaction at \(500 K\) and \(700 K\) are \(0.04 s ^{-1}\) and \(0.14 s ^{-1}\), respectively; then, activation energy of the reaction is
(Given : \(\log 3.5=0.5441, R =8.31 J K ^{-1} mol ^{-1}\) )

[Re-NEET 2024]

a

182310 J

b

18500 J

c

18219 J

d

18030 J

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

18219 J

Explanation

k2/k1 = 0.14/0.04 = 3.5

Arrhenius equation:

log(k2/k1) = [Ea/(2.303R)] × (T2 − T1)/(T1T2)

0.5441 = [Ea/(2.303 × 8.31)] × (200)/(500 × 700)

On solving:

Ea ≈ 18219 J/mol

Q5

If the half-life t1/2 for a first order reaction is 1 minute, then the time required for \(99.9 \%\) completion of the reaction is closest to

[NEET 2025]

a

5 minutes

b

10 minutes

c

2 minutes

d

4 minutes

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

10 minutes

Explanation

In a first-order reaction:

99.9% completion means
Remaining = 0.1% = 0.001

t = (2.303 / k) log(1 / 0.001)

log(1000) = 3

t = (2.303 / k) × 3

t1/2 = 0.693 / k

t /t1/2 = (2.303 × 3) / 0.693 ≈ 9.97 ≈ 10

Hence, the time = 10 × 1 minute = 10 minutes

Q6

The rate of a reaction quadruples when temperature changes from 27°C to 57°C. Calculate the energy of activation.
Given R=8.314JK-1mol-1, log4=0.6021

[NEET 2024]

a

3804 kJ/mol

b

38.04 kJ/mol

c

380.4 kJ/mol

d

3.80 kJ/mol

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

38.04 kJ/mol

Explanation

When temperature changes from T1=27∘ ⁣C=300 KT_1=27^\circ\!C=300\,K to T2=57∘ ⁣C=330 K,T_2=57^\circ\!C=330\,K, the rate quadruples:

ln⁡(k2k1)=ln⁡(4)≈1.386=EaR(1T1−1T2).\ln\left(\frac{k_2}{k_1}\right) = \ln(4)\approx1.386 = \frac{E_a}{R}\Bigl(\frac{1}{T_1}-\frac{1}{T_2}\Bigr).

So,

Ea=1.386 R(1300−1330)=1.386×8.314×11300−1330≈38 kJ mol−1.E_a = \frac{1.386\,R}{\left(\tfrac{1}{300}-\tfrac{1}{330}\right)} =1.386\times8.314\times\frac{1}{\tfrac{1}{300}-\tfrac{1}{330}} \approx 38\,\text{kJ mol}^{-1}.
