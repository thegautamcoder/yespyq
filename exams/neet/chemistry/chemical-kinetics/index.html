Q1 FREE PREVIEW
Which of the following will lead to an increase in the rate of the reaction?
aDecrease in temperature
bDecreasing concentration of reactants
cAddition of catalyst
dAddition of inhibitor
✓ Correct answer: c) Addition of catalyst
ExplanationThe presence of a catalyst in a reaction lowers the activation energy barrier and hence the reaction becomes faster.Hence, the addition of catalysts helps to increase the rate of a reaction.
