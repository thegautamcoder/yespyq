Q6

Identify the incorrect statement about \(PCl _5\)

[NEET 2024]

a

\(PCl _5\) possesses two different \(Cl - P - Cl\) bond angles.

b

All five \(P - Cl\) bonds are identical in length.

c

\(PCl _5\) exhibits \(sp ^3 d\) hybridisation.

d

\(PCl _5\) consists of five \(P - Cl\) (sigma) bonds.

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

All five \(P - Cl\) bonds are identical in length.

Explanation

In PCl₅, the molecule has trigonal bipyramidal geometry with two types of bonds: axial and equatorial. Due to greater repulsion in axial positions, the axial P–Cl bonds are longer than the equatorial P–Cl bonds. Hence, all five P–Cl bonds are not identical in length. Therefore, the incorrect statement is "All five \(P - Cl\) bonds are identical in length".

Q10

Identify the correct statement.

[NEET 2024]

a

Three canonical forms can be drawn for CO32- ion.

b

Three resonance structures can be drawn for ozone.

c

BF3 has non-zero dipole moment.

d

Dipole moment of NF3 is greater than that of NH3.

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

Three canonical forms can be drawn for CO32- ion.

Explanation

CO32− has three valid resonance (canonical) forms. O₃ has only two resonance structures. BF₃ is trigonal planar and thus has zero dipole moment. NF₃ has a dipole moment less than NH₃ because the lone-pair orbital dipole opposes the bond dipoles.
