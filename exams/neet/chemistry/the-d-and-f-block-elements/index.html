Q1 FREE PREVIEW

The pair of lanthanoid ions which are diamagnetic is : (2024)

a

Pm3+ and Sm3+

b

Ce4+ and Yb2+

c

Ce3+ and Eu2+

d

Gd3+and Eu3+

✓ Correct answer: b)

Ce4+ and Yb2+

Explanation

Ce⁴⁺ has a 4f⁰ configuration (no unpaired electrons) and Yb²⁺ has a 4f¹⁴ configuration (all f‐orbitals filled). Both ions are diamagnetic.

Q2 FREE PREVIEW

The green paramagnetic species formed by heating KMnO4 at 513 K is

[Re-NEET 2026]

a

K2MnO4

b

Mn3O4

c

MnO

d

KO2

✓ Correct answer: a)

K2MnO4

Explanation

2KMnO4 → K2MnO4 + MnO2 + O2

K2MnO4 will form on heating KMnO4 which has green colour.
