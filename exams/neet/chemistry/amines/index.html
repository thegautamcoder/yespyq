Which of the following reactions will NOT give primary amine as the product?

\(\mathrm{CH}_3 \mathrm{CONH}_2 \xrightarrow{\mathrm{Br}_2 / \mathrm{KOH}}\) Product

Answer & explanation — Premium

✓ Correct answer: b)

CH3NC→(ii)  H3O+(i)  LiAlH4 Product

Explanation

CH₃CN reduced by LiAlH₄ gives ethylamine, a primary amine.

CH₃NC (isonitrile) upon reduction with LiAlH₄ produces dimethylamine, a secondary amine (so NOT a primary amine).

Acetamide (CH₃CONH₂) under LiAlH₄ reduction yields ethylamine, a primary amine.

Acetamide + Br₂/KOH (Hofmann bromamide) also yields methylamine, a primary amine.

Given below are two statements

Statement I : Benzenediazonium salt is prepared by the reaction of aniline with nitrous açid at 273-278 K. It decomposes easily in the dry state.

Statement II : Insertion of iodine into the benzene ring is difficult and hence iodobenzene is prepared through the reaction of benzenediazonium salt with KI.

In the light of the above statements, choose the most appropriate answer from the options given below

[NEET 2025]

a

Statement I is correct but Statement II is incorrect

b

Statement I is incorrect but Statement II is correct

c

Both Statement I and Statement II are correct

d

Both Statement I and Statement II are incorrect

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

Both Statement I and Statement II are correct

Explanation

statement I: Benzenediazonium salt is prepared at 273–278 K by the reaction of aniline with nitrous acid. It decomposes easily in the dry state.
This statement is true. Aniline reacts with NaNO2 and HCl at 273–278 K to form benzenediazonium chloride. This compound is unstable in the dry state and can decompose explosively, so it is generally kept in solution only.

Statement II: Direct introduction of iodine into the benzene ring is difficult, and therefore iodobenzene is prepared by the reaction of benzenediazonium salt with KI.
This statement is also true. Direct electrophilic substitution of iodine in the benzene ring does not occur easily because the reactivity of iodine is low. Therefore, to prepare iodobenzene, benzenediazonium salt is reacted with KI, in which the –N2+ group is replaced by I.

Hence, both statements are correct.

✓ Correct answer: d)

Explanation

1.4-Nitrotoluene + Br₂: Bromination at the meta position forms 2-Bromo-4-nitrotoluene (Compound P).

a

Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

Propylamine

Explanation

  1. CH₃CH₂CH₂I reacts with NaCN → forms CH₃CH₂CH₂CN (Butanenitrile).

  2. Partial hydrolysis → converts nitrile to amide → CH₃CH₂CH₂CONH₂ (Butanamide).

  3. Hoffmann bromamide degradation with Br₂/NaOH → eliminates one carbon → forms CH₃CH₂CH₂NH₂ (Propylamine).

Hence, the major product is Propylamine.

Q7

Given below are two statements:

Statement I : Primary aliphatic amines react with \( \mathrm{HNO}_{2} \) to give unstable diazonium salts.

Statement II : Primary aromatic amines react with \( \mathrm{HNO}_{2} \) to form diazonium salts which are stable even above \( 300 \mathrm{~K} \).

In the light of the above Statements, choose the most appropriate answer from the options given below

[NEET 2022]

a

Statement I is incorrect but Statement II is correct.

b

Both Statement I and Statement II are correct

c

Both Statement I and Statement II are incorrect.

d

Statement I is correct but Statement II is incorrect.

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

Statement I is correct but Statement II is incorrect.

Explanation

Primary aliphatic amines (R−NH2R-NH_2) react with nitrous acid (HNO2HNO_2​) to form aliphatic diazonium salts (R−N2+X−R-N_2^+X^-).

However, aliphatic diazonium salts are highly unstable and decompose rapidly at room temperature, releasing nitrogen gas (N2N_2​) and forming alcohols, alkenes, or other side products.

Primary aromatic amines (e.g., aniline, C6H5NH2C_6H_5NH_2​) react with nitrous acid to form aromatic diazonium salts (C6H5N2+X−C_6H_5N_2^+X^-).

These diazonium salts are stable only at low temperatures (273-278 K or 0-5°C) because they are stabilized by resonance over the benzene ring.

However, above 300 K (27°C), they decompose rapidly, leading to side reactions such as the formation of phenols, halobenzenes, or azo compounds.

Q10

Given below are two statements:

Statement I : Aniline does not undergo Friedel Crafts alkylation reaction.

Statement II : Aniline cannot be prepared through Gabriel synthesis.

In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below

a

Statement I is false but Statement II is true.

b

Both Statement I and Statement II are true.

c

Both Statement I and Statement II are false.

d

Statement I is true but Statement II is false.

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

Both Statement I and Statement II are true.

Explanation

  • Friedel-Crafts alkylation involves the reaction of an aromatic compound with an alkyl halide (R−XR-X) in the presence of a Lewis acid catalyst like AlCl3AlCl_3​.

  • Aniline (C6H5NH2C_6H_5NH_2) does not undergo this reaction because:

    1. The lone pair on the −NH2-NH_2 group coordinates with the Lewis acid catalyst (AlCl₃), forming a complex.

    2. This deactivates the benzene ring, making it less reactive towards electrophilic substitution.

    3. As a result, the reaction does not proceed efficiently.

  • Gabriel Phthalimide Synthesis is used for preparing primary amines but is not suitable for preparing aryl amines (like aniline) because:

    1. The method involves nucleophilic substitution of alkyl halides (R−XR-X) with phthalimide anion.

    2. Aryl halides (C6H5XC_6H_5X) do not undergo simple nucleophilic substitution due to partial double-bond character in the C-X bond, making it unreactive in Gabriel synthesis.

    3. Thus, aniline cannot be synthesized using this method
