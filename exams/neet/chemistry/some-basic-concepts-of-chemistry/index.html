Q1
A metal chloride contains 55.0% of chlorine by weight. 100 mL vapours of the metal chloride at STP weigh 0.57 g. The molecular formula of the metal chloride is (Given: Atomic mass of chlorine is 35.5 u)
aMCl2
bMCl4
cMCl3
dMCl
🔒

✓ Correct answer: a) MCl2
ExplanationGiven: Mass = 0.57 g, Volume = 100 mL = 0.1 L At STP, molar volume = 22.4 L Moles = 0.1 / 22.4 = 0.004464 mol Molar mass = 0.57 / 0.004464 ≈ 127.7 g/mol Let metal chloride be MClₓ %Cl = (35.5x / (M + 35.5x)) × 100 = 55 Putting M + 35.5x = 127.7, solve: 35.5x = 70.24 ⇒ x = 2 So formula = MCl₂ Answer: Option A

Q5

On complete combustion, \(0.3 g\) of an organic compound gave \(0.2 g\) of \(CO _2\) and \(0.1 g\) of \(H _2 O\). The percentage composition of carbon and hydrogen in the compound, respectively are:

[Re-NEET 2024]

a

\(4.07 \%\) and \(15.02 \%\)

b

\(18.18 \%\) and \(3.70 \%\)

c

\(15.02 \%\) and \(4.07 \%\)

d

\(3.70 \%\) and \(18.18 \%\)

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

\(18.18 \%\) and \(3.70 \%\)

Explanation

From CO2:
Mass of C = (12 / 44) × 0.2 = 0.0545 g

Percentage of C = (0.0545 / 0.3) × 100 = 18.18%

From H2O:
Mass of H = (2 / 18) × 0.1 = 0.0111 g

Percentage of H = (0.0111 / 0.3) × 100 = 3.70%

Q6

1 gram of sodium hydroxide was treated with 25 mL of 0.75 M HCl solution, the mass of sodium hydroxide left unreacted is equal to

[NEET 2024]

a

200 mg

b

750 mg

c

250 mg

d

0 mg

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

250 mg

Explanation

Molar mass of NaOH = 40

Moles = 1 / 40 = 0.025 mol

Moles of HCl = 0.75 × 0.025 = 0.01875 mol

Reaction:
NaOH + HCl → NaCl + H₂O

NaOH remaining = 0.025 − 0.01875 = 0.00625 mol

Mass = 0.00625 × 40 = 0.25 g = 250 mg

Q8

1.0 g of \(H _2\) has same number of molecules as in

[Re-NEET 2024]

a

\(14 g\) of \(N _2\)

b

\(18 g\) of \(H _2 O\)

c

\(16 g\) of \(CO\)

d

\(28 g\) of \(N _2\)

Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

✓ Correct answer: a)

\(14 g\) of \(N _2\)

Explanation

Molar mass of H2 = 2

Moles of 1 g H2 = 1 / 2 = 0.5 mol
