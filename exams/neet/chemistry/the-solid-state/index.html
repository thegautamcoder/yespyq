Q1 FREE PREVIEW
Most crystals show good cleavage because their atoms, ions or molecules are
aweakly bonded together
bstrongly bonded together
cspherically symmetrical
darranged in planes
✓ Correct answer: d) arranged in planes
ExplanationCleavage in crystals refers to the tendency of a crystal to break along specific planes of weakness.This happens because the arrangement of atoms, ions, or molecules in a crystal lattice creates planes where the bonding forces are weaker compared to other directions.Weak bonding along specific planes allows easy cleavage.Strong bonding within the planes keeps the structure intact in other directions.
