bThe energy of an electron in 2s orbital of an atom is lower than the energy of an electron that is infinitely far away from the nucleus.
cAccording to Bohr’s model, the most negative energy value for an electron is given by n = 1, which corresponds to the most stable orbit.
dAccording to Bohr’s model, the magnitude of velocity of electrons increases with increase in values of n.
