Q4

Arrange the following compounds in increasing order of their solubilities in chloroform
NaCl,CH3OH, cyclohexane, CH3CN

[Re-NEET 2024]

a

NaCl<CH3CN<CH3OH<Cyclohexane

b

CH3OH<CH3CN<NaCl<Cyclohexane

c

NaCl<CH3OH<CH3CN< Cyclohexane

d

Cyclohexane <CH3CN<CH3OH<NaCl

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

NaCl<CH3OH<CH3CN< Cyclohexane

Explanation

NaCl is ionic, whereas chloroform is a weakly polar solvent; it cannot solvate ions ⇒ least soluble.

CH3OH is highly polar and has strong hydrogen bonding; due to strong self-association, its solubility in a weakly polar solvent like chloroform is limited.

CH3CN is polar but aprotic and interacts/mixes better with chloroform ⇒ more soluble than methanol.

Cyclohexane is non-polar and chloroform is relatively non-polar ⇒ “like dissolves like,” hence most soluble.

For a one‐electron ion, the energy is En=-k×Z2n2J

Q9

Mass of glucose \(\left( C _6 H _{12} O _6\right)\) required to be dissolved to prepare one litre of its solution which is isotonic with \(15 gL ^{-1}\) solution of urea \(\left( NH _2 CONH _2\right)\) is

(Given: Molar mass in \(g mol ^{-1} C : 12, H : 1, O : 16, N : 14\) )

[Re-NEET 2024]

a

55 g

b

15 g

c

30 g

d

45 g

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

45 g

Explanation

For isotonic solutions, the osmotic pressure is equal.

Osmotic pressure formula:

π = CRT

Hence, for isotonic solutions:

C1 = C2

First, calculate the molar mass of urea:

N = 14 × 2 = 28
H = 1 × 4 = 4
C = 12
O = 16

Total molar mass = 28 + 4 + 12 + 16 = 60 g mol-1

Moles of urea per litre:

Moles = mass / molar mass
= 15 / 60
= 0.25 mol L-1

Since the solutions are isotonic:

Moles of glucose per litre = 0.25 mol L-1

Molar mass of glucose (C6H12O6):

C = 6 × 12 = 72
H = 12 × 1 = 12
O = 6 × 16 = 96

Total = 72 + 12 + 96 = 180 g mol-1

Required mass of glucose:

= moles × molar mass
= 0.25 × 180
= 45 g

Q11

The Henry's law constant KH values of three gases (A, B, C) in water are 145, 2×10-5 and 35 kbar, respectively. The solubility of these gases in water follow the order

a

A > B > C

b

B > A > C

c

B > C > A

d

A > C > B

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

B > C > A

Explanation

According to Henry’s law,
Lower KH means higher solubility.
Given:

  • Kₕ(B) = 2 × 10⁻⁵ (lowest) → highest solubility

  • Kₕ(C) = 35

  • Kₕ(A) = 145 (highest) → lowest solubility
    So, B > C > A.
