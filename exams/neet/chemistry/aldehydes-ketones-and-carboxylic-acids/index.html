Q1 FREE PREVIEW
The structure of protein that is unaffected by heating is:
aSecondary structure
btertiary structure
cPrimary structure
dQuaternary structure
✓ Correct answer: c) Primary structure
ExplanationThe primary structure of protein is un- affected by heating.In a process called denaturation, heat breaks the bonds that held together the secondary and tertiary structures, but it does not alter the original chain of amino acids i.e. its primary structure.
