Q17

What is the main function of the spindle fibers during mitosis?

[NEET 2025]

a

To repair damaged DNA

b

To regulate cell growth

c

To separate the chromosomes

d

To synthesize new DNA

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

To separate the chromosomes

Explanation

During mitosis, the main function of spindle fibers is to separate the chromosomes and ensure they are evenly distributed to the two daughter cells. Spindle fibers are made of microtubules and form from structures called centrosomes. They attach to the centromeres of chromosomes and help pull the sister chromatids apart during anaphase, moving them toward opposite poles of the cell. This ensures each new cell gets an exact copy of the genetic material.

  • Option A (DNA repair) and Option D (DNA synthesis) happen before mitosis.

  • Option B (cell growth) is regulated during other cell cycle phases.

Q20

Match List-I with List-II.

List I (Event) List II [Stage of Prophase I (Meiosis I)]
A. Chiasmata formation I. Pachytene
B. Crossing over II. Diakinesis
C. Synaptonemal complex formation III. Diplotene
D. Terminalisation of chiasmata IV. Zygotene

Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

[Re-NEET 2024]

a

A-III, B-I, C-IV, D-II

b

A-II, B-I, C-III, D-IV

c

A-III, B-I, C-II, D-IV

d

A-II, B-III, C-IV, D-I

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

A-III, B-I, C-IV, D-II

Explanation
  • A. Chiasmata formation: Chiasmata are the points where homologous chromosomes remain attached after crossing over has occurred. These become visible during the diplotene stage as the synaptonemal complex dissolves and the chromosomes begin to separate.
  • B. Crossing over: This is the process of genetic material exchange between non-sister chromatids of homologous chromosomes. It occurs during the pachytene stage of Prophase I.
  • C. Synaptonemal complex formation: The synaptonemal complex is a protein structure that forms between homologous chromosomes during meiosis and mediates synapsis. It is formed during the zygotene stage.
  • D. Terminalisation of chiasmata: This is the movement of the chiasmata towards the ends of the chromosomes. It occurs during the diakinesis stage, which is the final stage of Prophase I.

Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

[NEET 2026]

a

A-II, B-III, C-IV, D-I

b

A-III, B-IV, C-I, D-II

c

A-I, B-II, C-III, D-IV

✓ Correct answer: a)

A-II, B-III, C-IV, D-I

The cell cycle is divided into interphase (G₁, S, G₂) and M phase.

Q25

Recombination between homologous chromosomes is completed by the end of:

[Re-NEET 2024]

a

Diakinesis

b

Zygotene

c

Diplotene

d

Pachytene

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

Pachytene

Explanation

During prophase I of meiosis, the stages are:

  1. Leptotene – Chromosomes start condensing.

  2. Zygotene – Homologous chromosomes pair (synapsis) to form bivalents.

  3. Pachytene – Crossing over (recombination) occurs between non-sister chromatids of homologous chromosomes.

  4. Diplotene – Chiasmata become visible as homologues begin to separate.

  5. Diakinesis – Terminalization of chiasmata and preparation for metaphase I.

Thus, genetic recombination is completed by the end of the Pachytene stage.
