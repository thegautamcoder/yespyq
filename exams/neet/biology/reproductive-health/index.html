ExplanationCorrect option: C (Morula) After induced fertilisation of the ovum in the laboratory (in vitro fertilisation), the stage that is transferred into the uterus is an embryo with more than 8 blastomeres.Early embryos such as the zygote, 2-cell, or 4-cell stages are usually transferred into the fallopian tube through ZIFT.A morula is a later embryonic stage (about 16 blastomeres) and is therefore suitable for intra-uterine transfer (IUT), where it can implant and continue normal development.Hence, among the given options, the morula is the correct stage transferred into the uterus.

Explanation
  • The correct answer is B. Tuberculosis.
  • Syphilis, gonorrhoea and genital warts are sexually transmitted diseases that spread mainly through sexual contact with an infected person. Syphilis is caused by Treponema pallidum, gonorrhoea by Neisseria gonorrhoeae, and genital warts by human papillomavirus (HPV).

Match List-I with List-II relating to examples of various kind of IUDs and barrier :

LIST I LIST II
A) Copper releasing IUD I) Vaults
B) Non medicated IUD II) Multiload 375
C) Contraceptive barrier III) LNG-20
D) Hormone releasing IUD IV) Lippes loop

Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

[Re-NEET 2024]

a

A-II, B-IV, C-III, D-I

b

A-IV, B-III, C-I, D-II

c

A-II, B-I, C-III, D-IV

d

A-II, B-IV, C-I, D-III

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

A-II, B-IV, C-I, D-III

Explanation
  • A) Copper releasing IUD: Multiload 375 is an example of a copper releasing IUD. It releases copper ions into the uterus, which prevents sperm from fertilizing an egg.
  • B) Non-medicated IUD: The Lippes loop is a non-medicated IUD. It doesn't contain any hormones or other medications, and it works primarily by preventing implantation of a fertilized egg.
  • C) Contraceptive barrier: Vaults is an example of a contraceptive barrier. It is a dome-shaped cap that is placed over the cervix before intercourse.
  • D) Hormone releasing IUD: LNG-20 is an example of a hormone releasing IUD. It releases the hormone levonorgestrel, which prevents ovulation and thickens cervical mucus.

Q18

Choose the correct statement regarding GIFT to overcome infertility.

[NEET 2026]

a

Ova collected from a female donor are transferred to the uterus of an infertile female.

b

It is the transfer of an ovum collected from a donor into the fallopian tube of another female who cannot produce ovum but can provide suitable environment for fertilization and development.

c

Early embryos with up to 8 blastomeres are transferred to the uterus of an infertile female.

d

Early embryos with up to 8 blastomeres are transferred into the fallopian tube of an infertile female.

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

It is the transfer of an ovum collected from a donor into the fallopian tube of another female who cannot produce ovum but can provide suitable environment for fertilization and development.

Explanation
  • GIFT (Gamete Intra Fallopian Transfer) is an assisted reproductive technique used to treat infertility.
  • In GIFT, both male and female gametes are transferred into the fallopian tube, allowing fertilization to occur inside the female body (in vivo fertilization).
  • It is specifically suitable for women who cannot produce ova but have a normal reproductive tract and can support fertilization and development.
  • The technique involves placing a donor ovum along with sperm into the fallopian tube, where natural fertilization takes place.
  • Option A describes egg donation and uterine transfer, which is incorrect.
  • Option C and D describe ETT (Embryo Transfer Technique), not GIFT, since they involve embryos (up to 8 blastomeres).
  • Therefore, the correct statement is Option B.
