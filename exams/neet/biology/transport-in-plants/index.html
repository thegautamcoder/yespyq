Q1 FREE PREVIEW
PYQ

The process responsible for facilitating loss of water in liquid form from the tip of grass blades at night and in early morning is

[NEET 2020]

a

Imbibition

b

Plasmolysis

c

Transpiration

d

Root pressure

✓ Correct answer: d)

Root pressure

Explanation

  • The loss of water in liquid form from the tips or edges of leaves, especially at night or early morning, is called guttation.

  • This occurs when transpiration is low (due to high humidity and low temperature) and root pressure is high.

  • Root pressure is generated by the active uptake of minerals and water from the soil by roots, which pushes water up into the xylem and eventually out through hydathodes (special pores at leaf tips).

Why not the others?

  • A. Imbibition → Absorption of water by dry seeds or colloidal substances, not related to water loss.

  • B. Plasmolysis → Loss of water from a plant cell in a hypertonic solution, leading to cell shrinkage.

  • C. Transpiration → Loss of water as vapor, not liquid, through stomata — occurs mostly during the day.

Final Answer: D. Root pressure
