Q1
Unisexuality of flowers prevents;
ageitonogamy, but not xenogamy.
bautogamy and geitonogamy.
cautogamy, but not geitonogamy.
dboth geitonogamy and xenogamy.
🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c) autogamy, but not geitonogamy.
ExplanationUnisexual flowers have either stamens (male) or carpels (female), so a single flower cannot self-pollinate, preventing autogamy.However, a plant with multiple unisexual flowers (especially monoecious plants) can still have pollen from male flowers reach female flowers on the same plant, allowing geitonogamy.Xenogamy (cross-pollination between different plants) is still possible.Thus, unisexuality specifically prevents self-pollination within the same flower but does not stop pollination between different flowers of the same plant.

Q4
What is the fate of the male gametes discharged in the synergid? [NEET 2019]
aOne fuses with egg, others degenerates in the synergid.
bAll fuse with the egg.
cOne fuses with the egg, others fuses with synergid nucleus.
dOne fuses with the egg and other fuses with central cell nuclei.
🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d) One fuses with the egg and other fuses with central cell nuclei.
ExplanationFate of Male Gametes in Double Fertilization: One male gamete fuses with the egg cell to form the zygote (syngamy).The second male gamete fuses with the two polar nuclei in the central cell to form the primary endosperm nucleus (PEN) (triple fusion).This process is unique to angiosperms and leads to the formation of both embryo and endosperm.Thus, the correct answer is D (One fuses with the egg and other fuses with central cell nuclei).

Q8

Pollen grains remain preserved as fossils due to the presence of :

[Re-NEET 2024]

a

Epidermal layer

b

Tapetum

c

Exine layer

d

Intine layer

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

Exine layer

Explanation

Pollen grains are generally spherical measuring about 25-50 micrometers in diameter. It has a prominent two-layered wall. The hard outer layer called the exine is made up of sporopollenin which is one of the most resistant organic material known. It can withstand high temperatures and strong acids and alkali. No enzyme that degrades sporopollenin is so far known. Pollen grain exine has prominent apertures called germ pores where sporopollenin is absent. Pollen grains are well preserved as fossils because of the presence of sporopollenin.

Q9

Given below are two statements: One is labelled as Assertion (A) and the other is labelled as Reason (R).
Assertion (A): A typical unfertilised, angiosperm embryo sac at maturity is 8 nucleate and 7-celled.
Reason (R): The egg apparatus has 2 polar nuclei.
In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below :

[NEET 2025]

a

A is true but R is false

b

A is false but R is true

c

Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A

d

Both A and R are true but R is NOT the correct explanation of A

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

A is true but R is false

Explanation

The Assertion (A) is correct. A mature, unfertilised embryo sac in angiosperms is 8-nucleate and 7-celled. It has 3 cells in the egg apparatus (1 egg + 2 synergids), 2 polar nuclei in the central cell, and 3 antipodal cells. These make 7 cells and 8 nuclei in total.

The Reason (R) is incorrect. The egg apparatus contains only 3 cells—1 egg cell and 2 synergids. The 2 polar nuclei are not part of the egg apparatus; they are located in the central cell of the embryo sac.

Hence, A is true, but R is false.

Correct developmental sequence:

Q12

Which one of the following is a triploid cell ?

[NEET 2026]

a

Central cell

b

Primary endosperm cell

c

Zygote

d

Synergid

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

Primary endosperm cell

Explanation

Triploid cells in angiosperms: formed by fusion of one male gamete with two polar nuclei during double fertilisation

  • Option A: Incorrect → central cell contains two polar nuclei (n + n), so it is diploid before fertilisation
  • Option B: Correct → primary endosperm cell is triploid (3n) formed after triple fusion
  • Option C: Incorrect → zygote is diploid (2n) formed by fusion of gametes
  • Option D: Incorrect → synergid is haploid (n)

Thus, the correct answer is: Primary endosperm cell

Final Answer: Option B

Explanation:

  • The nucellus is the central part of the ovule, enclosed by the integuments.

Q15

If the diploid chromosome number of typical angiosperm is 36, what would be the chromosome number in its endosperm?

[Re-NEET 2026]

a

18

b

36

c

54

d

72

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

54

Explanation
  • Diploid number (2n) = 36
  • Therefore, haploid number (n) = 18

In angiosperms, the endosperm is triploid (3n) because it forms by fusion of:

  • One male gamete (n = 18)
  • Two polar nuclei (n + n = 18 + 18)

Total chromosome number = 18 + 18 + 18 = 54

Conclusion:

Endosperm chromosome number = 54

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

Xenogamy

Explanation

Types of pollination: based on source of pollen—same flower, same plant, or different plant

  • Option A: Incorrect → geitonogamy involves transfer of pollen between flowers of the same plant, so genetically similar
  • Option B: Incorrect → autogamy is transfer within the same flower, so genetically identical
  • Option C: Correct → xenogamy involves transfer between different plants, bringing genetically different pollen
  • Option D: Incorrect → cleistogamy ensures self-pollination within closed flowers

Given below are two statements : One is labelled as Assertion (A) and the other is labelled as Reason (R).
Assertion (A): Cells of the tapetum possess dense cytoplasm and generally have more than one nucleus.
Reason (R): Presence of more than one nucleus in the tapetum increases the efficiency of nourishing the developing microspore mother cells
In light of the above statements, choose the most appropriate answer from the options given below:

[NEET 2025]

a

A is true but Ris false

b

A is false but R is true

c

Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A

d

Both A and R are true but R is NOT the correct explanation of A

Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A

Explanation

The tapetum is the innermost layer of the anther wall in flowering plants and plays a key role in nourishing the developing microspores (pollen grains).

  • Assertion (A) is true: Tapetal cells have dense cytoplasm and often possess more than one nucleus due to processes like mitosis without cytokinesis or nuclear fusion.

Since both statements are true and R correctly explains A, the correct answer is Option C.
