✓ Correct answer: b)

(c) and (d) only

Explanation

Analyzing the statements based on photorespiration:

(a) Do not occur in C3 plants: Incorrect, it occurs specifically in C3 plants (and is absent in C4 plants).

(b) CO2 is consumed and O2 is generated: Incorrect, it is the opposite. O2 is consumed and CO2 is released/evolved.

(c) Phosphoglycolate is formed: Correct, RuBP binds with O2 to form one molecule of phosphoglycerate and one molecule of phosphoglycolate. (d) No synthesis of ATP and NADPH: Correct, photorespiration is a wasteful process where there is no synthesis of ATP or NADPH.

Since statements (c) and (d) are correct, option (B) is the correct choice.

✓ Correct answer: d)

3, 4 and 5 only

Explanation

The dark reaction (also called the Calvin cycle or light-independent reaction) does not require light directly. It uses the products of the light reaction to fix carbon dioxide into carbohydrates.

Requirements of the dark reaction:

  • 3. Carbon dioxide (CO₂): Source of carbon for glucose formation.
  • 4. ATP: Provides energy for carbon fixation steps.
  • 5. NADPH: Provides reducing power (hydrogen and electrons) to reduce 3-phosphoglyceric acid to glyceraldehyde-3-phosphate.

Q7

Which one of the following products diffuses out of chloroplast during photosynthesis?

[Re-NEET 2024]

a

ADP

b

NAD

c

O2

d

ATP

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

O2

Explanation

Correct answer: C — O₂ (Oxygen)


During photosynthesis, oxygen is produced in the light-dependent reactions (in the thylakoid membrane) by photolysis of water:

[2H2O → 4H+ + 4e- + O₂↑]

  • This O₂ diffuses out of the chloroplast into the cytoplasm and then out of the leaf.

  • ATP and NADPH remain inside the chloroplast for use in the Calvin cycle (dark reactions).

Hence, O₂ is the product that diffuses out of the chloroplast.

Q8

How many turns of Calvin cycle are required for the formation of three molecules of glucose ?

[Re-NEET 2026]

a

6

b

3

c

1

d

18

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

18

Explanation

In the Calvin cycle, 1 turn fixes one molecule of carbon dioxide (CO2). To synthesize one single molecule of glucose (a 6-carbon sugar), 6 turns of the Calvin cycle are required because 6 CO2 molecules need to be fixed. Therefore, for the formation of 3 molecules of glucose, the total number of turns needed is: 3 × 6 = 18 turns. Thus, option (D) is correct.

How many ATP and NADPH molecules are required to make one molecule of glucose through the Calvin pathway?

Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

18 ATP and 12 NADPH

Explanation

In the Calvin cycle, the synthesis of one molecule of glucose requires fixation of 6 molecules of CO₂.

For every 1 CO₂ fixed:

  • 3 ATP are used
  • 2 NADPH are used

So, for 6 CO₂ (to form 1 glucose):

  • ATP required = 3 × 6 = 18 ATP
  • NADPH required = 2 × 6 = 12 NADPH

Correct answer: Option A

D. C3 plants exhibit 'Kranz' anatomy.
E. ATP synthesis in chloroplast occurs through chemiosmosis.

✓ Correct answer: b)

A and D only

Explanation
  • Statement A: Incorrect
    The water-splitting complex is associated with Photosystem II (PS II), not PS I.

Q16

Which of the following are required for the light reaction of Photosynthesis ?
A. CO2
B. O2
C. H2O
D. Chlorophyll
E. Light
Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

[Re-NEET 2024]

a

A, C, D and E only

b

C, D and E only

c

A and B only

d

A, C and E only

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

C, D and E only

Explanation

The light reaction of photosynthesis (occurs in thylakoid membranes) requires:

  • H₂O → source of electrons and protons; split during photolysis to release O₂.

  • Chlorophyll → pigment that captures light energy.

  • Light → energy source to drive photolysis and ATP/NADPH formation.

It does not require CO₂, because CO₂ fixation occurs during the dark reaction (Calvin cycle).
O₂ is a by-product, not a reactant.

Hence, required components are H₂O, chlorophyll, and light → C, D, and E only.
