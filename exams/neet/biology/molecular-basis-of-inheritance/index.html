ExplanationTranslation is the process where the information in messenger RNA (mRNA) is used to synthesize proteins.This process occurs in the ribosome, where transfer RNA (tRNA) reads the mRNA code and assembles amino acids in the correct sequence to form a protein.
Q3 FREE PREVIEW
Which one of the following is the genetic material of prokaryotic cell?
aNon-histonic double stranded DNA
bHistonic double stranded DNA
cHistone & DNA both are absent
dHistone without DNA
✓ Correct answer: a) Non-histonic double stranded DNA
ExplanationThe genetic material of a prokaryotic cell is: Option A: Non-histonic double stranded DNA Explanation: Prokaryotic cells contain double-stranded DNA that is not associated with histones.Unlike eukaryotic cells, prokaryotic DNA is circular and lacks histone proteins, which are present in eukaryotic chromosomes to help in DNA packaging.
Q4 FREE PREVIEW
Transformation experiment was first performed on which bacteria?
aE. coli
bDiplococcus pneumoniae
cSalmonella
dPasteurella pestis
✓ Correct answer: b) Diplococcus pneumoniae
ExplanationCorrect Answer: B. Diplococcus pneumoniae Explanation: The transformation experiment was first performed by Frederick Griffith in 1928. He worked with two strains of Diplococcus pneumoniae (now known as Streptococcus pneumoniae): S strain (smooth, virulent) R strain (rough, non-virulent) Griffith discovered that the heat-killed S strain could transfer genetic material to the R strain, transforming it into a virulent form.This experiment was the first evidence of the transfer of genetic information (later identified as DNA).Incorrect Options: A. E. coli – Used in later experiments (e.g., Hershey-Chase), not Griffith’s.C. Salmonella – Not involved in the transformation experiment.D. Pasteurella pestis – Old name for Yersinia pestis (plague bacterium); unrelated. ✅ Final Answer: B. Diplococcus pneumoniae

aEscherichia coli.

Q6 FREE PREVIEW
Which one of the following is not a part of a transcription unit in DNA?
aThe inducer
bA terminator
cA promoter
dThe structural gene
✓ Correct answer: a) The inducer
ExplanationA transcription unit in DNA consists of three main parts:1. Promoter – initiation site for RNA polymerase2. Structural gene – the region that is transcribed3. Terminator – signals the end of transcription The inducer is not a part of the DNA transcription unit.It is usually a small molecule (like lactose in lac operon) that regulates gene expression, not a DNA sequence.So, the correct answer is A.
Q7 FREE PREVIEW
Transformation was discovered by;
aMeselson and Stahl.
bHershey and Chase.
cGriffith.
dWatson and Crick.
✓ Correct answer: c) Griffith.
ExplanationFrederick Griffith discovered the phenomenon of transformation in 1928 while working with Streptococcus pneumoniae bacteria.He showed that harmless bacteria could acquire virulence (ability to cause disease) by taking up genetic material from dead, virulent bacteria.This process is known as transformation.
Q8 FREE PREVIEW
Identify the correct order of organisation of genetic material from largest to smallest:
aGenome, chromosome, nucleotide, gene
bGenome, chromosome, gene, nucleotide
cChromosome, genome, nucleotide, gene
dChromosome, gene, genome, nucleotide
✓ Correct answer: b) Genome, chromosome, gene, nucleotide
ExplanationThe genome is described as the genetic information repository of an organism.The genetic material, i.e., DNA is packaged into thread-like structures called chromosomes.Gene is the segment of DNA that codes for the protein.Nucleotide is the building block of genetic material.Therefore, the correct order of organisation is: Genome - Chromosome - Gene - Nucleotide [New NCERT Class 12th Page No. 83]
