Explanation

DNA fingerprinting involves identifying specific DNA sequences (VNTRs) through a series of well-defined steps.

  • Initially, DNA is isolated and cut into fragments using restriction endonucleases (A).

✓ Correct answer: a)

ρ (rho)

Explanation

The ρ (rho) factor is a protein that plays an important role in the termination of transcription in prokaryotes (like bacteria). It binds to the growing RNA chain and moves along it until it reaches the RNA polymerase. When it catches up, it causes the RNA polymerase to detach from the DNA, stopping transcription.

There are two types of transcription termination in prokaryotes:

  1. Rho-dependent – requires the ρ factor

  2. Rho-independent – relies on a GC-rich hairpin loop in RNA

Other options:

  • σ (sigma) helps start transcription

  • α and γ are not involved in transcription termination

So, the correct answer is A — ρ (rho).
