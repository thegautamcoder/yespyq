Q22

Arrange the following steps of somatic hybridisation in a correct sequence.

A. Digestion of cell walls.

B. Isolation of naked protoplasts.

C. Fusion of protoplasts to get a hybrid protoplast.

D. Isolation of single cells from two different varieties of plants.

E. Growing of hybrid protoplast to form a new plant.

Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

a

E, A, B, C, D

b

D, A, B, C, E

c

E, B, A, D, C

d

D, B, A, E, C

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

D, A, B, C, E

Explanation

Somatic hybridisation: involves fusion of protoplasts from different plant cells to form a hybrid and regenerate a whole plant.

  • First, single cells are isolated from two different plant varieties (D)
  • Then, cell walls are digested using enzymes (A)
  • This leads to formation and isolation of naked protoplasts (B)
  • Next, protoplasts are fused to form a hybrid protoplast (C)
  • Finally, hybrid protoplast is grown to regenerate a new plant (E)

Thus, the correct sequence is: D → A → B → C → E

Q29

Silencing of specific mRNA is possible via RNAi because of -

a

Complementary tRNA

b

Non-complementary ssRNA

c

Complementary dsRNA

d

Inhibitory ssRNA

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

Complementary dsRNA

Explanation

RNA interference (RNAi) is a biological process where gene expression is silenced by destroying specific mRNA molecules. This process is triggered by double-stranded RNA (dsRNA) that is complementary to the target mRNA.

Here’s how it works:

  • The complementary dsRNA is introduced or formed inside the cell.

  • It is processed into small interfering RNAs (siRNAs) by an enzyme called Dicer.

  • These siRNAs bind to the complementary mRNA and form a complex called RISC (RNA-induced silencing complex).

  • RISC then degrades the target mRNA, preventing the production of the protein.

Hence, complementary dsRNA enables RNAi.

Statement I: Transfer RNAs and ribosomal RNA do not interact with mRNA.

In the light of the above statements, choose the most appropriate answer from the options given below

[NEET 2025]

Statement I is correct but Statement II is incorrect

✓ Correct answer: b)

Statement I is incorrect but Statement II is correct

Explanation

Statement I is incorrect because transfer RNA (tRNA) and ribosomal RNA (rRNA) do interact with mRNA during protein synthesis. tRNA brings amino acids by matching its anticodon with the codon on mRNA, while rRNA (part of ribosomes) helps in reading the mRNA and forming peptide bonds.

Statement II is correct. RNA interference (RNAi) is a cellular defense mechanism found in almost all eukaryotic organisms. It helps protect the cell from viruses and controls gene expression. It works by silencing specific mRNA molecules, preventing the production of harmful or unwanted proteins.
