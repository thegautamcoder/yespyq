Which one of the following statements is correct?
aA sterile pistil is called a staminode
bThe seed in grasses is not endospermic
cMango is a parthenocarpic fruit
dA proteinaceous aleurone layer is present in maize grain

✓ Correct answer: d) A proteinaceous aleurone layer is present in maize grain
ExplanationA. A sterile pistil is called a staminode: This statement is incorrect.A sterile pistil (the female reproductive organ) is called a pistillode, while a sterile stamen (the male reproductive organ) is called a staminode.B. The seed in grasses is not endospermic: This statement is incorrect.Seeds in grasses (which are monocots) are typically endospermic (albuminous), meaning they retain the endosperm tissue for nourishment.C. Mango is a parthenocarpic fruit: This statement is incorrect.Parthenocarpic fruits develop without fertilization and are typically seedless (e.g., banana, grapes).Mangoes develop from a fertilized ovary and contain a seed.D. A proteinaceous aleurone layer is present in maize grain: This statement is correct.The maize grain (a type of fruit where the fruit wall is fused with the seed coat) has a distinct outer layer of the endosperm called the aleurone layer, which is rich in proteins and separates the embryo from the starchy endosperm.

Q5

In angiosperms, root hairs arise from which one of the following regions of the root?

[NEET 2026]

a

The root cap zone

b

The region of meristematic activity

c

The region of elongation

d

The region of maturation

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

The region of maturation

Explanation

The root is divided into different regions based on structure and function:

  • Root cap zone: Protects the root tip as it moves through the soil. It does not produce root hairs.
  • Region of meristematic activity: Cells actively divide here, but they are small and undifferentiated; root hairs are not formed in this zone.
  • Region of elongation: Cells increase in length, helping in root growth, but differentiation into root hairs does not occur here.
  • Region of maturation (differentiation): Cells differentiate into specialized structures. Epidermal cells form root hairs in this region, which help in absorption of water and minerals.

Answer: Option D (The region of maturation)

Q7

In racemose inflorescence, _________ .

[NEET 2026]

a

the main axis terminates in a flower

b

the growth is limited

c

flowers are borne in an acropetal succession

d

flowers are solitary

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

flowers are borne in an acropetal succession

Explanation

In racemose inflorescence, the main axis continues to grow and does not terminate in a flower. Flowers arise laterally in a definite sequence.

  • The main axis shows unlimited growth.
  • Flowers are arranged in acropetal succession, meaning older flowers are at the base and younger flowers are towards the apex.
  • Flowers are not solitary; they are arranged along the axis.
  • Option A: Incorrect — main axis does not terminate in a flower (this is cymose).
  • Option B: Incorrect — growth is unlimited.
  • Option C: Correct — flowers show acropetal succession.
  • Option D: Incorrect — flowers are not solitary.

Answer: Option C (flowers are borne in an acropetal succession)

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

A-IV, B-II, C-I, D-III

Explanation

  • Monadelphous – China rose: In this type, the filaments of all stamens are united into one bundle, while the anthers remain free.

  • Diadelphous – Pea: Here, the filaments are united into two bundles. Typically, nine stamens form one bundle and one stamen remains free.

  • Polyadelphous – Citrus: The filaments are grouped into many bundles, with each bundle containing a few stamens.

  • Epiphyllous – Lily: The stamens are attached to the perianth (petal-like structure) instead of the thalamus.

Cassia

d

Pisum

Explanation

Q13

Phyllotaxy is the pattern of arrangement of:

[Re-NEET 2026]

a

leaves

b

flowers

c

fruits

d

sepals

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

leaves

Explanation
  1. Phyllotaxy refers to the pattern of arrangement of leaves on the stem or branch.
  2. It helps in obtaining maximum sunlight for photosynthesis.
  3. Phyllotaxy is of three main types:
    • Alternate
    • Opposite
    • Whorled
  4. The arrangement of flowers, fruits, or sepals is not called phyllotaxy.

Therefore, phyllotaxy is the pattern of arrangement of leaves.
