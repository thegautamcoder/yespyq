Q20

These are regarded as major causes of biodiversity loss:
A. Over exploitation
B. Co-extinction
C. Mutation
D. Habitat loss and fragmentation
E. Migration

Choose the correct option:

[NEET 2024]

a

A, C and D only

b

A, B and D only

c

A, B, C and D only

d

A, B and E only

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

A, B and D only

Explanation

The major causes of biodiversity loss are often referred to as the "Evil Quartet" in ecology. These factors include:

  • Habitat loss and fragmentation
  • Over-exploitation
  • Co-extinction
  • Alien species invasion.

- Over-exploitation (A): Excessive harvesting of species beyond their capacity to recover leads to population decline and extinction.

- Habitat loss and fragmentation (D): Destruction and division of natural habitats is the most important cause of biodiversity loss.

- Co-extinction (B): When one species becomes extinct, the species dependent on it (like parasites, mutualists) also get extinct.

- Mutation (C) is a natural genetic process and not a cause of biodiversity loss.

- Migration (E) is a normal ecological phenomenon and does not cause biodiversity loss.

Hence, the correct combination is A, B and D

Q27

c

d

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

Zoos and botanical gardens

Explanation

Ex-situ conservation means protecting endangered species outside their natural habitat. This method is used when in-situ (on-site) conservation is not possible due to habitat loss or extreme threats. Zoos and botanical gardens are good examples, where animals and plants are taken care of in a controlled environment. These places provide proper food, medical care, and safe conditions for breeding. It also helps in education and research. On the other hand, protected areas, national parks, and wildlife sanctuaries are examples of in-situ conservation, where species are protected in their natural surroundings.
