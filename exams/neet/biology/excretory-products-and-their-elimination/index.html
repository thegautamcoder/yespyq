Q3

Given below are two statements :
Statement I : Concentrated urine is formed due to counter current mechanism in nephron.
Statement II : Counter current mechanism helps to maintain osmotic gradient in the medullary interstitium.
In the light of the above statements, choose the most appropriate answer from the options given below.

[Re-NEET 2024]

a

Statement I is correct but Statement II is incorrect.

b

Statement I is incorrect but Statement II is correct.

c

Both Statement I and Statement II are correct.

d

Both Statement I and Statement II are incorrect.

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

✓ Correct answer: c)

Both Statement I and Statement II are correct.

Explanation

Concentrated urine is formed in the nephron primarily due to the counter current mechanism, which involves the loop of Henle and vasa recta. This mechanism establishes and maintains a high osmotic gradient in the medullary interstitium, allowing reabsorption of water from the collecting duct under the influence of ADH. Therefore, both statements are correct and complement each other in explaining how concentrated urine is produced.

Explanation

Juxta medullary nephrons are characterized by:

  • Long Loop of Henle that extends deep into the renal medulla, which is crucial for the formation of concentrated urine through the counter-current mechanism.

✓ Correct answer: c)

Both Statement I and Statement II are false

Explanation

The descending limb of the loop of Henle is actually permeable to water and impermeable to electrolytes, allowing water reabsorption and concentrating the filtrate.
The proximal convoluted tubule (PCT) is lined with simple cuboidal epithelium (not columnar) possessing a brush border of microvilli, which significantly increases the surface area for effective reabsorption of nutrients, ions, and water.

Q6

Diuresis is prevented by:

a

renin from JG cells via switching off the osmoreceptors.

b

ANF from atria of the heart.

c

aldosterone from adrenal medulla.

d

vasopressin from neurohypophysis.

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

vasopressin from neurohypophysis.

Explanation
  • Vasopressin, also known as antidiuretic hormone (ADH), is secreted by the neurohypophysis (posterior pituitary) and plays a key role in preventing diuresis.
  • It acts on the kidneys to increase water reabsorption by making the distal tubules and collecting ducts more permeable to water.
  • This reduces urine volume and helps conserve body water, effectively preventing diuresis (excessive urination).

Q10

Arrange the following events occurring in the Renin-Angiotensin mechanism in the correct order:
A. Increase in blood pressure and Glomerular filtration rate.
B. Reabsorption of  Na+and water from distal parts of tubule due to Aldosterone.
C. Fall in Glomerular filtration rate.
D. Vasoconstriction by Angiotensin II and release of Aldosterone.
E. Renin converts Angiotensinogen into Angiotensin I, followed by Angiotensin II.
Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

[NEET 2026]

a

C, A, B, D, E

b

A, D, B, E, C

c

A, C, E, B, D

d

C, E, D, B, A

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

C, E, D, B, A

Explanation

Renin–Angiotensin Mechanism: Correct Sequence

The renin–angiotensin–aldosterone system (RAAS) is activated when there is a decrease in blood pressure or glomerular filtration rate (GFR).

Stepwise sequence:

  1. Fall in glomerular filtration rate (GFR) - This is the initiating event that stimulates the juxtaglomerular (JG) cells to release renin. → (C)
  2. Formation of angiotensin II - Renin converts angiotensinogen into angiotensin I, which is further converted into angiotensin II. → (E)
  3. Action of angiotensin II - Angiotensin II causes vasoconstriction and stimulates the release of aldosterone from the adrenal cortex. → (D)
  4. Role of aldosterone - Aldosterone increases reabsorption of Na⁺ and water from the distal parts of the nephron. → (B)
  5. Restoration of blood pressure and GFR - Increased reabsorption leads to an increase in blood volume, blood pressure, and GFR. → (A)

Correct order: C → E → D → B → A

Final answer: Option D
