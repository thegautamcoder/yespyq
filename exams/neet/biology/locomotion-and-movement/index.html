Q9

Select the correct statements regarding mechanism of muscle contraction.
A. It is initiated by a signal sent by CNS via sensory neuron.
B. Neurotransmitter generates action potential in the sarcolemma.
C. Increased \(\mathrm{Ca}^{++}\)level leads to the binding of calcium with troponin on actin filaments.
D. Masking of active site for actin is activated.
E. Utilising the energy from ATP hydrolysis to form cross bridge.

Choose the most appropriate answer from the options given below:

[Re-NEET 2024]

a

B, C and E only

b

C, D and E only

c

A and D only

d

B, D and E only

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

B, C and E only

Explanation

Muscle contraction is initiated by a signal sent by the central nervous system via a motor neuron.In the mechanism of muscle contraction, the neurotransmitter (usually acetylcholine) is released at the neuromuscular junction and generates an action potential in the sarcolemma. This leads to an increase in intracellular calcium ion (Ca²⁺) levels, which bind to troponin on the actin filaments, causing a conformational change that exposes the active sites. Using energy from ATP hydrolysis, myosin heads form cross-bridges with actin, resulting in contraction. Statement A is incorrect because motor neurons (not sensory neurons) initiate the signal, and statement D is misleading as contraction involves unmasking of actin sites, not masking.

acetyl choline

Explanation

Muscle contraction begins at the neuromuscular junction where a nerve impulse arrives.

  • The motor neuron releases acetylcholine (ACh)
  • ACh binds to receptors on the muscle membrane

Q12

Which of the following statements are correct with reference to human endoskeleton?
A. Human skull is monocondylic.
B. The joint between any two adjoining vertebrae is a cartilaginous joint.
C. In human beings, the number of cervical vertebrae is seven.
D. All ribs except the last 2 pairs are bicephalic.
E. The occipital bone of skull is articulated with atlas vertebra.
Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

[NEET 2026]

a

A, B and D only

b

B and E only

c

B, C and E only

d

C, D and E only

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

C, D and E only

Explanation

  • In the human endoskeleton, different bones and joints show specific structural features.

  • Statement A is incorrect. The human skull is dicondylic (has two occipital condyles), not monocondylic.

  • Statement B is incorrect. The joints between adjoining vertebrae are slightly movable (cartilaginous) only between vertebral bodies, while facet joints are synovial; hence not entirely cartilaginous.

  • Statement C is correct. Humans have seven cervical vertebrae.

  • Statement D is correct. All ribs except the last two pairs (floating ribs) are bicephalic (having two articulation points).

  • Statement E is correct. The occipital bone articulates with the atlas vertebra.

  • Thus, the correct statements are C, D and E.

  • Therefore, the correct answer is Option D: C, D and E only.

Q17

Choose the correct statements regarding muscle contraction.
A. A motor neuron carries a signal sent by the Central Nervous System (CNS) to the sarcolemma of the muscle fibre.
B. The neural signal generates an action potential which causes the release of Ca++ into sarcoplasm.
C. Increase in Ca++ inactivates the actin for breaking cross bridges.
D. Actin binds to the myosin head to form a cross bridge.
E. Shortening of sarcomere takes place, by pulling actin filaments towards the centre of ' A ' band.
Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

[NEET 2026]

a

A, B, D and E only

b

C and D only

c

C and E only

d

A and B only

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

A, B, D and E only

Explanation
  • Muscle contraction is explained by the sliding filament theory, where actin and myosin filaments interact under neural control.
  • Statement A is correct. A motor neuron transmits impulses from the CNS to the sarcolemma (muscle membrane) at the neuromuscular junction, initiating contraction.
  • Statement B is correct. The action potential spreads through the sarcolemma and T-tubules, causing the sarcoplasmic reticulum to release Ca²⁺ into the sarcoplasm.
  • Statement C is incorrect. Ca²⁺ binds to troponin, which shifts tropomyosin and exposes active sites on actin, thereby activating actin for cross-bridge formation (not inactivating it).
  • Statement D is correct. The myosin head binds to exposed active sites on actin, forming cross-bridges essential for contraction.
  • Statement E is correct. During contraction, actin filaments slide inward, pulling towards the centre of the A band, leading to shortening of sarcomere (I-band shortens, A-band remains constant).
  • Thus, the correct statements are A, B, D and E.
  • Therefore, the correct answer is Option A: A, B, D and E only.

Q19

The number of vertebrae in a human is:

[Re-NEET 2026]

a

7

b

12

c

26

d

206

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

26

Explanation

The vertebral column (backbone) of an adult human consists of 26 vertebrae. It is divided into five regions:

  • 7 cervical vertebrae in the neck region
  • 12 thoracic vertebrae in the chest region
  • 5 lumbar vertebrae in the abdominal region
  • 1 sacrum, formed by the fusion of 5 sacral vertebrae
  • 1 coccyx, formed by the fusion of 4 coccygeal vertebrae

Thus, the total number of vertebrae in an adult human is:

7 + 12 + 5 + 1 + 1 = 26

Therefore, the correct answer is (C) 26.
