Q6

Which of following organisms cannot fix nitrogen?
A. Azotobacter B. Oscillatoria
C. Anabaena D. Volvox
E. Nostoc
Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

[NEET 2025]

a

B only

b

E only

c

A only

d

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

D only

Explanation

Nitrogen fixation is the process of converting atmospheric nitrogen (N₂) into usable forms like ammonia. This process is carried out by certain bacteria and cyanobacteria.

  • Azotobacter – a free-living nitrogen-fixing bacteria in soil.

  • Oscillatoria – a cyanobacterium that can fix nitrogen.

  • Anabaena – a filamentous cyanobacterium with specialized cells (heterocysts) for nitrogen fixation.

  • Nostoc – another cyanobacterium capable of nitrogen fixation.

  • Volvox – a green algae, not involved in nitrogen fixation.

So, the only organism in the list that cannot fix nitrogen is Volvox.
Correct option: D. D only.

Q9

What is the reason behind production of large holes in 'Swiss Cheese' ?

[NEET 2026]

a

The production of large amount of CO2 by Clostridium butylicum

b

The production of large amount of CO2 and H2 by Trichoderma polysporum

c

The production of large amount of CO2 and H2 by lactic acid bacteria called Lactobacillus

d

The production of large amount of CO2 by Propionibacterium sharmanii

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

The production of large amount of CO2 by Propionibacterium sharmanii

Explanation
  • Swiss cheese is characterized by the presence of large holes or “eyes” formed during its ripening process due to microbial activity. Certain bacteria ferment lactic acid and release gases that get trapped within the curd, forming these characteristic holes.
  • Option A: Clostridium butylicum produces CO₂ in some fermentation processes, but it is not involved in Swiss cheese formation; hence incorrect.
  • Option B: Trichoderma polysporum is a fungus used for producing cyclosporin A and does not produce gases responsible for cheese holes; hence incorrect.
  • Option C: Lactic acid bacteria such as Lactobacillus mainly produce lactic acid and are involved in curd formation, not gas formation of this type; hence incorrect.
  • Option D: Propionibacterium sharmanii produces a large amount of CO₂ during ripening of Swiss cheese. The trapped CO₂ forms bubbles, resulting in the characteristic large holes; hence correct.
  • Hence, The production of large holes in Swiss cheese is due to the release of carbon dioxide by Propionibacterium sharmanii.
Q10

Streptokinase produced by bacterium Streptococcus is used for

[NEET 2025]

a

Liver disease treatment

b

Removing clots from blood vessels

c

Curd production

d

Ethanol production

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

Removing clots from blood vessels

Explanation

Streptokinase is an enzyme produced by the bacterium Streptococcus. It is used as a medicine to dissolve blood clots that block blood vessels. This is especially useful in treating patients with heart attacks or strokes caused by clot formation. Streptokinase works by converting plasminogen (a protein in blood) into plasmin, which breaks down fibrin — the main protein in blood clots. By removing the clot, it helps restore normal blood flow. It is a life-saving drug and must be used under medical supervision. It is not used in liver disease, curd, or ethanol production.
