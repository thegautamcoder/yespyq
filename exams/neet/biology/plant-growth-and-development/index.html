ExplanationEthylene is a natural, gaseous plant hormone that serves as the primary regulator and initiator of the fruit ripening process in many fruits (climacteric fruits like bananas, tomatoes, apples, etc.). Q19

Heterophyllous development in response to environment is an example of which of the following phenomena?

[NEET 2026]

a

Dedifferentiation

b

Elasticity

c

Redifferentiation

d

Plasticity

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

Plasticity

Explanation

Heterophylly refers to the presence of different types of leaves on the same plant. When this variation occurs due to environmental factors (such as light, water, or temperature), it is termed environmental heterophylly.

This is an example of phenotypic plasticity, where a single genotype can produce different phenotypes in response to environmental conditions.

  • Dedifferentiation: Cells regain the capacity to divide.
  • Redifferentiation: Cells mature again after dedifferentiation.
  • Elasticity: Not related to developmental changes in this context.
  • Plasticity: Ability of plants to modify their growth and development in response to the environment.

Answer: Option D (Plasticity)

Q26

Which one of the following is not a characteristic of plant cells in the phase of elongation?

[NEET 2026]

a

Increased vacuolation

b

Large conspicuous nuclei

c

Cell enlargement

d

New cell wall deposition

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

Large conspicuous nuclei

Explanation

The region of elongation in roots is characterized by rapid increase in cell size. The main features of cells in this phase include:

  • Cell enlargement due to water uptake.
  • Increased vacuolation, where small vacuoles fuse to form a large central vacuole.
  • New cell wall deposition, allowing the cell to expand.

However, large conspicuous nuclei are a feature of actively dividing cells in the meristematic region, not the elongation phase. In elongating cells, the nucleus becomes less prominent as vacuolation increases.

Answer: Option B (Large conspicuous nuclei)

Q27

Length of the stem at time 0 is 20 cm. The arithmetic growth rate is 30 cm per day. What is the length of the stem at the end of the 7th day?

[Re-NEET 2026]

a

50 cm

b

170 cm

c

230 cm

d

460 cm

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

230 cm

Explanation
  • According to the formula for arithmetic growth:
    Lₜ = L₀ + rt
  • Where:
    L₀ = initial length at time 0 = 20 cm
    r = growth rate = 30 cm/day
    t = time = 7 days
  • Substituting values:
    L₇ = 20 + (30 × 7)
  • Calculation:
    L₇ = 20 + 210
    L₇ = 230 cm
  • Therefore, the length of the stem at the end of 7th day is:
    230 cm
