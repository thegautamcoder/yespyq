Q10

Which of the following are characteristics of prokaryotic cells?

(a) Ribosomes are made of 50S and 30S subunits

(b) They can have plasmids

(c) They contain mesosome

(d) They have peroxisomes

Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

[Re-NEET 2026]

a

(b) and (c) only

b

(a) and (c) only

c

(a), (c) and (d) only

d

(a), (b) and (c) only

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

(a), (b) and (c) only

Explanation

Correct answer: (D)

Prokaryotic cells possess several characteristic features:

  • (a) Ribosomes are made of 50S and 30S subunits
    Prokaryotic ribosomes are 70S, consisting of a 50S large subunit and a 30S small subunit.
  • (b) They can have plasmids
    Many prokaryotes, especially bacteria, contain plasmids, which are small, circular, extrachromosomal DNA molecules.
  • (c) They contain mesosomes
    Mesosomes are infoldings of the plasma membrane in prokaryotic cells and help in cell wall formation, DNA replication, and respiration.
  • (d) They have peroxisomes
    Peroxisomes are membrane-bound organelles found in eukaryotic cells. Prokaryotic cells lack membrane-bound organelles.

Therefore, statements (a), (b), and (c) are correct, while (d) is incorrect.

Q11

From the statements given below choose the correct option:
A. The eukaryotic ribosomes are 80S and prokaryotic ribosomes are 70S.
B. Each ribosome has two sub-units.

C. The two sub-units of 80S ribosome are 60S and 40S while that of 70S are 50S and 30S.
D. The two sub-units of 80S ribosome are 60S and 20S and that of 70S are 50S and 20S.
E. The two sub-units of 80S are 60S and 30S and that of 70S are 50S and 30S.

[NEET 2025]

a

A, B, E are true

b

B, D, E are true

c

А, B, С are true

d

A, B, D are true

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

А, B, С are true

Explanation

  • Statement A is true: In cells, eukaryotic ribosomes are 80S and prokaryotic ribosomes are 70S, where "S" refers to Svedberg units, a measure of sedimentation rate during centrifugation.

  • Statement B is true: All ribosomes (both prokaryotic and eukaryotic) are made of two sub-units — one large and one small.

  • Statement C is true: The 80S ribosome (in eukaryotes) is made up of 60S (large) and 40S (small) subunits. The 70S ribosome (in prokaryotes) has 50S (large) and 30S (small) subunits.

Q14

Given below are two statements : one is labelled as Assertion (A) and the other is labelled as Reason (R).
Assertion (A) : The primary function of the Golgi apparatus is to package the materials made by the endoplasmic reticulum and deliver it to intracellular targets and outside the cell.
Reason (R): Vesicles containing materials made by the endoplasmic reticulum fuse with the cis face of the Golgi apparatus, and they are modified and released from the trans face of the Golgi apparatus.
In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below:

[NEET 2025]

a

A is true but R is false

b

A is false but R is true

c

Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A

d

Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A

Explanation

The Golgi apparatus is an important cell organelle whose primary function is to modify, sort, and package materials (like proteins and lipids) made by the endoplasmic reticulum (ER). These materials are delivered to their destinations inside or outside the cell.

Vesicles from the ER carry these materials and fuse with the cis face (the receiving side) of the Golgi. After modification, the materials are released from the trans face (the shipping side).

Since Reason (R) correctly explains Assertion (A), the correct answer is Option C.

✓ Correct answer: d)

The correct option is D. Both Statement I and Statement II are false.

The Golgi complex consists of flattened, membranous cisternae located near the nucleus. It has a cis face (forming face, towards ER) and a trans face (maturing face, towards plasma membrane). Q22

A specialised membranous structure in a prokaryotic cell which helps in cell wall formation, DNA replication and respiration is:

[NEET 2025]

a

b

Endoplasmic Reticulum

c

Mesosome

d

Chromatophores

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

Mesosome

Explanation

In prokaryotic cells (like bacteria), a mesosome is a specialized infolding of the plasma membrane. It plays important roles in:

  • Cell wall formation during cell division

  • DNA replication and distribution to daughter cells

  • Respiration, as it provides surface area for respiratory enzymes

Mesosomes are somewhat similar to mitochondria in function, although prokaryotes lack mitochondria.

  • Cristae are folds of the inner membrane in mitochondria (found only in eukaryotes)

  • Endoplasmic reticulum is also only in eukaryotes

  • Chromatophores are pigment-containing structures, mostly in photosynthetic bacteria

Thus, the correct answer is C — Mesosome.

A, C and E only

B, C and E only

A, B and D only

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

✓ Correct answer: d)

Explanation

  • The plasma membrane of eukaryotic cells follows the fluid mosaic model and has a specific structural organisation.
