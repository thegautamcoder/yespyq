Q8

Which of the following are primary consumers in a food chain?

[Re-NEET 2026]

a

Parasites

b

Predators

c

Herbivores

d

Carnivores

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

Herbivores

Explanation

In any fundamental ecological food chain operating within a healthy ecosystem, the critical initial trophic level is universally occupied by the autotrophic primary producers, typically green photosynthesizing plants. Following these producers, the strict secondary trophic level is always composed entirely of the primary consumers. Biologically, primary consumers are exclusively herbivores—organisms perfectly adapted to feed directly and exclusively upon the plant producers to obtain their vital cellular energy. Common examples extensively include various insects, grazing cattle, and deer. Conversely, strict carnivores and apex predators sequentially occupy the significantly higher, successive trophic levels by subsequently consuming these initial herbivores.

Match List I with List II :

List I List II
A. Productivity I. Gross primary productivity minus respiration losses
B. Net primary productivity II. Rate of formation of new organic matter by consumers
C. Gross primary productivity III. Rate of biomass production
D. Secondary productivity IV. Rate of production of organic matter during photosynthesis

Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

[NEET 2026]

a

A-I, B-II, C-III, D-IV

b

A-III, B-I, C-IV, D-II

c

A-III, B-I, C-II, D-IV

d

A-I, B-III, C-IV, D-II

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

A-III, B-I, C-IV, D-II

Explanation
  • A. Productivity - This is a general term that refers to the rate of biomass production in an ecosystem. It can be primary (by producers) or secondary (by consumers).
    → III (Rate of biomass production)

  • B. Net primary productivity (NPP) - Plants produce organic matter through photosynthesis (GPP), but a part of this is used in respiration. The remaining biomass is stored and available to herbivores.
    So, NPP = GPP − respiration losses.
    → I (Gross primary productivity minus respiration losses)|

  • C. Gross primary productivity (GPP) - This is the total rate of production of organic matter by producers during photosynthesis, before any loss due to respiration.
    → IV (Rate of production of organic matter during photosynthesis)

  • D. Secondary productivity - This refers to the rate at which consumers (herbivores, carnivores) convert ingested food into new organic matter (biomass).
    → II (Rate of formation of new organic matter by consumers)

Correct matching: A–III, B–I, C–IV, D–II

Q10

Which of the following statements are correct?

(a) Energy flow from producers to consumers is unidirectional

(b) Energy pyramid can never be inverted

(c) Transfer of energy follows the 1% law

Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

[Re-NEET 2026]

a

(a), (b) and (c)

b

(a) and (b) only

c

(a) and (c) only

d

(b) and (c) only

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

(a) and (b) only

Explanation

(a) Energy flow from producers to consumers is unidirectional

  • Correct. Energy moves in one direction:
    Sun → Producers → Consumers → Decomposers
    It does not return back to the previous trophic level.

(b) Energy pyramid can never be inverted

  • Correct. The energy pyramid is always upright because energy decreases at each successive trophic level.

(c) Transfer of energy follows the 1% law

  • Incorrect. Energy transfer follows the 10% law (Lindeman's law), where only about 10% of energy is transferred from one trophic level to the next.

Final Answer: Option B — (a) and (b) only

Q11

Which of the following is the unit of productivity of an Ecosystem?

[NEET 2025]

a

Kcal m-3

b

(Kcal m-2)yr-1

c

gm-2

d

Kcal m-2

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

(Kcal m-2)yr-1

Explanation

In an ecosystem, productivity refers to the rate at which energy is produced or stored by organisms, mainly plants (producers) through photosynthesis. It is usually expressed as energy produced per unit area per unit time.

  • Kcal (kilocalorie) represents energy

  • m² (square meter) represents area

  • yr⁻¹ (per year) represents time

So, (Kcal m⁻²) yr⁻¹ is the correct unit for productivity, especially primary productivity (like Gross and Net Primary Productivity).

Other options:

  • Kcal m⁻³ — used for volume-based measurements

  • gm⁻² — measures biomass, not energy

  • Kcal m⁻² — lacks time component

Hence, correct answer: Option (B).

Q12

Ecological pyramids represent the relationship between the organisms at different trophic levels and they are generally inverted for :

[NEET 2026]

a

Pyramid of energy in pond ecosystem

b

Pyramid of biomass in sea

c

Pyramid of number in grassland

d

Pyramid of biomass in grassland

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

Pyramid of biomass in sea

Explanation

  • Ecological pyramids depict trophic relationships in terms of number, biomass, or energy across different trophic levels in an ecosystem.

  • The pyramid of energy is always upright in all ecosystems, including ponds, because energy decreases at each successive trophic level due to loss as heat.

  • The pyramid of numbers in grassland is generally upright, as producers are most abundant.

  • The pyramid of biomass in grassland is also upright since producer biomass is greater than that of herbivores and carnivores.

  • In contrast, the pyramid of biomass in aquatic ecosystems (sea) is typically inverted because phytoplankton (producers) have low standing biomass but high turnover rate compared to zooplankton.

  • Therefore, the correct example of an inverted ecological pyramid is pyramid of biomass in sea (Option B).

Q15

Match List I with List II :

List I List II
A. Decomposition I. Accumulation of dark coloured amorphous colloidal substance
B. Detritus II. Release of inorganic nutrients by activity of microbes in soil
C. Mineralisation III. Breaking down of complex organic matter into inorganic substances
D. Humification IV. Dead remains of plants and animals including fecal matter

Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

[NEET 2026]

a

A-I, B-II, C-III, D-IV

b

A-IV, B-III, C-I, D-II

c

A-III, B-IV, C-II, D-I

d

A-III, B-II, C-I, D-IV

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

A-III, B-IV, C-II, D-I

Explanation

  • A. Decomposition → III
    Decomposition is the overall process in which complex organic matter (dead plants, animals, waste) is broken down into simpler inorganic substances like CO₂, water, and minerals by decomposers (bacteria and fungi).

  • B. Detritus → IV
    Detritus includes dead remains of plants and animals, along with fecal matter. It forms the raw material on which decomposers act.

  • C. Mineralisation → II
    Mineralisation is a step of decomposition in which microbes convert organic substances into inorganic nutrients (like nitrates, phosphates), making them available to plants.

  • D. Humification → I
    Humification is the process leading to the formation and accumulation of humus, a dark-coloured, amorphous, colloidal substance that is resistant to further decomposition and improves soil fertility.

Final Answer: A–III, B–IV, C–II, D–I (Option C)

Q16

Given below are two statements:
Statement I: In-ecosystem, there is unidirectional flow of energy of sun from producers to consumers.

Statement II: Ecosystems are exempted from 2nd law of thermodynamics.
In the light of the above statements, choose the most appropriate answer from the options given below:

[NEET 2025]

a

Statement I is correct but statement II is incorrect

b

Statement I is incorrect but statement II is correct

c

Both statement I and statement II are correct

d

Both statement I and statement II are incorrect

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

Statement I is correct but statement II is incorrect

Explanation

  • Statement I is correct:
    In an ecosystem, energy flows in a unidirectional manner — it starts from the sun, is captured by producers (plants) through photosynthesis, and then passes on to herbivores (primary consumers), and further to carnivores and decomposers. This flow never reverses, making it unidirectional.

  • Statement II is incorrect:
    Ecosystems are not exempt from the second law of thermodynamics, which states that energy transformations are inefficient, and some energy is always lost as heat. This is why energy decreases at each trophic level.

Hence, Option 1 is correct.
