Q5

A population of diploid organisms is at Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium. If the frequency of allele A is 0.1, the frequency of AA is ___________.

[Re-NEET 2026]

a

0.01

b

0.02

c

0.10

d

0.99

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

0.01

Explanation

For a population in Hardy–Weinberg equilibrium:

p2+2pq+q2=1p^2 + 2pq + q^2 = 1

Where:

  • pp = frequency of allele A
  • qq = frequency of allele a
  • p2p^2 = frequency of genotype AA
  • 2pq2pq = frequency of genotype Aa
  • q2q^2 = frequency of genotype aa

Given: p=0.1p = 0.1

Frequency of AA genotype: AA=p2AA = p^2=(0.1)2= (0.1)^2=0.01= 0.01

Therefore, the frequency of AA = 0.01.

Correct Answer: Option A (0.01)

Constant gene pool

Explanation

The correct answer is A. Constant gene pool. A constant gene pool means allele frequencies remain unchanged, which is a key condition for Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium. In contrast, genetic recombination, genetic drift, and gene migration introduce changes in allele frequencies, disrupting the equilibrium. Q11

Which of the following is not an example of convergent evolution?

[NEET 2026]

a

Eyes of octopus and mammals

b

Fore limbs of whales and bats

c

Wings of butterflies and birds

d

Flippers of penguins and dolphins

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

Fore limbs of whales and bats

Explanation
  • Convergent evolution refers to the development of similar structures in unrelated organisms due to similar environmental pressures, resulting in analogous organs.
  • Option A (eyes of octopus and mammals) is an example of convergent evolution, as these structures evolved independently but perform similar functions.
  • Option C (wings of butterflies and birds) is also an example of convergent evolution, as both are used for flight but have different structural origins.
  • Option D (flippers of penguins and dolphins) is another example of convergent evolution, as both are adapted for swimming but evolved independently.
  • Option B (fore limbs of whales and bats) is not an example of convergent evolution. These are homologous organs, derived from a common ancestor but modified for different functions (swimming in whales and flying in bats), representing divergent evolution.
  • Therefore, the correct answer is Option B: Fore limbs of whales and bats.

Explanation

Q13

Natural selection can lead to _______.

(a) stabilisation

(b) genetic drift

(c) directional change

(d) disruption

Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

[Re-NEET 2026]

a

(a) only

b

(a), (c) and (d) only

c

(a), (b), (c) and (d) only

d

(a) and (c) only

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

(a), (c) and (d) only

Explanation
  • Natural selection is a process where organisms with better-adapted traits survive and reproduce more successfully.
  • It can lead to stabilisation, where average traits are favoured and extreme variations are reduced.
  • It can also cause directional change, where one extreme trait is consistently favoured, shifting the population in one direction.
  • Another outcome is disruptive selection, where both extreme traits are favoured while intermediate forms are eliminated.
  • However, genetic drift is not a result of natural selection; it is a random change in allele frequencies due to chance events, especially in small populations.
  • Therefore, options (a), (c), and (d) are correct, while (b) is incorrect.

Q15

Evolution of human appears parallel to the progressive development of brain and language skills. As such, the evolution of individual species in the sequence of their appearance is:

[NEET 2026]

a

Homo habilis → Homo erectus → Ramapithecus → Neanderthal → Homo sapiens

b

Ramapithecus → Homo habilis → Homo erectus → Neanderthal → Homo sapiens

c

Homo sapiens → Ramapithecus → Homo habilis → Neanderthal → Homo erectus

d

Neanderthal → Ramapithecus → Homo habilis → Homo erectus → Homo sapiens

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

Ramapithecus → Homo habilis → Homo erectus → Neanderthal → Homo sapiens

Explanation

  • Human evolution reflects a gradual increase in brain capacity, tool use, and development of language across successive hominid forms.

  • The earliest form in the sequence is Ramapithecus, showing more human-like traits compared to apes.

  • It was followed by Homo habilis, known as “handy man,” associated with primitive stone tools and a slightly enlarged brain.

  • Next came Homo erectus, who showed improved tool technology, upright posture, and possible control of fire.

  • Neanderthals appeared later, with a large brain, robust body, and evidence of cultural and social behaviour.

  • Finally, Homo sapiens evolved, characterized by advanced cognition, complex language, and symbolic thinking.

  • Hence, the correct evolutionary sequence is Ramapithecus → Homo habilis → Homo erectus → Neanderthal → Homo sapiens (Option B).

Q19

Sweet potato and potato represent a certain type of evolution. Select the correct combination of terms to explain the evolution.

[NEET 2025]

a

Homology, convergent

b

Analogy, divergent

c

Analogy, convergent

d

Homology, divergent

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

Analogy, convergent

Explanation

Sweet potato (a root modification) and potato (a stem modification) both store food and look similar, but they originate from different plant parts. This means they perform the same function (food storage) but have different origins and structures.

This is an example of an analogous organ — same function, different origin.

Such similarity due to similar environmental pressure or function (not due to common ancestry) is called convergent evolution.

So, the evolution between sweet potato and potato is explained by:
Analogy (similar function)
Convergent evolution (not due to common origin)

Hence, Option C is correct.

Q20

Given below are two statements: one is labelled as Assertion A and the other is labelled as Reason R.

Assertion A: Forelimbs of human and bats are homologous.

Reason R: Forelimbs of humans and bats have similar anatomical structure.

In the light of the above statements, choose the most appropriate answer from the options given below:

[Re-NEET 2026]

a

Both A and R are correct and R is the correct explanation of A

b

Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A

c

A is true but R is false

d

A is false but R is true

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

Both A and R are correct and R is the correct explanation of A

Explanation

Homologous organs are organs that have the same basic anatomical structure and common origin, but may perform different functions.

  • Assertion A: Forelimbs of humans and bats are homologous.
    This statement is correct because the forelimbs of humans and bats possess the same basic skeletal plan inherited from a common ancestor, although they perform different functions (grasping in humans and flying in bats).
  • Reason R: Forelimbs of humans and bats have similar anatomical structure.
    This statement is correct because both forelimbs contain the same set of bones arranged in a similar pattern (humerus, radius, ulna, carpals, metacarpals and phalanges).

The similarity in anatomical structure due to common ancestry is the basis for considering them homologous organs. Therefore, the reason correctly explains the assertion.

Correct Answer: A. Both A and R are correct and R is the correct explanation of A.
