Q6

The following are the stages of life cycle of Plasmodium. Arrange the stages in the proper order.
A. The parasites reproduce asexually in RBC's, bursting the cells.
B. The parasites reproduce asexually in liver cells, bursting the cells and releasing into blood.
C. Gametocytes develop in RBC's.
D. Sporozoites reach the liver through the blood.
E. Female mosquito injects sporozoites into humans during bite.

Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

[NEET 2026]

a

A, B, C, D, E

b

E, D, B, A, C

c

C, A, B, D, E

d

E, C, D, B, A

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

E, D, B, A, C

Explanation
  • In the life cycle of Plasmodium in humans, infection begins when an infected female mosquito injects parasites into the bloodstream.
  • First, sporozoites are injected into humans during the mosquito bite (E).
  • These sporozoites travel through blood to the liver (D).
  • In the liver, parasites reproduce asexually and burst liver cells, releasing into blood (B).
  • They then infect RBCs and reproduce asexually, causing RBCs to burst (A).
  • Finally, some parasites differentiate into gametocytes in RBCs (C).
  • Thus, the correct order is: E → D → B → A → C
  • Therefore, the correct answer is Option B.

Q10

After maturation, in primary lymphoid organs, the lymphocytes migrate for interaction with antigens to secondary lymphoid organ(s)/tissue(s) like:
A. thymus

B. bone marrow
C. spleen

D. lymph nodes
E. Peyer's patches
Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

[NEET 2025]

a

E, A, B only

b

C, D, E only

c

B, C, D only

d

A, B, C only

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

C, D, E only

Explanation

Primary lymphoid organs are where lymphocytes mature, and they include the bone marrow (for B cells) and thymus (for T cells). After maturation, lymphocytes move to secondary lymphoid organs, where they encounter antigens and initiate immune responses.

The secondary lymphoid organs/tissues include:

  • Spleen (C) – filters blood and traps blood-borne antigens.

  • Lymph nodes (D) – filter lymph and are sites of immune cell activation.

  • Peyer’s patches (E) – found in the small intestine and handle gut-associated antigens.

So, the correct secondary lymphoid organs are C, D, and E, hence Option B is correct.

Q11

Which of the following type of immunity is present at the time of birth and is a nonspecific type of defence in the human body?

[NEET 2025]

a

Cell-mediated Immunity

b

Humoral Immunity

c

Acquired Immunity

d

Innate Immunity

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

Innate Immunity

Explanation

Innate immunity is the immunity present from birth and provides a nonspecific defense against all kinds of pathogens. It does not depend on prior exposure to any microorganism. This type of immunity includes physical barriers (like skin), chemical barriers (like saliva and stomach acid), and cellular defenses (like phagocytic white blood cells).

Unlike acquired immunity, which develops over time and is specific to particular pathogens, innate immunity acts quickly and is the first line of defense in the human body. Therefore, the correct answer is Option D – Innate Immunity.
