Identify the site where Wuchereria bancrofti is normally found in the human body.
aMuscles of the legs
bBlood vessels of the thigh region
cSkin between the fingers
dLymphatic vessels of the lower limbs
✓ Correct answer: d) Lymphatic vessels of the lower limbs
ExplanationWuchereria bancrofti is a filarial parasite causing lymphatic filariasis.It resides in the lymphatic vessels, commonly affecting lower limbs.This leads to swelling and elephantiasis.Transmitted via mosquitoes, it's a major tropical disease.Symptoms include lymphedema and swelling.
Q2 FREE PREVIEW
The cell-mediated immunity inside the human body is carried out by:
aErythrocytes
bT-lymphocytes
cB-lymphocytes
dThrombocytes
✓ Correct answer: b) T-lymphocytes
ExplanationThe correct answer is B: T-lymphocytes.Cell-mediated immunity is the branch of adaptive immunity that does not involve antibodies.It is carried out by T-lymphocytes (T- cells), which: Recognize antigen-presenting cells Directly kill infected or cancerous cells Release cytokines to activate other immune cells B-lymphocytes are responsible for humoral (antibody-mediated) immunity, not cell-mediated immunity.Erythrocytes (red blood cells) and thrombocytes (platelets) have no role in adaptive immunity.
