Q12

Match List-I with List-II :

List I – Organisms List II – Mode of Nutrition
A. Euglenoid I. Parasitic
B. Dinoflagellate II. Saprophytic
C. Slime mould III. Photosynthetic
D. Plasmodium IV. Switching between photosynthetic and heterotrophic mode

Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

[Re-NEET 2024]

a

A-III, B-IV, C-II, D-I

b

A-IV, B-II, C-I, D-III

c

A-IV, B-III, C-II, D-I

d

A-IV, B-II, C-III, D-I

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

A-IV, B-III, C-II, D-I

Explanation

A. Euglenoid – Switching between photosynthetic and heterotrophic mode
Euglenoids have mixotrophic nutrition. They photosynthesise in sunlight using chloroplasts, but in darkness they absorb dissolved organic nutrients, switching to heterotrophic mode.

B. Dinoflagellate – Photosynthetic
Most dinoflagellates possess photosynthetic pigments like chlorophylls and carotenoids. They are free-living, aquatic, and perform autotrophic nutrition, forming an important part of marine phytoplankton.

C. Slime mould – Saprophytic
Slime moulds feed on decaying organic matter, bacteria, and detritus. Their saprophytic mode helps recycle nutrients in ecosystems, especially in moist, decaying habitats.

D. Plasmodium – Parasitic
Plasmodium is an obligate parasite living inside host cells. It depends completely on the host for nutrition and causes malaria by invading human liver and blood cells.
