✓ Correct answer: b) more than that in the blood
ExplanationThe partial pressure of oxygen (pO₂) in the alveoli of the lungs is higher than in the deoxygenated blood arriving via pulmonary arteries.In alveoli, pO₂ is around 104 mmHg, while in deoxygenated blood it is about 40 mmHg.This difference allows oxygen to diffuse from alveoli into the blood.Carbon dioxide moves in the opposite direction due to its higher partial pressure in blood.This gas exchange maintains proper oxygenation of blood for delivery to body tissues.

dFree in blood plasma

✓ Correct answer: c) Bicarbonate in blood plasma and RBCs
ExplanationThe bulk of carbon dioxide (CO₂) released from body tissues into the blood is transported mainly as bicarbonate ions (HCO₃⁻).About 70% of CO₂ enters red blood cells, where it is converted to carbonic acid by carbonic anhydrase and then dissociates into bicarbonate and hydrogen ions.The bicarbonate then diffuses into the blood plasma (with chloride shift maintaining ionic balance).A smaller fraction is transported as carbamino-haemoglobin (~20–25%) and an even smaller amount as dissolved CO₂ (~5– 10%).Therefore, the correct option is C: Bicarbonate in blood plasma and RBCs.

Q15

Which of the following factors are favourable for the formation of oxyhaemoglobin in alveoli?

[NEET 2024]

a

Low pCO2 and High temperature

b

High pO2 and High pCO2

c

High pO2 and Lesser H+ concentration

d

Low pCO2 and High H+ concentration

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

High pO2 and Lesser H+ concentration

Explanation

Inside the alveoli, oxygen binds easily with haemoglobin to form oxyhaemoglobin because:

  • The oxygen level (pO₂) is high.
  • The carbon dioxide level (pCO₂) and H⁺ concentration are low, so the environment is less acidic (higher pH).
  • These conditions make haemoglobin’s affinity for oxygen stronger.
  • Hence, oxyhaemoglobin formation is most efficient under high pO₂ and low H⁺ conditions.

Incorrect Options:

  • A – high temp reduces affinity
  • B – high CO₂ reduces binding
  • D – high H⁺ causes oxygen to dissociate.

Q20

In humans, respiration occurs in the following steps. Arrange these steps in the correct order.
A. Diffusion of O2 and CO2 between blood and tissues
B. Diffusion of O2 and CO2 across alveolar membrane
C. Pulmonary ventilation by which atmospheric air is drawn in and CO2 rich alveolar air is released out
D. Cellular respiration
E. Transport of gases by the blood

Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

[NEET 2026]

a

A, B, C, D, E

b

E, A, C, D, B

c

C, A, B, E, D

d

C, B, E, A, D

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

C, B, E, A, D

Explanation
  • Respiration in humans occurs in a sequential manner involving ventilation, gas exchange, transport, and utilization of gases.
  • First, pulmonary ventilation takes place, where atmospheric air is inhaled and CO₂-rich air is exhaled (C).
  • Next, diffusion of O₂ and CO₂ across the alveolar membrane occurs between alveoli and blood (B).
  • This is followed by transport of gases by the blood to different tissues (E).
  • Then, diffusion of O₂ and CO₂ between blood and tissues takes place (A).
  • Finally, cellular respiration occurs within cells to release energy (D).
  • Thus, the correct order is: C → B → E → A → D
  • Therefore, the correct answer is Option D.
