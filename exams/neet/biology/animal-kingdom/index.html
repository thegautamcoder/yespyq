Q5

A group of researchers procured some fish-like animals and upon investigation the following characters were observed :
A. Endoskeleton was made of cartilage.
B. Ectoparasitic; as they were found attached on fish skin with their circular sucking mouth.
C. Paired fins and scales were absent, but 7 pairs of gill slits were present.
Which of the following species of animals did they consider to fit best with these characters ?

[NEET 2026]

a

Exocoetus sp.

b

Branchiostoma sp.

c

Petromyzon sp.

d

Scoliodon sp.

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

Petromyzon sp.

Explanation

  • The given organism shows primitive jawless vertebrate characters typical of cyclostomes, which represent early-diverging vertebrates.

  • It possesses an endoskeleton made of cartilage, a characteristic feature of early vertebrates lacking true bone.

  • It is ectoparasitic in nature and attaches to fishes using a circular sucking mouth for feeding and blood sucking.

  • It lacks paired fins and scales but bears seven pairs of gill slits for respiration throughout its life cycle.

  • These features are absent in Exocoetus, Branchiostoma, and Scoliodon, which do not show a parasitic sucking mouth condition or jawless state.

  • Hence the organism best fits Petromyzon, a jawless cyclostome fish commonly known as lamprey found attached to marine fishes.

  • Therefore correct identification among given options is Option C which corresponds to lamprey species.

Q9

Given below are two statements : one is labelled as Assertion (A) and other is labelled as Reason (R) .

Assertion (A) : All vertebrates are chordates but all chordates are not vertebrate.

Reason (R) : The members of subphylum vertebrata possess notochord during the embryonic period, the notochord is replaced by a cartilaginous or bony vertebral column in adults.

In the light of the above statements,choose the correct answer from the options given below:

[NEET 2025]

a

A is true but R is false

b

A is false but R is true

c

Both A and R are true and R is correct explanation of A

d

Both A and R are true but R is not correct explanation of A

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

Both A and R are true and R is correct explanation of A

Explanation
  • The Assertion (A) is correct because all vertebrates belong to the phylum Chordata, but not all chordates have a vertebral column.
  • Some chordates like urochordates (e.g., Herdmania) and cephalochordates (e.g., Amphioxus) lack a backbone and are not vertebrates.
  • The Reason (R) is also correct because in vertebrates, the notochord (a defining feature of chordates) appears during the embryonic stage, and later gets replaced by a vertebral column made of bone or cartilage.
  • Thus, the Reason explains why all chordates are not vertebrates, making option C the correct one.

b

c

tracheal tubes

d

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

simple diffusion over their entire body surfaces

Explanation

Sponges (Phylum Porifera) have a very simple body organization and lack specialized respiratory organs. Water continuously flows through their body via pores (ostia) and exits through the osculum.

  • Oxygen (O₂) diffuses directly into cells from the surrounding water
  • Carbon dioxide (CO₂) diffuses out into the water
  • This exchange occurs across the entire body surface

Why other options are incorrect:

  • B. Moist cuticle → seen in organisms like earthworms
  • C. Tracheal tubes → found in insects
  • D. Gills → present in fishes and some aquatic animals

Correct answer: A - simple diffusion over their entire body surfaces

Q17

All living members of the class Cyclostomata are:

a

Symbiotic

b

Ectoparasite

c

Free living

d

Endoparasite

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

Ectoparasite

Explanation

All living members of the class Cyclostomata (like lampreys and hagfishes) are ectoparasites, meaning they live on the outside of other animals (mostly fishes) and feed on their blood and body fluids. They attach to the host using their circular sucking mouth and rasping tongue. They do not kill the host but weaken it by continuously feeding. Cyclostomes have a slimy, scaleless body and lack jaws. Since they feed from the outside of the host’s body, they are not endoparasites (which live inside), nor are they symbiotic or free-living in the true sense.

Q20

Given below are two statements : One is labelled Assertion A and the other is labelled as Reason R

Assertion A: Members of subphylum vertebrata possess notochord during the embryonic period. The notochord is replaced by a cartilaginous or bony vertebral column in the adult.
Reason R: Thus all chordates are vertebrates but not all vertebrates are chordates.

In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below.

A is true but R is false.

Q24

Role of the water vascular system in Echinoderms is :
A. Respiration and Locomotion
B. Excretion and Locomotion
C. Capture and transport of food
D. Digestion and Respiration
E. Digestion and Excretion
Choose the correct answer from the options given below

[NEET 2025]

a

B and C Only

b

B, D and E Only

c

A and B Only

d

A and C Only

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

A and C Only

Explanation

The water vascular system in echinoderms (like starfish and sea urchins) is a network of fluid-filled canals unique to this group. It plays a key role in:

  • Locomotion: Helps the animal move using tube feet, which operate through hydraulic pressure.

It is not directly involved in digestion, excretion, or circulation. Thus, the correct answer is A and C only.

Q26

The flightless bird with forelimbs modified as paddle-like structures suited for swimming is known as :

[NEET 2026]

a

Struthio

b

Psittacula

c

Neophron

d

Aptenodytes

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

Aptenodytes

Explanation

  • Flightless birds show adaptations according to their habitat and mode of life, especially reduced wings or modified forelimbs.

  • Struthio (ostrich) is a flightless bird adapted for running, not swimming, with strong legs and reduced wings.

  • Psittacula (parakeet) is a flying bird with well-developed wings, not flightless.

  • Neophron (Egyptian vulture) is a soaring bird with functional wings, not adapted for swimming or flightlessness.

  • Aptenodytes (penguin) is a flightless bird whose forelimbs are modified into paddle-like flippers for efficient swimming in aquatic environments.

  • These flippers help in propulsion underwater, making penguins excellent swimmers despite being unable to fly.

  • Therefore, the correct answer is Option D: Aptenodytes.
