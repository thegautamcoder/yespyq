Q4 FREE PREVIEW
Identify the vertebrate group of animals characterized by crop and gizzard in its digestive system
aAves
bReptilia
cAmphibia
dOsteichthyes
✓ Correct answer: a) Aves
ExplanationThe correct answer is Option A: Aves.Birds (Aves) have a specialized digestive system that includes: Crop – A pouch-like enlargement of the esophagus used to store and soften food before digestion.Gizzard – A muscular stomach that grinds food, often with the help of ingested stones, since birds lack teeth.These adaptations help birds efficiently process food to meet their high energy requirements for flight.Reptiles and amphibians have simpler digestive systems without both crop and gizzard.Osteichthyes (bony fishes) also lack these structures.Thus, the presence of both crop and gizzard is a defining feature of birds.
Q5 FREE PREVIEW

A group of researchers procured some fish-like animals and upon investigation the following characters were observed :
A. Endoskeleton was made of cartilage.
B. Ectoparasitic; as they were found attached on fish skin with their circular sucking mouth.
C. Paired fins and scales were absent, but 7 pairs of gill slits were present.
Which of the following species of animals did they consider to fit best with these characters ?

[NEET 2026]

a

Exocoetus sp.

b

Branchiostoma sp.

c

Petromyzon sp.

d

Scoliodon sp.

✓ Correct answer: c)

Petromyzon sp.

Explanation

  • The given organism shows primitive jawless vertebrate characters typical of cyclostomes, which represent early-diverging vertebrates.

  • It possesses an endoskeleton made of cartilage, a characteristic feature of early vertebrates lacking true bone.

  • It is ectoparasitic in nature and attaches to fishes using a circular sucking mouth for feeding and blood sucking.

  • It lacks paired fins and scales but bears seven pairs of gill slits for respiration throughout its life cycle.

  • These features are absent in Exocoetus, Branchiostoma, and Scoliodon, which do not show a parasitic sucking mouth condition or jawless state.

  • Hence the organism best fits Petromyzon, a jawless cyclostome fish commonly known as lamprey found attached to marine fishes.

  • Therefore correct identification among given options is Option C which corresponds to lamprey species.
