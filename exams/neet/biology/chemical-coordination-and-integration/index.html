Q1
Fight-or-flight reactions cause activation of;
athe parathyroid glands, leading to increased metabolic rate.
bthe kidney, leading to suppression of reninangiotensin-aldosterone pathway.
cthe adrenal medulla, leading to increased secretion of epinephrine and norepinephrine.
dthe pancreas leading to a reduction in the blood sugar levels.
🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c) the adrenal medulla, leading to increased secretion of epinephrine and norepinephrine.
ExplanationThe "fight-or-flight" response is an acute stress reaction triggered by the sympathetic nervous system in response to perceived danger or emergency.Activation of Adrenal Medulla (C): When the brain (specifically the hypothalamus) perceives a threat, it sends nerve impulses to the adrenal medulla (the inner part of the adrenal glands).This triggers the rapid release of catecholamines—epinephrine (adrenaline) and norepinephrine (noradrenaline)—into the bloodstream.These "emergency hormones" prepare the body for immediate action by increasing heart rate, blood pressure, and breathing rate, and by mobilising energy sources like glucose.A. Parathyroid glands: These glands regulate calcium and phosphate levels in the blood; they are not directly involved in the rapid fight- or-flight metabolic surge.B. Kidney (Renin-angiotensin-aldosterone): While stress can eventually impact kidney function, the fight-or-flight response typically leads to the activation (or "triggering") of the renin-angiotensin-aldosterone system (RAAS) to maintain blood pressure, not its suppression.D. Pancreas: During fight-or-flight, the body needs more sugar for energy.Activation actually leads to an increase in blood sugar levels (via the breakdown of glycogen in the liver), not a reduction.

Q5

Which of the following statements related to pituitary gland are correct?

(a) It is divided anatomically into adenohypophysis and neurohypophysis.

(b) It secretes follicle stimulating hormone.

(c) It secretes melanocyte stimulating hormone.

(d) It does not secrete prolactin.

Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

[Re-NEET 2026]

a

(a) and (b) only

b

(a), (b) and (c) only

c

(c) and (d) only

d

(b) and (c) only

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

(a), (b) and (c) only

Explanation
  • (a) It is divided anatomically into adenohypophysis and neurohypophysis.
    The pituitary gland is anatomically divided into adenohypophysis (anterior pituitary) and neurohypophysis (posterior pituitary).
  • (b) It secretes follicle stimulating hormone (FSH).
    FSH is secreted by the anterior pituitary (adenohypophysis) and regulates gamete formation.
  • (c) It secretes melanocyte stimulating hormone (MSH).
    The pars intermedia of the adenohypophysis secretes melanocyte-stimulating hormone (MSH).
  • (d) It does not secrete prolactin.
    This statement is incorrect because prolactin (PRL) is secreted by the anterior pituitary gland and stimulates milk production after childbirth.

Therefore, statements (a), (b), and (c) are correct, while (d) is incorrect.

Q9

Consider the following statements regarding function of adrenal medullary hormones :
A. It causes pupilary constriction
B. It is a hyperglycemic hormone
C. It causes piloerection
D. It increases strength of heart contraction
Choose the correct answer from the options given below

[NEET 2025]

a

A, C and D Only

b

D Only

c

C and D Only

d

B, C and D Only

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

B, C and D Only

Explanation

The adrenal medulla secretes two main hormones — adrenaline (epinephrine) and noradrenaline (norepinephrine) — which are part of the fight or flight response.

  • B. Hyperglycemic hormone : These hormones increase blood sugar levels by stimulating glycogen breakdown, so they are hyperglycemic.

  • C. Causes piloerection : They cause piloerection (hair standing on end) as part of a stress response.

  • D. Increases heart strength : Adrenaline increases the strength and rate of heart contractions.

  • A. Pupillary constriction : These hormones cause pupil dilation (not constriction) to improve vision in danger.

Hence, the correct option is D: B, C and D only.

Q11

Which of the following hormones released from the pituitary is actually synthesized in the hypothalamus?

[NEET 2025]

a

Follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH)

b

Adenocorticotrophic hormone (ACTH)

c

Luteinizing hormone (LH)

d

Anti-diuretic hormone (ADH)

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

Anti-diuretic hormone (ADH)

Explanation

Anti-diuretic hormone (ADH), also called vasopressin, is released by the posterior pituitary, but it is actually synthesized in the hypothalamus — specifically in the supraoptic and paraventricular nuclei. After synthesis, ADH is transported to the posterior pituitary through nerve fibers and released into the blood. ADH helps in water reabsorption in the kidneys, reducing water loss in urine.

On the other hand, hormones like FSH, LH, and ACTH are synthesized and secreted by the anterior pituitary, not the hypothalamus.

So, the correct answer is Option D: ADH.

Q12

Why can't insulin be given orally to diabetic patients?

[NEET 2025]

a

Because of structural variation

b

Its bioavailability will be increased

c

Human body will elicit strong immune response

d

It will be digested in Gastro-Intestinal (GI) tract

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

It will be digested in Gastro-Intestinal (GI) tract

Explanation

Insulin is a protein hormone, and if it is taken orally, it will pass through the digestive system. In the stomach and intestines, there are digestive enzymes that break down proteins into amino acids. This means the insulin would be broken down before it can enter the bloodstream, making it ineffective.

That’s why insulin is usually given through injections, which allow it to go directly into the bloodstream and work properly to control blood sugar levels.

So, the correct answer is D — It will be digested in the GI tract.

Q14

Given below are two statements : one is labelled as Assertion A and the other is labelled as Reason R :
Assertion A : FSH acts upon ovarian follicles in female and Leydig cells in male.
Reason R : Growing ovarian follicles secrete estrogen in female while interstitial cells secrete androgen in male human being.
In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below :

[NEET 2024]

a

A is false but R is true

b

Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A.

c

Both A and R are true but R is NOT the correct explanation of A.

d

A is true but R is false

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

A is false but R is true

Explanation

FSH (Follicle Stimulating Hormone) acts on ovarian follicles in females to stimulate follicular growth and estrogen secretion.

However, in males, FSH does not act on Leydig cells. It acts on Sertoli cells in the seminiferous tubules to support spermatogenesis.

Leydig cells are acted upon by LH (Luteinizing Hormone), not FSH.

Q17

Which one of the following statements is incorrect?

[Re-NEET 2026]

a

α-cells of pancreas secrete glucagon

b

α-cells of pancreas secrete insulin

c

Glucagon stimulates glycogenolysis

d

β-cells of pancreas secrete insulin

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

α-cells of pancreas secrete insulin

Explanation

The endocrine part of the pancreas consists of Islets of Langerhans, containing two main primary types of cells: α-cells secrete glucagon (a hyperglycemic hormone). Thus statement (A) is correct, and statement (C) is correct because glucagon acts on hepatocytes to stimulate glycogenolysis. β-cells secrete insulin (a hypoglycemic hormone). Thus statement (D) is correct. Statement (B) states that α-cells secrete insulin, which is entirely false/incorrect. Hence, option (B) is the correct answer.
