Q26

Identify the correct sequence of steps in each cycle of Polymerase Chain Reaction :

[NEET 2026]

a

Denaturation → Annealing → Extension

b

Denaturation → Extension → Annealing

c

Extension → Annealing → Denaturation

d

Annealing → Denaturation → Extension

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

Denaturation → Annealing → Extension

Explanation

Each cycle of Polymerase Chain Reaction (PCR) involves three main steps carried out in a specific order.

  • First, denaturation occurs, where the double-stranded DNA is heated to separate into single strands.
  • Next, annealing takes place, where primers bind (hybridise) to the complementary sequences on the DNA strands.
  • Finally, extension occurs, where DNA polymerase synthesizes new DNA strands by extending the primers.

Thus, the correct sequence is - Denaturation → Annealing → Extension

Final answer: Option A

Q28

Which of the following statements are not true regarding restriction endonucleases?
A. They are called molecular scissors.
B. These are the enzymes responsible for restricting the growth of bacteriophages in E. coli.
C. They cut the DNA only at the centre of the palindromic sites.
D. They remove nucleotides only from the ends of DNA fragments.
E. They recognise specific palindromic base-pair sequences.
Choose the answer from the options given below :

[NEET 2026]

a

A and B only

b

B and E only

c

C and D only

d

A and E only

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

C and D only

Explanation
  • A. They are called molecular scissors → True
    Restriction endonucleases cleave DNA at specific recognition sites, so they are commonly referred to as molecular scissors in genetic engineering.
  • B. Responsible for restricting bacteriophage growth in E. coli → True
    These enzymes are part of the bacterial defense system. They cut foreign DNA (like bacteriophage DNA), thereby “restricting” its growth.
  • C. They cut DNA only at the centre of palindromic sites → False
    Restriction enzymes recognize palindromic sequences, but they do not always cut exactly at the center. Many produce staggered (sticky) ends, while some produce blunt ends.
  • D. They remove nucleotides only from ends of DNA fragments → False
    This is a property of exonucleases. Restriction endonucleases cut within the DNA strand at specific internal sites, not from the ends.
  • E. They recognise specific palindromic base-pair sequences → True
    Each restriction enzyme has a specific recognition sequence, usually a palindrome (same sequence when read 5'→3' on both strands).

Final Answer: C and D only (Option C)

✓ Correct answer: a)

Polymerase chain reaction (PCR) amplifies DNA following the equation

✓ Correct answer: d)

2n

Explanation

The Polymerase Chain Reaction (PCR) is a technique used to amplify a specific segment of DNA. It works by repeating cycles of denaturation, annealing, and extension. After each cycle, the number of DNA copies doubles.

Q39

Recombinant DNA molecule can be created normally by cutting the vector DNA and target DNA respectively with:

[Re-NEET 2024]

a

Hind II, Hind II

b

Hind II, Alu I

c

Hind II, EcoR I

d

Hind II, Bam H

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

Hind II, Hind II

Explanation

Recombinant DNA technology involves joining DNA fragments from different sources. To create a recombinant DNA molecule, both the vector DNA (the carrier) and the target DNA (the desired gene) must be cut with the same restriction enzyme, so that their "sticky ends" can complementarily pair and be joined together by DNA ligase. If you use different restriction enzymes, the ends won't match and the DNA fragments cannot be ligated together.
