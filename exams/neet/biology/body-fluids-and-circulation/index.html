Q7

The WBC count of a person's blood sample is 8000/cu.mm. How many eosinophils and lymphocytes would be in the same blood sample approximately?

[NEET 2026]

a

160-240/cu.mm and 1600-2000/cu.mm, respectively

b

100-120/cu.mm and 160-200/cu.mm, respectively

c

300-500/cu.mm and 500-700/cu.mm, respectively

d

300-500/cu.mm and 1200-1500/cu.mm, respectively

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

160-240/cu.mm and 1600-2000/cu.mm, respectively

Explanation

The normal total WBC count in human blood is about 6000–8000 cells/cu.mm. The differential count shows the approximate percentage of different types of WBCs:

  • Eosinophils: 2–3%
  • Lymphocytes: 20–25%

For a total WBC count of 8000/cu.mm:

  • Eosinophils = 2–3% of 8000 = 160–240/cu.mm
  • Lymphocytes = 20–25% of 8000 = 1600–2000/cu.mm

Option A gives values of 160–240/cu.mm (eosinophils) and 1600–2000/cu.mm (lymphocytes), which match the calculated ranges.

Therefore, the correct answer is Option A: 160–240/cu.mm and 1600–2000/cu.mm, respectively.

Q8

The mother has  A+ blood group, the father has B+ and the child is  A+. What can be the possible genotypes of all three, respectively?
A. IAIAIBiIBi

B. IAIA |IBi |IAi
C. IBi |IAIA |IAIB
D.  IAIA∣IBIB∣IAi
E.  I Ai I B i I Ai

Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

[Re-NEET 2024]

a

C and D

b

D and A

c

A and B

d

B and E

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

B and E

Explanation

The child’s blood group is A⁺, so genotype must be Iᴬi (receiving Iᴬ from one parent and i from the other).

Hence, both these combinations are possible:

  • Option B: Mother IᴬIᴬ, Father Iᴮi, Child Iᴬi

  • Option E: Mother Iᴬi, Father Iᴮi, Child Iᴬi

Therefore, correct answer = D (B and E)

Q9

Following are the stages of pathway for conduction of an action potential through the heart:
A. AV bundle
B. Purkinje fibres
C. AV node
D. Bundle branches
E. SA node

Choose the correct sequence of pathway from the options given below :

[NEET 2024]

a

E-A-D-B-C

b

E-C-A-D-B

c

A-E-C-B-D

d

B-D-E-C-A

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

E-C-A-D-B

Explanation

The correct sequence for the conduction of an action potential through the heart is E-C-A-D-B, i.e., SA node → AV node → AV bundle → Bundle branches → Purkinje fibres.

The SA node, located in the right atrium, initiates the impulse, which spreads to the AV node.

From the AV node, the impulse travels to the AV bundle (Bundle of His), then down the right and left bundle branches, and finally reaches the Purkinje fibres, which distribute the impulse to the ventricular muscles, causing contraction.

Q13

'Lub' sound of Heart is caused by the ______

[Re-NEET 2024]

a

Closure of the semilunar valves

b

Opening of tricuspid and bicuspid valves

c

Opening of the semilunar valves

d

Closure of the tricuspid and bicuspid valves

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

Closure of the tricuspid and bicuspid valves

Explanation

The “lub” sound of the heart, also known as the first heart sound, is produced by the closure of the atrioventricular (AV) valves—namely the tricuspid and bicuspid (mitral) valves—at the beginning of ventricular systole. This prevents the backflow of blood into the atria when the ventricles contract. It is followed by the “dub” sound, which results from the closure of the semilunar valves.

Q19

Cardiac activities of the heart are regulated by:
A. Nodal tissue
B. A special neural centre in the medulla oblongata
C. Adrenal medullary hormones
D. Adrenal cortical hormones
Choose the correct answer given below:

[NEET 2025]

a

A, C and D Only

b

A, В and D Only

c

A, B and C Only

d

A, B, C and D

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

A, B and C Only

Explanation

The cardiac activities of the heart are regulated by:

  • A. Nodal tissue: Includes the SA node (pacemaker) and AV node, which generate and conduct electrical impulses to regulate heartbeat.

  • B. A special neural centre in the medulla oblongata: This brain region controls heart rate through the autonomic nervous system (sympathetic increases, parasympathetic decreases heart rate).

  • C. Adrenal medullary hormones: Hormones like adrenaline and noradrenaline increase heart rate and force of contraction.

  • D. Adrenal cortical hormones are mainly involved in metabolism and electrolyte balance, not directly in heart rate regulation.

So, the correct answer is C — A, B, and C only.

Q20

Which of the following statements is incorrect ?

[Re-NEET 2026]

a

Blood coagulates in response to an injury

b

Blood clot consists of fibrins

c

Fibrin is produced from fibrinogen

d

Fibrinogen is produced from fibrin

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

Fibrinogen is produced from fibrin

Explanation

Evaluating cascading statements for coagulation of blood:

(A) Blood coagulates in response to an injury: Correct, it prevents excessive loss of blood.

(B) Blood clot consists of fibrins: Correct, clots are formed mainly of a network of threads called fibrins.

(C) Fibrin is produced from fibrinogen: Correct, inactive fibrinogen is converted into active fibrins by the enzyme thrombin.

(D) Fibrinogen is produced from fibrin: Incorrect, it is the reverse. Fibrinogen is synthesized in the liver and floats in plasma, which is converted to fibrin during clotting.

Therefore, statement (D) is incorrect.

List I

List II

I.

QRS complex

[NEET 2024]

a

A-IV, B-II, C-I, D-III

b

A-I, B-III, C-IV, D-II

c

A-III, B-II, C-IV, D-I

✓ Correct answer: c)

A-III, B-II, C-IV, D-I

  1. P wave represents depolarisation of atria (Atria contract due to electrical excitation).
