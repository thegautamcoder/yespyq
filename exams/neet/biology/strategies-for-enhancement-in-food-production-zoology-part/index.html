Q1 FREE PREVIEW

Which one of the following is not a step in Multiple Ovulation Embryo Transfer Technology (MOET)?

[NEET 2021]

a

Cow is administered hormone having LH like activity for super ovulation

b

Cow yields about 6-8 eggs at a time

c

Cow fertilized by artificial insemination

d

Fertilized eggs are transferred to surrogate mothers at 8-32 call stage

✓ Correct answer: a)

Cow is administered hormone having LH like activity for super ovulation

Explanation

MOET involves administering hormones with FSH-like activity, not LH-like activity, to induce superovulation in cows. This leads to the production of multiple eggs, which are then fertilized and transferred to surrogate mothers.
