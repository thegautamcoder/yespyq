Q7

Which one of the following is an appropriate example of 'sexual deceit' ?

[NEET 2026]

a

Female wasp and fig

b

Cuckoo and crow

c

Ophrys and bumblebee

d

Sea anemone and clown fish

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

Ophrys and bumblebee

Explanation

  • Sexual deceit is a pollination strategy in which flowers mimic the appearance, odour, or signals of female insects to attract male pollinators without offering any reward.

  • Ophrys orchid is a classic example where the flower closely resembles a female bumblebee in shape, colour, texture and releases similar pheromone-like chemicals.

  • Male bumblebee is deceived by this mimicry and attempts to mate with the flower through pseudocopulation, mistaking it for a real female bee.

  • During this false mating attempt, the pollen sacs get attached to the body of the bee and are carried to another flower, ensuring pollination.

  • Therefore, option C (Ophrys and bumblebee) correctly represents sexual deceit in pollination.

Q8

The equation of Verhulst-Pearl logistic growth is dNdt=rNK-NK.  From this equation, K indicates:

[NEET 2024]

a

Population density

b

Intrinsic rate of natural increase

c

Biotic potential

d

Carrying capacity

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

Carrying capacity

Explanation

The given equation represents the logistic growth model (Verhulst-Pearl logistic growth equation):

dNdt=rN(K−NK)\frac{dN}{dt} = rN \left( \frac{K - N}{K} \right)

where:

  • NN = Population size
  • rr = Intrinsic rate of natural increase
  • KK = Carrying capacity of the environment

KK in this equation represents the carrying capacity, which is the maximum number of individuals an environment can sustain indefinitely based on available resources such as food, water, and shelter.

Thus, the correct answer is D. Carrying capacity.

Population interactions describe how two different species affect each other’s survival and reproduction.

Q11

Epiphytes that are growing on a mango branch is an example of which of the following?

a

Predation

b

Amensalism

c

Commensalism

d

Mutualism

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

Commensalism

Explanation

Epiphytes are plants like orchids that grow on the branches of trees such as mango without harming them. They use the tree only for support to get better access to sunlight and air. They do not take nutrients from the tree and do not harm it in any way. This type of relationship is called commensalism, where one organism benefits (the epiphyte) and the other is neither helped nor harmed (the mango tree).

  • Predation involves killing.

  • Amensalism harms one, the other is unaffected.

  • Mutualism benefits both.

So, commensalism is the correct answer.

Q12

When will the population density increase, under special conditions?

[Re-NEET 2024]

a

Deaths exceeds number of births and also number of emigrants equals number of immigrants.

b

Births plus number of immigrants equals number of deaths plus number of emigrants.

c

Births plus number of emigrants is more than the number of deaths plus number of immigrants.

d

Births plus number of immigrants is more than the sum of number of deaths and number of emigrants.

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

Births plus number of immigrants is more than the sum of number of deaths and number of emigrants.

Explanation

The correct answer is option D

Explaination

Population density increases when the overall population of an area grows. This growth occurs when the number of people entering the area (through births and immigration) is greater than the number of people leaving (through deaths and emigration).

  • Births add to the population.
  • Immigration brings people into the area, also increasing the population.
  • Deaths reduce the population.
  • Emigration is when people leave the area, which also reduces the population.

To increase population density, the combined effect of births and immigration (which add to the population) must be greater than the combined effect of deaths and emigration (which decrease the population). Thus, the population density will increase under the condition where births plus immigrants exceed deaths plus emigrants.

Option D: Births plus number of immigrants is more than the sum of number of deaths and number of emigrants correctly describes this situation.

Q17

Which one of the following equations represents the Verhulst-Pearl Logistic Growth of population?

a

dNdt=rNN-KN

b

dNdt=Nr-KK

c

dNdt=r(K-NK)

d

dNdt=rN(K-NK)

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

dNdt=rN(K-NK)

Explanation

The Verhulst-Pearl Logistic Growth Equation describes population growth when resources are limited. Unlike exponential growth, it accounts for carrying capacity (K) — the maximum population size an environment can support.

The correct equation is:

dNdt=rN(K−NK)\frac{dN}{dt} = rN \left(\frac{K - N}{K}\right)

Where:

  • NN = population size

  • rr = intrinsic growth rate

  • KK = carrying capacity

  • dNdt\frac{dN}{dt}​ = rate of population growth

As NN approaches KK, growth slows down and eventually stops. This is known as logistic or S-shaped growth.

So, Option D is correct.

Q19

Statement-I: Fig fruit is a non-vegetarian fruit as it has enclosed fig wasps in it.
Statement-II: Fig wasp and fig tree exhibit mutual relationship as fig wasp completes its life cycle in fig fruit and fig fruit gets pollinated by fig wasp.
In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below:

[NEET 2025]

a

Statement-I is correct but Statement-II is incorrect

b

Statement-I is incorrect but Statement-II is correct

c

Both Statement-I and Statement-II are correct.

d

Both Statement-I and Statement-II are incorrect

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

Both Statement-I and Statement-II are incorrect

Explanation

  • Statement I: "Fig fruit is a non-vegetarian fruit as it has enclosed fig wasps in it" — Incorrect
    This is a misconception. While it's true that fig wasps enter the fig fruit to pollinate, not all fig fruits contain wasps. Moreover, the fig often digests the wasp's body with enzymes, so no visible remains are present. Labeling fig as “non-vegetarian” is unscientific and incorrect, especially because it does not contain animal flesh or live insects in edible form.

  • Statement II: "Fig wasp and fig tree exhibit mutual relationship..." — Incorrect
    This is not true for all fig species. While some fig trees and fig wasps do share mutualism, it is not universal. Some figs are parthenocarpic (develop without fertilization or pollination), and some fig species do not depend on wasps at all for pollination.

Hence, Both Statement-I and Statement-II are incorrect, making Option D the correct answer.
