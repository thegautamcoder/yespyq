Q3

Consider the following:
A. The reductive division for the human female gametogenesis starts earlier than that of the male gametogenesis.
B. The gap between the first meiotic division and the second meiotic division is much shorter for males compared to females.
C. The first polar body is associated with the formation of the primary oocyte.
D. Luteinizing Hormone (LH) surge leads to disintegration of the endometrium and onset of menstrual bleeding.
Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

[NEET 2025]

a

B and D are true

b

B and C are true

c

A and B are true

d

A and C are true

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

A and B are true

Explanation

  • A. True — In females, meiosis begins during fetal development, long before birth. In contrast, spermatogenesis (male gametogenesis) starts at puberty.

  • B. True — In males, meiosis I and II occur continuously and rapidly during sperm formation. In females, the first meiotic division pauses at prophase I until puberty, and the second division occurs only after fertilization, causing a long gap.

  • C. False — The first polar body is formed after the primary oocyte completes meiosis I, not during its formation.

  • D. False — The LH surge triggers ovulation, not menstrual bleeding. Menstruation occurs if fertilization does not happen.

Thus, statements A and B are correct, so the answer is Option C.

Given below are two statements : One is labelled as Assertion A and the other is labelled as Reason R.
Assertion A: During menstrual cycle, the ovulation takes place approximately on 14th  day.
Reason R : Rapid secretion of LH in the middle of menstrual cycle induces rupture of Graafian follicle and thereby the release of ovum.
In the light of the above statements, choose the most appropriate answer from the options given below.

[Re-NEET 2024]

a

A is correct but R is not correct.

b

A is not correct but R is correct.

Both A and R are correct and R is the correct explanation of A.

✓ Correct answer: c)

Q8

Match List I with List II related to embryonic development at various months of pregnancy :

List I List II
A. The foetus movement starts and hair appears on the head I. 24 weeks of pregnancy
B. The foetus develops limbs and digits II. 20 weeks of pregnancy
C. The foetus develops external genital organs III. 8 weeks of pregnancy
D. The foetus body is covered with fine hair; eyelids separate and eyelashes are formed IV. 12 weeks of pregnancy
Choose the correct answer from the options given below : [NEET 2026]
a

A-III, B-II, C-IV, D-I

b

A-II, B-IV, C-III, D-I

c

A-IV, B-II, C-III, D-I

d

A-II, B-III, C-IV, D-I

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

A-II, B-III, C-IV, D-I

Explanation

  • Human embryonic and foetal development proceeds in a highly coordinated chronological sequence with distinct morphological milestones.

  • At 8 weeks of pregnancy, the embryo is now termed a foetus; major organ systems are formed and limbs and digits develop, marking early structural differentiation.

  • At 12 weeks, external genital organs begin to differentiate, allowing early sex determination in development.

  • At 20 weeks, the mother can first feel foetal movements and hair starts appearing on the head, indicating active growth.

  • At 24 weeks, significant maturation occurs; the body is covered with fine hair (lanugo), and eyelids separate with eyelashes formation, showing advanced sensory and protective development.

  • These stages reflect progressive organ maturation and functional preparation for birth.

  • Hence, the correct matching is A-II, B-III, C-IV, D-I, corresponding to Option D.

Q9

Twins are born to a family that lives next door to you. The twins are a boy and a girl. Which of the following must be true?

[NEET 2025]

a

They were conceived through in vitro fertilization.

b

They are monozygotic twins.

c

They have 75% identical genetic content.

d

They are fraternal twins.

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

They are fraternal twins.

Explanation

When a boy and a girl are born as twins, they must be fraternal twins (also called dizygotic twins). This is because identical (monozygotic) twins come from the same fertilized egg and always have the same genetic material, meaning they are always the same sex.

Fraternal twins develop from two separate eggs fertilized by two different sperm. They can be of different sexes and are genetically like regular siblings, sharing about 50% of their DNA.

So, if the twins are a boy and a girl, they cannot be identical, and the correct answer is D — fraternal twins.

✓ Correct answer: d)

Menarche

Explanation

The first menstruation in a girl's life is called menarche. It usually happens between the ages of 10 and 14 and marks the beginning of her reproductive years.   • Ovulation is the release of an egg from the ovary.

  • Menopause is when menstruation permanently stops in older women.

Q16

Given below are two statements:

Statement I: Ovulation is caused by LH surge leading to rupture of Graafian follicles.

Statement II: Graafian follicle remaining after ovulation transform into corpus luteum and secretes large amount of estrogen.

In the light of the above statements, choose the most appropriate answer from the options given below:

[Re-NEET 2026]

a

Both Statement I and Statement II are correct

b

Both Statement I and Statement II are incorrect

c

Statement I is correct but Statement II is incorrect

d

Statement I is incorrect but Statement II is correct

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

Statement I is correct but Statement II is incorrect

Explanation

Ovulation is precisely triggered by a rapid mid-cycle surge in Luteinizing Hormone (LH), which reliably induces the rupture of the mature Graafian follicle, making Statement I completely accurate. Following this crucial biological event, the ruptured follicular remnants successfully transform into a temporary endocrine structure strictly known as the corpus luteum. However, Statement II is factually incorrect because the primary physiological function of the newly formed corpus luteum is to secrete large quantities of progesterone, not estrogen. Progesterone is absolutely essential for the continuous maintenance and stabilization of the endometrium to facilitate potential embryonic implantation.

Q17

Spermatogonia undergo a series of cell divisions to produce sperms. Select the correct statements from the following :
A. Spermatogonia always undergo meiotic cell division.
B. Primary spermatocytes divide mitotically to produce secondary-spermatocytes.
C. Secondary spermatocytes, through their second meiotic division, produce haploid spermatids.
D. Spermatids produce spermatozoa through mitosis.
E. Spermatids transform into spermatozoa by spermiogenesis.
Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

[NEET 2026]

a

C and E only

b

A, C and E only

c

B, C and D only

d

A and E only

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

C and E only

Explanation

  • Spermatogenesis is a process in which diploid spermatogonia give rise to haploid spermatozoa through mitotic and meiotic divisions followed by differentiation.

  • Statement A is incorrect because spermatogonia do not directly undergo meiosis; they first multiply by mitosis to maintain their population.

  • Statement B is incorrect as primary spermatocytes do not divide mitotically; instead, they undergo meiosis I to form secondary spermatocytes.

  • Statement C is correct because each secondary spermatocyte undergoes meiosis II to produce haploid spermatids.

  • Statement D is incorrect since spermatids do not divide mitotically; they are non-dividing cells.

  • Statement E is correct as spermatids transform into mature spermatozoa through spermiogenesis, involving structural and functional differentiation.

  • Therefore, only statements C and E are correct, making Option A the correct answer.
