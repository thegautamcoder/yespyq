Q1 FREE PREVIEW

In which one of the following, the ovules are not enclosed by an ovary wall and remain exposed?

[NEET 2026]

a

Selaginella

b

Funaria

c

Pinus

d

Wolffia

✓ Correct answer: c)

Pinus

Explanation

The condition where ovules are not enclosed by an ovary wall (i.e., exposed ovules) is a characteristic feature of gymnosperms.

A. Selaginella – a pteridophyte; does not have seeds or ovules
B. Funaria – a bryophyte; no ovules
C. Pinus – a gymnosperm; ovules are naked (not enclosed by ovary)
D. Wolffia – an angiosperm; ovules enclosed within ovary

Correct answer: Option C

Q2 FREE PREVIEW

Read the following statements and choose the set of correct statements: In the members of Phaeophyceae,
A. Asexual reproduction occurs usually by biflagellate zoospores.
B. Sexual reproduction is by oogamous method only.
C. Stored food is in the form of carbohydrates which is either mannitol or laminarin.
D. The major pigments found are chlorophyll a, c and carotenoids and xanthophyll.
E. Vegetative cells have a cellulosic wall, usually covered on the outside by gelatinous coating of algin.
Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

[NEET 2024]

a

A, B, C and E only

b

A, B, C and D only

c

B, C, D and E only

d

A, C, D and E only

✓ Correct answer: d)

A, C, D and E only

Explanation
  • A. Asexual reproduction in Phaeophyceae occurs usually by biflagellate zoospores.
  • C. Stored food in Phaeophyceae is in the form of complex carbohydrates, either as mannitol or laminarin.
  • D. Major pigments found are chlorophyll a, c, carotenoids, and xanthophyll.
  • E. Vegetative cells have a cellulosic wall, usually covered on the outside by a gelatinous coating of algin.

Statement B is incorrect because sexual reproduction in Phaeophyceae can be isogamous, anisogamous, or oogamous, not exclusively oogamous.

Q3 FREE PREVIEW

Which of the following is the correct match?

[Re-NEET 2024]

a

Gymnosperms: Cedrus, Pinus, Sequoia

b

Angiosperms : Wolffia, Eucalyptus, Sequoia

c

Bryophytes : Polytrichum, Polysiphonia, Sphagnum

d

Pteridophytes : Equisetum, Ginkgo, Adiantum

✓ Correct answer: a)

Gymnosperms: Cedrus, Pinus, Sequoia

Explanation

A. Gymnosperms: Cedrus, Pinus, Sequoia
→ All three (Cedrus, Pinus, Sequoia) are gymnosperms (conifers).

B. Angiosperms: Wolffia, Eucalyptus, Sequoia
→ Sequoia is a gymnosperm, not an angiosperm.

C. Bryophytes: Polytrichum, Polysiphonia, Sphagnum
→ Polysiphonia is an alga (Rhodophyceae), not a bryophyte.

D. Pteridophytes: Equisetum, Ginkgo, Adiantum
→ Ginkgo is a gymnosperm, not a pteridophyte.

Correct answer: A — Gymnosperms: Cedrus, Pinus, Sequoia
