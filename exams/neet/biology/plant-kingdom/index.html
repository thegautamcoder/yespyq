Q4

In bryophytes, the gemmae help in which one of the following?

[NEET 2025]

a

Nutrient absorption

b

Gaseous exchange

c

Sexual reproduction

d

Asexual reproduction

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

Asexual reproduction

Explanation

In bryophytes like Marchantia, gemmae are small, green, disc-like structures involved in asexual reproduction. They are produced inside cup-like structures called gemma cups, usually found on the surface of the thallus (plant body). When water (like rain) splashes into the gemma cups, gemmae are dispersed to nearby places. If they land in a suitable environment, each gemma can grow into a new plant that is genetically identical to the parent. Gemmae do not help in nutrient absorption, gaseous exchange, or sexual reproduction. Hence, they play a key role in vegetative (asexual) reproduction in bryophytes.

Q5

The correct sequence of events in the life cycle of bryophytes is
A. Fusion of antherozoid with egg.
B. Attachment of gametophyte to substratum.
C. Reduction division to produce haploid spores.
D. Formation of sporophyte.
E. Release of antherozoids into water.
Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

[NEET 2025]

a

B, E, A, D, C

b

D, E, A, B, C

c

D, E, A, C, B

d

B, E, A, C, D

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

B, E, A, D, C

Explanation

The correct sequence in the life cycle of bryophytes (like mosses and liverworts), which follows an alternation of generations, is:

  1. B — Attachment of gametophyte to substratum
    → The gametophyte (haploid) grows and attaches to the soil or rock.

  2. E — Release of antherozoids into water
    → Male gametes (antherozoids) are released and need water to swim to the female part.

  3. A — Fusion of antherozoid with egg
    → Fertilization occurs in the archegonium, forming a diploid zygote.

  4. D — Formation of sporophyte
    → The zygote develops into a sporophyte (diploid), which remains attached to the gametophyte.

  5. C — Reduction division to produce haploid spores
    → The sporophyte undergoes meiosis to form haploid spores for a new generation.

So, the correct order is B, E, A, D, C → Option A.

Q6

Given below are the stages in the life cycle of pteridophytes. Arrange the following stages in the correct sequence.
A. Prothallus stage
B. Meiosis in spore mother cells
C. Fertilisation
D. Formation of archegonia and antheridia in gametophyte.
E. Transfer of antherozoids to the archegonia in presence of water.
Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

[NEET 2025]

a

D, E, C, A, B

b

E, D, C, B, A

c

B, A, D, E, C

d

B, A, E, C, D

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

B, A, D, E, C

Explanation

The life cycle of pteridophytes shows alternation of generations — a diploid sporophyte and a haploid gametophyte stage. The correct sequence is:

  1. B. Meiosis in spore mother cells – The diploid sporophyte undergoes meiosis to produce haploid spores.

  2. A. Prothallus stage – Spores germinate to form the gametophyte, called the prothallus.

  3. D. Formation of archegonia and antheridia – The gametophyte produces male (antheridia) and female (archegonia) sex organs.

  4. E. Transfer of antherozoids to archegonia in presence of water – Sperm (antherozoids) swim to the egg using water.

  5. C. Fertilisation – Sperm fertilizes the egg, forming a zygote that grows into a new sporophyte.

Thus, the correct order is C: B, A, D, E, C.

Q7

Sphenopsida class belongs to _______.

[Re-NEET 2026]

a

bryophytes

b

angiosperms

c

gymnosperms

d

pteridophytes

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

pteridophytes

Explanation

Pteridophytes are classified into four distinct classes :

1. Psilopsida (e.g., Psilotum)

2. Lycopsida (e.g., Selaginella, Lycopodium)

3. Sphenopsida (e.g., Equisetum)

4. Pteropsida (e.g., Dryopteris, Pteris, Adiantum).

Thus, Sphenopsida belongs to pteridophytes, making option (D) the correct answer.

Q9

Given below are two statements:

Statement I: In gymnosperms, the male and female gametophytes remain within the sporangia.

Statement II: In gymnosperms, seeds are not covered.

In the light of the above statements, choose the most appropriate answer from the options given below:

[Re-NEET 2026]

a

Both Statement I and Statement II are correct

b

Both Statement I and Statement II are incorrect

c

Statement I is correct but Statement II is incorrect

d

Statement I is incorrect but Statement II is correct

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

Both Statement I and Statement II are correct

Explanation

Both statements are completely accurate: Statement I is correct: Unlike bryophytes and pteridophytes, in gymnosperms, the male and female gametophytes do not have an independent free-living existence. They remain retained within the sporangia retained on the sporophytes. Statement II is correct: The word 'gymnosperm' literally means 'naked seeds' (gymnos = naked, sperma = seed). The ovules are not enclosed by any ovary wall and remain exposed both before and after fertilization. Thus, option (A) is the correct choice.

Q11

Which one of the following is the characteristic feature of gymnosperms?

[NEET 2025]

a

Seeds are absent.

b

Gymnosperms have flowers for reproduction.

c

Seeds are enclosed in fruits.

d

Seeds are naked.

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

Seeds are naked.

Explanation

A key feature of gymnosperms is that their seeds are naked, meaning the seeds are not enclosed within fruits. Unlike angiosperms (flowering plants), gymnosperms do not form flowers or fruits. Instead, they produce cones (like in pine trees), and the seeds develop on the surface of cone scales, exposed to the environment.

Option A is incorrect because seeds are present.
Option B is incorrect because gymnosperms do not have flowers.
Option C is incorrect because their seeds are not enclosed in fruits.

So, the correct and defining feature is that gymnosperm seeds are naked.
