Accelerated rates of loss of ice due to climate change in the case of oceans and frozen areas onland particularly in Greenland, the Arctic and the Antarctic will not produce?
aIncrease in tropical cyclone winds
bRainfall and extreme waves
cGreenhouse gas emissions
dRise in sea level
✓ Correct answer: c) Greenhouse gas emissions
ExplanationThe correct answer is: C. Greenhouse gas emissions Explanation: The passage mentions that accelerated rates of ice loss in areas like Greenland, the Arctic, and the Antarctic will lead to: Rise in sea levels Increase in tropical cyclone winds Rainfall and extreme waves
