Q1 FREE PREVIEW
Give one word for the following expression: One who does things for pleasure and not for money.
aMagician
bAmateur
cEmployee
dClown
✓ Correct answer: b) Amateur
ExplanationThe best option for the expression "One who does things for pleasure and not for money" is: B. Amateur Amateur is the most precise term for someone who engages in an activity for enjoyment, not financial gain.
Q2 FREE PREVIEW
In the following question, out of the four alternatives, select the one which is SIMILAR in meaning to the given word. Prerogative
aPrivilege
bClaim
cGrant
dAfford
✓ Correct answer: a) Privilege
ExplanationThe most similar meaning to "prerogative" is A. Privilege.Prerogative refers to a special right or advantage that someone has because of their position or status.Privilege also signifies a special right, benefit, or advantage that someone enjoys.Claim implies asserting a right or title.Grant means to bestow a right or privilege.Afford refers to having the resources to do or buy something.
Q3 FREE PREVIEW
Give one word for the following expression: The expression of spoken sounds by an alphabet.
aAlphabet
bAlphabetic
cAlphabeticiation
dAlphabetism
✓ Correct answer: d) Alphabetism
ExplanationThe best option for the expression "The expression of spoken sounds by an alphabet" is: Alphabetism: This word specifically refers to the ability to understand and use a writing system that represents spoken language with letters.It's the most precise term for the given expression.Therefore, "Alphabetism" is the most suitable one-word answer.
Q4 FREE PREVIEW
Choose the correct synonym of the word given below. Garner
aCollect
bDispel
cDisperse
dDissipate
✓ Correct answer: a) Collect
ExplanationThe correct synonym for "garner" is A. Collect.Garner means to acquire or accumulate something, often through effort.Collect also means to gather or bring things together.Dispel, Disperse, and Dissipate all have the opposite meaning of gathering things together.They signify scattering or driving something away.
Q5 FREE PREVIEW
Choose the word OPPOSITE in meaning to the given word. Erratic
aAberration
bNormal
cArcade
dSarcastic
✓ Correct answer: b) Normal
ExplanationThe word opposite in meaning to "erratic" is B. Normal.Erratic means unpredictable, irregular, or inconsistent.Normal signifies something that is typical, usual, or expected.It's the opposite of being unpredictable.Aberration can be synonymous with erratic.Arcade is a roofed passageway with shops.Sarcastic describes using mocking or cutting remarks.
Q6 FREE PREVIEW
Select the most appropriate meaning of the given idiom. Walking on thin ice
awalking carefully
bperforming a ballet
cslipping on ice
ddoing something risky
✓ Correct answer: d) doing something risky
ExplanationThe most appropriate meaning of the idiom "walking on thin ice" is D. doing something risky.Doing something risky accurately reflects the figurative meaning of being in a precarious or dangerous situation, just like walking on ice that might break.Living this life is certainly like walking on thin ice.
Q7 FREE PREVIEW
Find the meaning of the given idiom. Round peg in a square hole
aUntrustworthy
bInvalid
cIn an unpleasant situation
dA misfit
✓ Correct answer: d) A misfit
ExplanationThe meaning of the idiom "round peg in a square hole" is D. A misfit.A misfit perfectly captures the essence of the idiom - someone who doesn't fit well in a particular situation or role, just like a round peg wouldn't fit in a square hole.
