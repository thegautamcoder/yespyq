Q1 FREE PREVIEW

Who published 'Independence'?

a

Annie Besant

b

Motilal Nchru

c

Gandhiji

d

Martin Luther

✓ Correct answer: a)

Annie Besant

Explanation

Annie Besant published the newspaper ‘Independence’ to spread nationalist ideas and demand self-rule (Home Rule) for India. The paper played an important role in creating political awareness among Indians.
