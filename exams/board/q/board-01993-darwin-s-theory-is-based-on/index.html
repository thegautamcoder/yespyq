Darwin's theory is based on

Q1 FREE PREVIEW
PYQ

Darwin's theory is based on

a

Mutation

b

Acquired character

c

Natural selection

d

None of these

✓ Correct answer: c)

Natural selection

Explanation

Darwin’s theory of evolution is based on the principle of natural selection, which states that individuals with favorable variations are more likely to survive, reproduce, and pass on their traits to the next generation. Over time, this leads to the gradual evolution of species. Darwin did not base his theory on mutation or acquired characters; these ideas came from other scientists. Hence, the correct answer is natural selection.
