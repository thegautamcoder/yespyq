My children will not be judged by the _______ of their skin.

a

colour

b

food habits

c

education

d

culture

✓ Correct answer: a)

colour

Explanation

This line is taken from the famous speech "I Have a Dream", which was delivered by Martin Luther King Jr. on August 28, 1963.

In this specific part of the speech, King expresses his hope for a future where his four children live in a nation where they are judged by the content of their character rather than the colour of their skin.
