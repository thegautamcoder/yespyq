Which of the following is not an ancient method of water harvesting?

a

Katta

b

Kulh

c

Dug well

d

Irish

✓ Correct answer: d)

Irish

Explanation

Katta Temporary check dam used in Kerala.
Kulh Traditional water channel system in Himachal Pradesh
Dug well Common traditional method for storing groundwater.
Irish Not related to Water Harvesting

Correct Answer: D — Irish ✅

Explanation:
Ancient water harvesting methods used in India include Katta, Kulh, and Dug well. These were traditional systems to store and manage rainwater. Irish is not an ancient method of water harvesting.
