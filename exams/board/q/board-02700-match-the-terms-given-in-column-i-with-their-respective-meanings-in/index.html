Match the terms given in Column I with their respective meanings in Column II. Select the correct option. 1

Column I

Column II

(a) Private enterprises hiring less than 10 employees

(ii) Self-employed

(b) Workers using their own resources to earn livelihood

(iii) Unorganised Sector

(c) Unemployment occurring in a specific season

(iv) Disguised Unemployment

(d) Zero Marginal Productivity

✓ Correct answer: c)

Explanation

(C) (i)-(c); (ii)-(b); (iii)-(a); (iv)-(d)

Explanation:

(i) Seasonal Unemployment → (c)

This type of unemployment occurs during a particular season, especially in agriculture.

(ii) Self-employed → (b)

Self-employed persons use their own resources to earn income, such as shopkeepers and farmers.

(iii) Unorganised Sector → (a)

It includes small private enterprises employing less than 10 workers without formal regulations.

(iv) Disguised Unemployment → (d)

In this situation, some workers have zero marginal productivity, meaning removing them does not affect output.

Hence, the correct matching is:\((i)-(c),(ii)-(b),(iii)-(a),(iv)-(d)\)
