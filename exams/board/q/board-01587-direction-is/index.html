Direction is

Q1 FREE PREVIEW

Direction is

a

Compulsory

b

Necessary

c

Unnecessary

d

Wastage of time

✓ Correct answer: a)

Compulsory

Explanation

Correct Answer: (A) Compulsory

Explanation of the correct option:
Direction is a compulsory function of management because it involves instructing, guiding, counselling, motivating and leading people in the organisation to achieve organisational objectives. Direction is considered the heart of the management process as it initiates action and converts plans into performance. Without proper direction, other management functions such as planning, organising and staffing become ineffective. In the modern competitive environment, no organisation can function or survive without the active process of direction. It acts as a vital link between preparation and results by ensuring that employees work towards organisational goals efficiently. Therefore, direction is a fundamental and unavoidable requirement in management. Hence, the correct answer is (A) Compulsory.

Explanation of incorrect options:

(B) Necessary:
This option is incorrect because although direction is necessary, the term “compulsory” expresses a stronger sense of indispensability in management.

(C) Unnecessary:
This option is incorrect because without direction employees may lack guidance and organisational activities may not function effectively.

(D) Wastage of time:
This option is incorrect because direction improves coordination, motivation and performance instead of wasting time.

Conclusion:
Direction plays a vital role in initiating action, guiding employees and converting plans into actual performance. Therefore, it is a compulsory function of management. Hence, the correct answer is (A) Compulsory.
