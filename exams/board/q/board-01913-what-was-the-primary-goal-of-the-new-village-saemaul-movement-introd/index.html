What was the primary goal of the New Village (Saemaul) Movement introduced in 1970?

a

To promote urban industrialization

b

To modernize rural areas and empower the rural population

c

To reduce foreign investment in agriculture

d

To increase government control over rural production

✓ Correct answer: b)

To modernize rural areas and empower the rural population

Explanation

Correct Answer: B. To modernize rural areas and empower the rural population

Explanation: The Saemaul Movement aimed to transform the rural population’s mindset and improve living conditions in villages, encouraging self-help and modernization in agriculture and beyond.
