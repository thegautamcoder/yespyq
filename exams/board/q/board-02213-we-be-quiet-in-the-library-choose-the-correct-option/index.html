We ................. be quiet in the library. (Choose the correct option)

Q1 FREE PREVIEW

We ................. be quiet in the library.

(Choose the correct option)

a

mustn't

b

doesn't have to

c

doesn't

d

must

✓ Correct answer: d)

must

Explanation

Sentence: We ______ be quiet in the library.

  • This sentence talks about a rule or strong obligation. In libraries, being quiet is compulsory, not optional.

  • "Must" is used to express strong necessity, duty, or rule.

  • Library rules require silence, so "must" fits perfectly.

Thus, the correct sentence is: We must be quiet in the library.

The other options are incorrect because:

A. mustn't:

  • "Mustn't" means prohibition (something is not allowed).

  • "We mustn't be quiet" means we are not allowed to be quiet, which is illogical here.

B. doesn't have to:

  • "Doesn't have to" means no necessity.

  • Also grammatically incorrect with the subject 'we' (should be "don’t have to").

C. doesn't:

  • "Doesn't" cannot be used before a base verb like 'be' in this structure.

  • Grammatically incorrect.
