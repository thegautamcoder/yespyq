Q1 FREE PREVIEW

Which of the following is not an olfactory indicator?

a

Clove oil

b

Vanilla

c

Sweet potato

d

All of these

✓ Correct answer: c)

Sweet potato

Explanation

Correct answer: C) Sweet potato

The substance which is NOT an olfactory indicator is sweet potato.

Explanation:
Olfactory indicators are substances whose smell changes in acidic or basic solutions.

  • Clove oil – olfactory indicator

  • Vanilla – olfactory indicator

  • Sweet potato – not an olfactory indicator

Hence, the correct answer is C) Sweet potato.
