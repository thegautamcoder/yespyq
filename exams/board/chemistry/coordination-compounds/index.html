Higher CFSE values correspond to more stable complexes.

✓ Correct answer: d)

Explanation

EDTA (ethylenediaminetetraacetic acid) is a hexadentate ligand, meaning it can donate six pairs of electrons to a metal ion — four from carboxylate oxygens and two from amine nitrogens.

\(\left[ Co \left( NH _3\right)_6\right]^{3+} \text { and }\left[ CoF _6\right]^{3-} \) are respectively known as :

a

Spin paired Complex, Spin free Complex

b

Spin free Complex, Spin paired Complex

c

Inner orbital Complex, Spin paired Complex

d

Outer orbital Complex, Inner orbital Complex

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

Spin paired Complex, Spin free Complex

Explanation

For the complex \( [\text{Co}(\text{NH}_3)_6]^{3+} \), \( \text{Co} \) is in the \( +3 \) oxidation state and has a \( 3d^6 \) configuration. Ammonia \( (\text{NH}_3) \) is a strong-field ligand, which causes electron pairing in the \( 3d \) orbitals. This results in an inner orbital complex with no unpaired electrons, making it a spin-paired complex.

For the complex \( [\text{CoF}_6]^{3-} \), \( \text{Co} \) is also in the \( +3 \) oxidation state with a \( 3d^6 \) configuration. Fluoride \( (\text{F}^-) \) is a weak-field ligand, which does not cause electron pairing. This results in an outer orbital complex with four unpaired electrons, making it a spin-free complex.
