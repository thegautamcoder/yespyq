Q5

Why NH4Cl is added before NH4OH for the ppt. of Fe3+ ions?

a

To decrease OH-ion concentration

b

To increase Cl-ion concentration

c

To increase NH4+ ion concentration

d

To decrease H+ ion concentration

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

To decrease OH-ion concentration

Explanation

When precipitating Fe³⁺ as Fe(OH)₃, we add NH₄Cl before NH₄OH because:

  • NH₄Cl dissociates to give NH₄⁺ ions.

  • The NH₄⁺ ions suppress the ionization of NH₄OH (common-ion effect).

  • Thus, the concentration of OH⁻ decreases, preventing excess OH⁻ in solution.

This is important because:

  • If OH⁻ concentration becomes too high, other cations (like Zn²⁺, Al³⁺) might also precipitate.

  • Lower OH⁻ ensures selective precipitation of Fe(OH)₃.

Hence, NH₄Cl is added to control (reduce) OH⁻ concentration, giving a clean precipitate.

✔ A is correct.
