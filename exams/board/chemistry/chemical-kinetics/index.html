Q1

A drug becomes ineffective when it decomposes to 50 % its concentration. If 16 mg of said drug becomes 4 mg in 12 months, find the time in which drug becomes ineffective given that decomposition of drug follows first order kinetics.

Memory Based Question 29/Jan/25 Evening shift

a

6 months

b

3 months

c

2 months

d

12 months

🔒

✓ Correct answer: a)

6 months

Explanation

The drug follows first-order kinetics. Using the formula k=2.303tlog⁡[A0][A] , we find k=0.1156 month−1. The half-life is t1/2=0.693k=6 months.

Correct answer: A (6 months).

Q2

The rate of a reaction quadruples when temperature changes from 27°C to 57°C. Calculate the energy of activation.
Given R=8.314JK-1mol-1, log4=0.6021

[NEET 2024]

a

3804 kJ/mol

b

38.04 kJ/mol

c

380.4 kJ/mol

d

3.80 kJ/mol

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

38.04 kJ/mol

Explanation

When temperature changes from T1=27∘ ⁣C=300 KT_1=27^\circ\!C=300\,K to T2=57∘ ⁣C=330 K,T_2=57^\circ\!C=330\,K, the rate quadruples:

ln⁡(k2k1)=ln⁡(4)≈1.386=EaR(1T1−1T2).\ln\left(\frac{k_2}{k_1}\right) = \ln(4)\approx1.386 = \frac{E_a}{R}\Bigl(\frac{1}{T_1}-\frac{1}{T_2}\Bigr).

So,

Ea=1.386 R(1300−1330)=1.386×8.314×11300−1330≈38 kJ mol−1.E_a = \frac{1.386\,R}{\left(\tfrac{1}{300}-\tfrac{1}{330}\right)} =1.386\times8.314\times\frac{1}{\tfrac{1}{300}-\tfrac{1}{330}} \approx 38\,\text{kJ mol}^{-1}.

Q3

For an elementary reaction

A + B→C+D

When volume becomes 13rd, rate of reaction becomes:

a

8 times

b

9 times

c

6 times

d

2 times

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

9 times

Explanation

For the elementary reaction A + B → C + D, rate law is Rate = k[A][B]. When volume becomes 1/3rd, concentration increases 3 times. Since rate ∝ [A][B], new rate = 9 times the initial rate. Final answer: Rate increases 9 times.

Q4

The reaction A2+B2→2AB follows the mechanism

A2⇌k-1k1A+A (fast) A+B2→k2AB+B (slow) A+B→AB (fast) 

The overall order of the reaction is

a

2

b

1.5

c

2.5

d

3

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

1.5

Explanation

Rate =k2[A]B2

K=[A]2A2

Rearrange to express [A]:

[A]=[K]1/2A21/2

Substituting this into the rate law:

  Rate =k2K1/2A21/2B2 Rate =kA21/2B2 

where k=k2K1/2 is a new rate constant. From the rate law:

 Rate =kA21/2B21

Order with respect to A2=0.5
Order with respect to B2=1
The overall order of the reaction:

0.5+1=1.5

Find the order of the reaction

A+B →F

if the mechanism of the reaction is given below:
Step 1: A+B →D (slow)
Step 2: D →C+E (fast)
Step 3: C+E→F( fast)

b

2

d

4

Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

Explanation

The order of the reaction is determined by the rate law expression of the slowest step of the reaction, which is the rate-determining step

Q10

Consider an elementary reaction

A(g)+B(g)→C(g)+D(g)


If the volume of reaction mixture is suddenly reduced to 13 of its initial volume, the reaction rate will become ' x ' times of the original reaction rate. The value of x is:

[JEE Main 2025, 28 Jan (Shift 2)]

a

13

b

19

c

3

d

9

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

9

Explanation

The rate law for the reaction is given by: R1=k[A]1[B]1
Now, if the volume is reduced to one-third, the concentration of each reactant increases by a factor of 3 . Therefore, the new rate R2 is:

 R2=k[3A]1[3B]1 R2=9R1
