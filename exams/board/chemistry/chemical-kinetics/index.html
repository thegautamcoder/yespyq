Q1 FREE PREVIEW

A drug becomes ineffective when it decomposes to 50 % its concentration. If 16 mg of said drug becomes 4 mg in 12 months, find the time in which drug becomes ineffective given that decomposition of drug follows first order kinetics.

Memory Based Question 29/Jan/25 Evening shift

a

6 months

b

3 months

c

2 months

d

12 months

✓ Correct answer: a)

6 months

Explanation

The drug follows first-order kinetics. Using the formula k=2.303tlog⁡[A0][A] , we find k=0.1156 month−1. The half-life is t1/2=0.693k=6 months.

Correct answer: A (6 months).
