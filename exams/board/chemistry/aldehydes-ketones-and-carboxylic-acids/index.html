Q1 FREE PREVIEW

\({\mathrm{CH}}_{3}-\mathrm{C}\equiv \mathrm{CH}\overset{\mathrm{Pd}/\mathrm{C}}{\underset{{\mathrm{H}}_{2}}{\to }}\mathrm{A}\overset{(\mathrm{i}){\mathrm{O}}_{3}}{\underset{(\mathrm{ii})\mathrm{Zn},{\mathrm{H}}_{2}\mathrm{O}}{\to }}\mathrm{B}+\mathrm{C}\)

Products B and C are :

Memory Based Question 28/Jan/25 Evening shift

a

Ethanal and formaldehyde

b

Propanal and formaldehyde

c

Ethanal and propanal

d

Propanal only

✓ Correct answer: a)

Ethanal and formaldehyde

Explanation

Step 1: Hydrogenation (H₂/Pd-C)
Propyne (CH₃-C≡CH) undergoes partial hydrogenation to form propene (CH₃-CH=CH₂).

Step 2: Ozonolysis (O₃/Zn, H₂O)
Ozonolysis cleaves the C=C bond in propene, producing:
- Ethanal (CH₃CHO)
- Formaldehyde (HCHO)

Thus, the correct answer is: (A) Ethanal and Formaldehyde.
