Q1

Statement 1: Fructose can give Tollens test even though it does not have an aldehyde group.

Statement 2: When reacted with base fructose can undergo rearrangement to produce an aldehyde group.

(JEE mains memory based shift-1 23/01/2025)

a

Both Statement 1 and 2 are true and statement 2 is the correct explanation of statement 1

b

Both Statement 1 and 2 are true but statement 2 is not the correct explanation of statement 1

c

Statement 1 is true and statement 2 is false

d

Both statement 1 and 2 are false

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

Statement 1 is true and statement 2 is false

Explanation

Statement 1 is true because fructose can give the Tollens’ test due to its ability to form an aldehyde group in an alkaline medium.

Statement 2 should be: When reacted with a base, fructose undergoes tautomerization to form an aldehyde group.

A. Tollen's reagent

D. NH2OH

E. NaHSO3

[NEET 2024]

a

E and D

b

B and C

c

A and D

d

B and E

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

✓ Correct answer: d)

B and E

Explanation

Glucose (being an α‐hydroxy aldehyde in equilibrium)

  • does give Tollen’s test (A), react with HCN (C) and form an oxime with NH₂OH (D).

    Hence, B and E are the reagents with no characteristic reaction.

Explanation

The C1-C4 glycosidic linkage is present in maltose, lactose, and amylopectin. However, sucrose has a C1-C2 glycosidic bond between glucose (α-1) and fructose (β-2). Thus, sucrose does not contain a C1-C4 linkage.

Q9

Given below are two statements:
Statement I : D-glucose pentaacetate reacts with 2, 4-dinitrophenylhydrazine
Statement II : Starch, on heating with concentrated sulfuric acid at 100°C and 2-3 atmosphere pressure produces glucose.

In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below

[JEE Main 2025, 28 Jan (Shift 1)]

a

Both Statement I and Statement II are false

b

Both Statement I and Statement II are true

c

Statement I is true but Statement II is false

d

Statement I is false but Statement II is true

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

Statement I is false but Statement II is true

Explanation

2, 4-Dinitrophenylhydrazine (DNPH) reacts specifically with free aldehydes and ketones to form hydrazones.

Since D-glucose pentaacetate does not have a free aldehyde group, it does not react with DNPH.

Therefore, Statement I is false.

Q11

Identify correct conversion during acidic hydrolysis from the following :
(A) starch gives galactose.
(B) cane sugar gives equal amount of glucose and fructose.
(C) milk sugar gives glucose and galactose.
(D) amylopectin gives glucose and fructose.
(E) amylose gives only glucose.

Choose the correct answer from the options given below

[JEE Main 2025, 28 Jan (Shift 2)]

a

(B), (C) and (E) only

b

(A),(B) and (C) only

c

(C), (D) and (E) only

d

(B), (C) and (D) only

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

(B), (C) and (E) only

Explanation

- Starch, upon acidic hydrolysis, gives glucose molecules.
- Cane sugar (sucrose) is a disaccharide made up of glucose and fructose. Upon hydrolysis, it breaks down into equal amounts of glucose and fructose.
- Milk sugar (lactose) is a disaccharide made of glucose and galactose. Upon hydrolysis, it breaks down into glucose and galactose.
- Amylopectin is a polysaccharide composed of glucose units.
- Amylose, a type of starch, is a polysaccharide made up of glucose units, and upon hydrolysis, it yields only glucose.

Q16

The alpha-helix and beta-pleated sheet structures of protein are associated with its

[JEE Main 2025, 23 Jan (Shift 2)]

a

quaternary structure

b

primary structure

c

tertiary structure

d

secondary structure

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

secondary structure

Explanation

The correct answer is (D) Secondary structure.

The α-helix and β-pleated sheet are secondary structures of proteins, stabilized by hydrogen bonds between the amide (−NH) and carbonyl (−C=O) groups of the peptide backbone. These structures provide proteins with stability and specific folding patterns, distinct from primary (sequence), tertiary (3D folding), and quaternary (subunit arrangement) structures.
