Q1 FREE PREVIEW

Lanthanoid ions with 4f7 configuration are :
(A) Eu2+
(B) Gd3+
(C) Eu3+
(D) Tb3+
(E)  Sm2+

Choose the correct answer from the options given below

a

(A) and (B) only

b

(B) and (C) only

c

(A) and (D) only

d

(B) and (E) only

✓ Correct answer: a)

(A) and (B) only

Explanation

Eu2+:[Xe]4f76s0Gd3+:[Xe]4f75d06s0Eu3+:[Xe]4f66s0Tb3+:[Xe]4f86s0Sm2+:[Xe]4f66s0

Hence, only Eu2+ and Gd3+ have 4f7 configuration.

Q3 FREE PREVIEW

Consider the following element in in TI, Al, and Pb .The most stable oxidation states of elements with highest and lowest first Ionisation enthalpies, respectively are:

Memory Based Question 28/Jan/25 Morning shift

a

+4 and +1

b

+2 and +3

c

+4 and +3

d

+1 and +4

✓ Correct answer: b)

+2 and +3

Explanation

The ionization energy depends upon the size and effective nuclear charge. when we move down the group I.E decreases and across the period the I.E increases.

The order of I.E is given as Pb>Tl >Al>In (Tl has more I.E than Al due to poor shielding of f orbital electrons)

The most stable oxidation state of Pb is +2, due to the inert pair effect, and In has a stable oxidation state is +3 .
