Q4

The species formed on fluorination of phosphorus pentachloride in a polar organic solvent are

[JEE Advanced 2024, Paper-2]

a

PF4+PF6-and PCl4+PF6-

b

PCl4+PCl4F2-and PCl4+PF6-

c

PF3 and PCl3

d

PF5 and PCl3

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

PCl4+PCl4F2-and PCl4+PF6-

Explanation

In a polar solvent, PCl5 undergoes auto-ionization to form ionic species [PCl4]+ [PCl6]–. Upon fluorination, fluorine, being more electronegative and smaller, tends to replace chlorine in the coordination sphere. This results in the formation of ionic isomers such as [PCl4]+ [PCl4F2]– and [PCl4]+ [PF6]–.

Q5

Among Group 16 elements, which one does NOT show -2 oxidation state?

a

PO

b

O

c

Se

d

Te

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

PO

Explanation

Oxygen, sulphur, selenium, and tellurium can show the −2 oxidation state. However, polonium (being a heavier, more metallic Group 16 element) does not typically exhibit a stable −2 state; its common oxidation states are +2 and +4 (and it can reach +6 in some compounds), but the −2 state is unknown for Po.
