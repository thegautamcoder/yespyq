Q4

The species formed on fluorination of phosphorus pentachloride in a polar organic solvent are

[JEE Advanced 2024, Paper-2]

a

PF4+PF6-and PCl4+PF6-

b

PCl4+PCl4F2-and PCl4+PF6-

c

PF3 and PCl3

d

PF5 and PCl3

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

PCl4+PCl4F2-and PCl4+PF6-

Explanation

In a polar solvent, PCl5 undergoes auto-ionization to form ionic species [PCl4]+ [PCl6]–. Upon fluorination, fluorine, being more electronegative and smaller, tends to replace chlorine in the coordination sphere. This results in the formation of ionic isomers such as [PCl4]+ [PCl4F2]– and [PCl4]+ [PF6]–.

Q9

Which of the following group-15 element forms pπ-pπ bond and strongest basic hydride? (memory based jee mains shift-2 28/01/2025)

c

Z=33

d

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

Z=7

Explanation

Nitrogen (Z=7) forms strong pπ−pπ bonds (e.g., N≡N in N₂) due to its small size and effective orbital overlap. It also has the strongest basic hydride (NH₃) because of its highly available lone pair. Heavier Group-15 elements (P, As, Sb) have weaker bonding and less basic hydrides. Correct answer: (A) Z=7.

Q20

Identify the incorrect statements about group 15 elements :
(A) Dinitrogen is a diatomic gas which acts like an inert gas at room temperature.
(B) The common oxidation states of these elements are \(-3,+3\) and +5 .
(C) Nitrogen has unique ability to form \(p \pi- p \pi\) multiple bonds.
(D) The stability of +5 oxidation states increases down the group.
(E) Nitrogen shows a maximum covalency of 6.

Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

a

(A), (C), (E) only

b

(D) and (E) only

c

(A), (B), (C) only

d

(B), (D), (E) only

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

(D) and (E) only

Explanation

Statement (D) is incorrect: The stability of the +5 oxidation state decreases down Group 15, while the stability of the +3 oxidation state increases. This is due to the inert pair effect, where the outermost s-electrons become reluctant to participate in bonding for the heavier elements.

Statement (E) is incorrect: Nitrogen (N) is in the second and has only four available orbitals (one s and three p) in its valence shell, allowing a maximum covalency of 4. It cannot expand its octet to a covalency of 6 because it lacks d-orbitals.
