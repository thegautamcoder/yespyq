Q1 FREE PREVIEW

Compounds formed by transfer of electrons are called

a

Organic

b

Covalent

c

Electrovalent

d

None of these

✓ Correct answer: c)

Electrovalent

Explanation

Compounds formed by the transfer of electrons are called ionic compounds.

Correct answer: C) Ionic compounds

Explanation:

  • In these compounds, one atom loses electrons and another atom gains electrons, creating an electrostatic attraction between them.

  • Examples: NaCl, KBr
