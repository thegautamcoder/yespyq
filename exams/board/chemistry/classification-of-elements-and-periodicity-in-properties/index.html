Q6

An element ' E ' has the ionisation enthalpy value of 374 kJ mol-1. ' E ' reacts with elements A, B, C and D with electron gain enthalpy values of -328,-349,-325 and -295 kJ mol-1, respectively. The correct order of the products EA, EB, EC and ED in terms of ionic character is :

a

EA > EB > EC > ED

b

EB > EA > EC > ED

c

ED > EC > EA > EB

d

ED > EC > EB > EA

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

EB > EA > EC > ED

Explanation

Higher electron gain enthalpy (more negative) means a stronger attraction for electrons, increasing ionic character. Since B (–349 kJ/mol) has the most negative electron gain enthalpy, it forms the most ionic bond with E . The decreasing order of electron gain enthalpy is:
B (–349) > A (–328) > C (–325) > D (–295)
This means EB has the most ionic character, followed by EA, EC, and ED.

Q7

Statement -1: Correct order of ionic radius for Mg2+, Na+, O2-and F- is F->O2->Na+>Mg2+

Statement -2: The correct order of magnitude of electron gain enthalpy for the 17th group follows the order Cl>F>Br >I (Magnitude only )

(Memory based 29 Jan /2025 morning shift )

a

Both Statement -1 and Statement -2 are correct

b

Statement -1 is correct and Statement -2 is incorrect

c

Both statement -1 and Statement -2 are incorrect

d

Statement -1 is incorrect and Statement -2 is correct

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

Statement -1 is incorrect and Statement -2 is correct

Explanation

The radius of a cation will be smaller than that of the anion as a cation will have a greater positive charge (i.e. number of protons) so it will attract the electrons in the outermost orbital with greater force and hence the smaller size. The more negative charge on the anion more its ionic radius and more charge on the cation , the smaller its ionic radius. Hence, the correct order of ionic radius will be O2->F->Na+>Mg2+

Down the group, the value of electron gain enthalpy becomes less negative and it should follow the order F>Cl>Br>I
However due to the extremely small size of F, the electron gain enthalpy of F is less negative than Cl. The incoming electron feels repulsion to the electrons of F.
Therefore, the correct order will be F<Cl>Br>I

Q25

In case of isoelectronic species the size of \(F ^{-}, Ne\) and \(Na ^{+}\)is affected by :

[JEE Main 2024, 1 Feb (Shift 1)]

a

Principal quantum number (n)

b

Ionic radius

c

Electron-electron interaction in the outer orbitals

d

Nuclear charge (z)

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

Nuclear charge (z)

Explanation

For the isoelectronic species F⁻, Ne, Na⁺, all have 10 electrons.

So, their size cannot depend on:

  • (A) Principal quantum number (n) → same n

  • (B) Ionic radius → this is what we are trying to compare, not the cause

  • (C) Electron–electron interaction → same number of electrons → nearly same e–e repulsion

The only factor that changes is:

✔ Nuclear charge (Z)

More nuclear charge pulls electrons inward → smaller radius.

Order of Z:
F (Z=9) < Ne (Z=10) < Na (Z=11)

Thus radius order:
F⁻ > Ne > Na⁺

Which matches the observed trend.

✅ Correct Answer: D — Nuclear charge (Z)
