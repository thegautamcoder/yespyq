Q1 FREE PREVIEW

The products A and B in the following reactions, respectively are

A←Ag-NO2CH3-CH2-CH2-Br→AgCNB

[JEE Main 2025, 28 Jan (Shift 1)]

a

CH3-CH2-CH2-NO2,CH3-CH2-CH2-CN

b

CH3-CH2-CH2-ONO,CH3-CH2-CH2-NC

c

CH3-CH2-CH2-ONO,CH3-CH2-CH2-CN

d

CH3-CH2-CH2-NO2,CH3-CH2-CH2-NC

✓ Correct answer: d)

CH3-CH2-CH2-NO2,CH3-CH2-CH2-NC

Explanation

AgCN is covalent compound and AgNO2 is ionic compound.
A is CH3-CH2-CH2-NO2,B is CH3-CH2- CH2-NC.
