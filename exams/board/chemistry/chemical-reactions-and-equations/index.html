Q1 FREE PREVIEW

What is the chemical name of slaked lime ?

a

Calcium Oxychloride

b

Calcium Chloride

c

Calcium Oxide

d

Calcium Hydroxide

✓ Correct answer: d)

Calcium Hydroxide

Explanation

The chemical name of slaked lime is calcium hydroxide (Ca(OH)₂).

Explanation:

  • When quick lime (CaO) is mixed with water, it reacts as:
    \([CaO+{H}_{2}O\to Ca(OH{)}_{2}]\)

  • The product Ca(OH)₂ is called slaked lime.

Chemical Name Formula Common Name
Calcium Oxychloride \({\mathrm{CaOCl}}_{2}\) Bleaching Powder
Calcium Chloride \({\mathrm{CaCl}}_{2}\) Drying Agent
Calcium Oxide CaO Quicklime
Calcium Hydroxide \(\mathrm{Ca}{(\mathrm{OH})}_{2}\) Slaked lime
