Q3

The four quantum numbers for the electron in the outer most orbital of potassium (atomic no. 19) are

a

n=4,l=0,m=0,s=+1/2

b

n=2,l=0,m=0,s=+1/2

c

n=3,l=2,m=−1,s=+1/2

d

n=3,l=0,m=1,s=+1/2

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

n=4,l=0,m=0,s=+1/2

Explanation

Step-by-step Short Solution:
- Potassium (Z = 19)

Electron configuration:

\[
1 s^2 2 s^2 2 p^6 3 s^2 3 p^6 4 s^1
\]

  • The outermost electron is in the 4 s orbital, so:
  • Principal quantum number \(n=4\)
  • Azimuthal quantum number \(l=0\) (since it's an 's' orbital)
  • Magnetic quantum number \(m=0\) (only one value for \(l=0\) )
  • Spin quantum number \(s=+\frac{1}{2}\) (assumed to be \(+1 / 2\) for the first electron)

Final Answer:
A) \(n=4, l=0, m=0, s=+\frac{1}{2}\)
