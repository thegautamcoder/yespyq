Q3

Which of the following molecules(s) show/s paramagnetic behavior?
(A) O2
(B) N2
(C) F2
(D) S2
(E) Cl2
Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

a

B only

b

A & C only

c

A & E only

d

A & D only

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

A & D only

Explanation

• O2 → 16 e; configuration ends with π*(2px)1, π*(2py)1 → 2 unpaired electrons paramagnetic
• N2 → 14 e; fully paired → diamagnetic
• F2 → 18 e; fully paired → diamagnetic
• S2 → 32 e; MO similar to O2, unpaired electrons in n* orbitals → paramagnetic
• Cl2 → 34 e; fully paired → diamagnetic
So, paramagnetic molecules are O2 and S2.
Correct Option: D (A & D only)

Q4

Number of σ and π bonds respectively in hex-1-en-4-yne are:

Memory Based Question 29/Jan/25 Evening shift

a

13,3

b

14,3

c

3,14

d

14,13

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

14,3

Explanation

Hex-1-en-4-yne (C₆H₈) contains:

  • Sigma (σ) bonds: 14 (4 C–C single bonds, 8 C–H bonds, 1 sigma bond in C=C, and 1 sigma bond in C≡C).
  • Pi (π) bonds: 3 (one from C=C and two from C≡C).

Thus, the correct answer is 14 σ and 3 π bonds.

Q5

The correct statement/s about Hydrogen bonding is/are
A. Hydrogen bonding exists when H is covalently bonded to the highly electronegative atom.
B. Intermolecular H bonding is present in o-nitro phenol
C. Intramolecular H bonding is present in HF
D. The magnitude of H bonding depends on the physical state of the compound.
E. H-bonding has powerful effect on the structure and properties of compounds

Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

[JEE Main 2024, 4 Apr (Shift 2)]

a

A, B, C only

b

A only

c

A, B, D only

d

A, D, E only

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

A, D, E only

Explanation

  • A – True: Hydrogen bonding occurs when H is covalently bonded to a highly electronegative atom (F, O, N).

  • B – False: o-Nitrophenol shows intramolecular, not intermolecular, hydrogen bonding.

  • C – False: HF has intermolecular hydrogen bonding; intramolecular H-bonding is not possible in a diatomic molecule.

  • D – True: Strength of H-bonding depends on physical state (solid > liquid > gas).

  • E – True: Hydrogen bonding strongly affects structure and properties (boiling point, solubility, etc.).

Hence, A, D and E are correct → Option D.

Q20

Given below are two statements :
Statement (I): Experimentally determined oxygen-oxygen bond lengths in the O3 are found to be same and the bond length is greater than that of a O = O (double bond) but less than that of a single (O − O) bond.
Statement (II) : The strong lone pair-lone pair repulsion between oxygen atoms is solely responsible for the fact that the bond length in ozone is smaller than that of a double bond (O = O) but more than that of a single bond (O − O).
In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below :

[JEE Main 2025, 24 Jan (Shift 2)]

a

Statement I is false but Statement II is true

b

Both Statement I and Statement II are true

c

Both Statement I and Statement II are false

d

Statement I is true but Statement II is false

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

Statement I is true but Statement II is false

Explanation

The central oxygen atom in ozone is sp2 hybridized containing a lone pair of electron, as a result ozone has an angular structure with a bond angle of 117°. It is a resonance hybrid of two resonating structures. Because of resonance, both the oxygen- oxygen bonds have partial double bond character. In other words, both the oxygen-oxygen bonds are equal ( 128 pm ) and lie in between those of oxygen- oxygen double bond length of 121 pm and oxygen-oxygen single bond length of 148 pm .
