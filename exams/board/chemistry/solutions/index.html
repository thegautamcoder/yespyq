The density of ' \(x\) ' M solution (' \(x\) ' molar) of NaOH is 1.12 g mL-1, while in molality, the concentration of the solution is 3 m (3 molal). Then \(x\) is
(Given : Molar mass of NaOH is 40 g/mol )

Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

3.0

Explanation

  • Density of solution = 1.12 g/mL

  • Molality (m) = 3 mol/kg

  • Molar mass of NaOH = 40 g/mol

  • Molarity (x) = ?

We are to find the molarity xx (in mol/L) of NaOH.

Molality m=mol of solutemass of solvent in kgm = \frac{\text{mol of solute}}{\text{mass of solvent in kg}}

Given:

Molality=3 mol/kg\text{Molality} = 3 \, \mathrm{mol/kg}

Let’s assume we have 1 kg (1000 g) of water. Then the number of moles of NaOH = 3 mol.

Mass of NaOH =

3 mol×40 g/mol=120 g3 \, \text{mol} \times 40 \, \mathrm{g/mol} = 120 \, \mathrm{g}

So total mass of solution =

1000 g (water)+120 g (NaOH)=1120 g1000 \, \mathrm{g} \text{ (water)} + 120 \, \mathrm{g} \text{ (NaOH)} = 1120 \, \mathrm{g}

Given density = 1.12 g/mL

So for 1120 g of solution:

Volume=1120 g1.12 g/mL=1000 mL=1 L\text{Volume} = \frac{1120 \, \mathrm{g}}{1.12 \, \mathrm{g/mL}} = 1000 \, \mathrm{mL} = 1 \, \mathrm{L}

Molarity x=mol of solutevolume of solution in Lx = \frac{\text{mol of solute}}{\text{volume of solution in L}}

We have 3.0 mol NaOH in 1 L of solution

x = 3.0

KbH2O=0.52Kkgmol-1

Which of the following is correct ?

Q5

The Henry's law constant KH values of three gases (A, B, C) in water are 145, 2×10-5 and 35 kbar, respectively. The solubility of these gases in water follow the order

a

A>B>C

b

B>A>C

c

B>C>A

d

A>C>B

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

B>C>A

Explanation

According to Henry’s law,
Lower KH means higher solubility.
Given:

  • Kₕ(B) = 2 × 10⁻⁵ (lowest) → highest solubility

  • Kₕ(C) = 35

  • Kₕ(A) = 145 (highest) → lowest solubility
    So, B > C > A.

Q9

When a non-volatile solute is added to the solvent, the vapour pressure of the solvent decreases by 10 mm Hg. The mole fraction of the solute in the solution is 0.2. What would be the mole fraction of the solvent if decrease in vapour pressure is 20 mm Hg?

[JEE Main 2025, 23 Jan (Shift 2)]

a

0.2

b

0.6

c

0.4

d

0.8

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

0.6

Explanation

Using Raoult’s Law,

ΔPP0=mole fraction of solute(XB)

Given, ΔP = 10 mm Hg and XB = 0.2, so P₀ = 10/0.2 = 50 mm Hg.

For ΔP = 20 mm Hg,

XB=2050=0.4

Thus, mole fraction of solvent (XA) = 1 - 0.4 = 0.6.
