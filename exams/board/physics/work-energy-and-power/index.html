Read the following statements carefully and choose the correct option.

Assertion: Work done by a central force is independent of the path.

Reason: Potential energy is associated with every force.

(Shift - I Memory Based)

a

Both Assertion and Reason are correct.

b

Assertion is correct, Reason is incorrect.

c

Assertion is incorrect, Reason is correct.

d

Both Assertion and Reason are incorrect.

✓ Correct answer: b)

Assertion is correct, Reason is incorrect.

Explanation

Assertion : True

Reason : False : As potential energy is associated to conservative forces only.
