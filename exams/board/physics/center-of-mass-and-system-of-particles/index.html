Q1 FREE PREVIEW

A disc of radius 20 cm has its center at origin. If a disc of radius 5 cm is cut from it such that its edge touches the edge of the bigger disc, find the location of centre of mass of the new system.

(Shift I Memory Based)

a

(-1 cm, 0 cm)

b

(-1 cm, -1 cm)

c

(1 cm, 1 cm)

d

(1cm, - 1 cm)

✓ Correct answer: a)

(-1 cm, 0 cm)

Explanation

R = 20 cm, r = 5 cm

Let the mass of big disc is M and the small disc is m.

Mass per unit area = Mπ 202mass of smaller disc, m = Mπ 202×π52= M16

The coordinates of centre of mass is given as

xcm = M×0 - m×15M - m=-M16×15M - M16 = -1 cm ycm = 0 cm
