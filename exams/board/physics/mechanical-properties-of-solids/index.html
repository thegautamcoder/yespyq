Q1 FREE PREVIEW

A material has a bulk modulus of 25 × 1011 N/m2. If it undergoes a volumetric strain of 0.2%, what is the excess pressure applied ?

(Shift -II Memory based)

a

5 × 108 N/m2

b

5 × 109 N/m2

c

5 × 1010 N/m2

d

5 × 1011 N/m2

✓ Correct answer: b)

5 × 109 N/m2

Explanation

Bulk Modulus is given by B =P V∆Vso, P = B ∆VVP = 25×1011×0.2100=5×109 N/m2
