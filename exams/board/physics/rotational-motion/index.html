Q1 FREE PREVIEW
PYQ

Torque on a uniform disk of mass \(2\text{ }\text{kg}\), radius \(1\text{ }\text{m}\), is given as \(\tau (t)=5{t}^{2}−8t\). If the disk was initially at rest, find the power by torque at \(t=1\text{ }\text{s}\).

a

5 W

b

3 W

c

7 W

d

9 W

✓ Correct answer: c)

7 W

Explanation

(3)

\(\begin{aligned}& \rho=\vec{\tau} \cdot \vec{\omega} \\& \tau=l \alpha \\& I=\frac{M R^2}{2}=1 \\& \alpha=5 t^2-8 t \\& \omega=\frac{5}{3} t^3-4 t^2 \\& \text { At } t=1, \omega=\frac{5}{3}-4=-\frac{7}{3} \\& t(3)=5-8=-3 \\& \rho=\vec{\tau} \cdot \vec{\omega}=7 \mathrm{~W}\end{aligned}\)

\(\mathrm{Relative}\mathrm{displacement}:\\ r{⃗}_{AB}=r{⃗}_{A}-r{⃗}_{B}=\left(\frac{{\alpha }_{1}}{3}-{\alpha }_{1}\right)\hat{\mathrm{i}}+\left(\frac{{\alpha }_{2}}{2}-{\alpha }_{2}\right)\hat{\mathrm{j}}+\left({\alpha }_{3}-\frac{{\alpha }_{3}}{3}\right)\hat{\mathrm{k}}\\ r{⃗}_{AB}=\left(-\frac{2{\alpha }_{1}}{3}\right)\hat{\mathrm{i}}+\left(-\frac{{\alpha }_{2}}{2}\right)\hat{\mathrm{j}}+\left(\frac{2{\alpha }_{3}}{3}\right)\hat{\mathrm{k}}\\ \mathrm{Substitute}{\alpha }_{1}=2,{\alpha }_{2}=3\mathrm{n},{\alpha }_{3}=4\mathrm{p}\\ r{⃗}_{AB}=\left(-\frac{4}{3}\right)\hat{\mathrm{i}}+\left(-\frac{3\mathrm{n}}{2}\right)\hat{\mathrm{j}}+\left(\frac{8\mathrm{p}}{3}\right)\hat{\mathrm{k}}\)

Relative velocity is given by
