Q1 FREE PREVIEW

Torque on a uniform disk of mass 2 kg2 \, \text{kg}, radius 1 m1 \, \text{m}, is given as τ(t)=5t2−8t\tau(t) = 5t^2 - 8t. If the disk was initially at rest, find the power by torque at t=1 st = 1 \, \text{s}.

a

5 W

b

3 W

c

7 W

d

9 W

✓ Correct answer: c)

7 W

Explanation

(3)

\(
\begin{aligned}
& \rho=\vec{\tau} \cdot \vec{\omega} \\
& \tau=l \alpha \\
& I=\frac{M R^2}{2}=1 \\
& \alpha=5 t^2-8 t \\
& \omega=\frac{5}{3} t^3-4 t^2 \\
& \text { At } t=1, \omega=\frac{5}{3}-4=-\frac{7}{3} \\
& t(3)=5-8=-3 \\
& \rho=\vec{\tau} \cdot \vec{\omega}=7 \mathrm{~W}
\end{aligned}
\)
