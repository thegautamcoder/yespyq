Q1 FREE PREVIEW

Which of following reaction is correct ? (Where symbols have their usual meanings)

(Shift - I Memory Based)

a

n→p+e−+νn \rightarrow p + e^- + \nu

b

n→p+e++νn \rightarrow p + e^+ + \nu

c

n→p+e++ν

d

n→p+e−+ν

✓ Correct answer: d)

n→p+e−+ν

Explanation

It is the equation of neutron (n) decays into a proton (p), in which an electron (e⁻) is emitted, and an antineutrino is emitted to conserve lepton number.

Q2 FREE PREVIEW

Energy released when two deuterons (H12) fuse to form a helium nucleus (H2e4) is :
(Given : Binding energy per nucleon of H12=1.1MeV and binding energy per nucleon of H2e4=7.0MeV )

a

8.1 MeV

b

5.9 MeV 

c

23.6 MeV

d

26.8 MeV

✓ Correct answer: c)

23.6 MeV

Explanation


Binding energy per nucleon of deuteron \( \left(^2_1\text{H}\right) = 1.1\, \text{MeV} \)
Binding energy per nucleon of helium-4 \( \left(^4_2\text{He}\right) = 7.0\, \text{MeV} \)

Two deuterons \( \Rightarrow \) total nucleons = 2 × 2 = 4

\[
\text{BE}_{\text{reactants}} = 2 \times (1.1 \times 2) = 4.4\, \text{MeV}
\]

\[
\text{BE}_{\text{product}} = 4 \times 7.0 = 28.0\, \text{MeV}
\]

\[
Q = \text{BE}_{\text{product}} - \text{BE}_{\text{reactants}} = 28.0 - 4.4 = \boxed{23.6\, \text{MeV}}
\]
