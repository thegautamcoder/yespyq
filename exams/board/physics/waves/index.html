Q1

The fundamental frequency of a closed organ pipe is equal to the first overtone frequency of an open organ pipe. If length of the open pipe is 60 cm, the length of the closed pipe will be:

a

15 cm

b

60 cm

c

45 cm

d

30 cm

Explanation

Closed pipe: fundamental f1=v4Lc.

v4Lc=vLo⇒Lc=Lo4=604=15 cm.

Q7

Statement-I : Velocity of sound in solids in more compared to that in gases.

Statement-II : Bulk modulus of gas is more than that of solids.

a

Statement-I is correct statement-II is correct

b

Statement-I is correct statement-II is incorrect

c

Statement-I is incorrect statement-II is correct

d

Statement-l is incorrect statement-II is incorrect

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

Statement-I is correct statement-II is incorrect

ExplanationStatement-I: Velocity of Sound in Solids is Greater than in Gases

  • The velocity of sound is given by:

    v=Bρv = \sqrt{\frac{B}{\rho}}

    where:

    • BB is the bulk modulus (measure of elasticity),
    • ρ\rho is density.

  • Solids have a much higher bulk modulus than gases, which increases the sound velocity.

  • In gases, BB is small, leading to a lower sound velocity.

Statement-I is Correct.

Statement-II: Bulk Modulus of Gas is More than that of Solids
  • The bulk modulus (BB) quantifies the resistance to compression.
  • Solids have a significantly higher bulk modulus than gases because they are less compressible.
  • Gases are highly compressible, so their bulk modulus is much smaller.

Statement-II is Incorrect.
