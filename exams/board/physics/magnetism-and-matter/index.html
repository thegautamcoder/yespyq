Q1 FREE PREVIEW

The magnetic moment of an iron bar is \(\mathrm{M}\). It is now bent in such a way that it forms an arc section of a circle subtending an angle of \(60^\circ\) at the centre. The magnetic moment of this arc section is :

[Re-NEET 2024]

a

\(\frac{3\mathrm{M}}{\pi }\)

b

\(\frac{4\mathrm{M}}{\pi }\)

c

\(\frac{\mathrm{M}}{\pi }\)

d

\(\frac{2\mathrm{M}}{\pi }\)

✓ Correct answer: a)

\(\frac{3\mathrm{M}}{\pi }\)

Explanation
  • Arc Length = L = Rθ (θ=π/3). Chord Length L' = 2Rsin(θ/2) = 2Rsin(π/6) = R. Since R = L/θ = 3L/π, the new magnetic length is L' = 3L/π.
  • New Moment M' = m × L' = m × (3L/π). Substitute m = M/L. M' = (M/L) × (3L/π) = 3M/π.

(NEW NCERT 12th Page No. 137, 138)

Q2 FREE PREVIEW

Which of the following substances has relative magnetic permeability \({\mu }_{\mathrm{r}}≫1\) ?

a

Aluminium

b

Copper chloride

c

Nickel

d

Sodium chloride

✓ Correct answer: c)

Nickel

Explanation

Relative magnetic permeability (\(\mu_r\)) is a measure of how much a material can become magnetized in an applied magnetic field compared to vacuum. When \(\mu_r \gg 1\), it indicates that the material is highly magnetic, typically found in ferromagnetic materials. Examples of substances with \(\mu_r \gg 1\) include iron, cobalt, and nickel.
