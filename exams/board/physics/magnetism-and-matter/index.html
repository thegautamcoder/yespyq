Q1

The magnetic moment of an iron bar is M. It is now bent in such a way that it forms an arc section of a circle subtending an angle of 60° at the centre. The magnetic moment of this arc section is :

[Re-NEET 2024]

a

3Mπ

b

4Mπ

c

Mπ

d

2Mπ

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

3Mπ

Explanation
  • Arc Length = L = Rθ (θ=π/3). Chord Length L' = 2Rsin(θ/2) = 2Rsin(π/6) = R. Since R = L/θ = 3L/π, the new magnetic length is L' = 3L/π.
  • New Moment M' = m × L' = m × (3L/π). Substitute m = M/L. M' = (M/L) × (3L/π) = 3M/π.

(NEW NCERT 12th Page No. 137, 138)

Q2

Which of the following substances has relative magnetic permeability μr≫1 ?

a

Aluminium

b

Copper chloride

c

Nickel

d

Sodium chloride

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

Nickel

Explanation

Relative magnetic permeability (\(\mu_r\)) is a measure of how much a material can become magnetized in an applied magnetic field compared to vacuum. When \(\mu_r \gg 1\), it indicates that the material is highly magnetic, typically found in ferromagnetic materials. Examples of substances with \(\mu_r \gg 1\) include iron, cobalt, and nickel.

Q3

The magnetic potential energy, when a magnetic bar of magnetic moment m→ is placed perpendicular to the magnetic field B→ is :

[Re-NEET 2024]

a

-mB2

b

zero

c

-mB

d

mB

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

zero

Explanation
  • Magnetic potential energy U = – m ⋅ B = –mB cosθ.
  • m is perpendicular to B, so θ = 90°.
  • U = –mB cos(90°) = –mB * 0 = 0.

    (NEW NCERT 12th Page No. 137, 138, 139, 140, 141)

Q7

Which of the following pairs is that of paramagnetic materials?

a

Copper and Aluminium

b

Sodium and Calcium

c

Lead and Iron

d

Nickel and Cobalt

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

Sodium and Calcium

Explanation

Paramagnetic materials have unpaired electrons, which allows them to be weakly attracted to a magnetic field. Sodium and Calcium both have unpaired electrons in their outer shells, making them paramagnetic.
