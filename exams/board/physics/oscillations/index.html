Q1 FREE PREVIEW

A particle is executing simple harmonic motion with time period 2 s and amplitude 1 cm . If D and d are the total distance and displacement covered by the particle in 12.5 s , then Dd is

a

10

b

25

c

154

d

165

✓ Correct answer: b)

25

Explanation

The period T is 2 seconds, so the number of complete cycles in 12.5 seconds is:

n=12.52=6.25 cycles. 

In one full cycle, the particle moves a distance of 4 A (since it goes from +A to -A and back to +A).
So, in 6.25 cycles, the total distance covered is:

D=6.25×4A=6.25×4×1=25cm

Since after 6 full cycles, the particle returns to its starting position, it only moves A in the last incomplete cycle.
Therefore, the displacement is d =1 cm.

Dd=251=25
