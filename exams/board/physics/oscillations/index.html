Q1

A particle is executing simple harmonic motion with time period 2 s and amplitude 1 cm . If D and d are the total distance and displacement covered by the particle in 12.5 s , then Dd is

a

10

b

25

c

154

d

165

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

25

Explanation

The period T is 2 seconds, so the number of complete cycles in 12.5 seconds is:

n=12.52=6.25 cycles. 

In one full cycle, the particle moves a distance of 4 A (since it goes from +A to -A and back to +A).
So, in 6.25 cycles, the total distance covered is:

D=6.25×4A=6.25×4×1=25cm

Since after 6 full cycles, the particle returns to its starting position, it only moves A in the last incomplete cycle.
Therefore, the displacement is d =1 cm.

Dd=251=25

Q3

Two particles of same mass are performing SHM vertically with two different springs of spring constants \(K_1\) and \(K_2\). If amplitude of both is same. Find ratio of the maximum speed of two particles.

a

\(\sqrt{\frac{K_1}{K_2}}\)

b

\(\sqrt{K_1 K_2}\)

c

\(\sqrt{\frac{K_2}{K_1}}\)

d

\(\sqrt{\frac{K_1+K_2}{K_1-K_2}}\)

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

\(\sqrt{\frac{K_1}{K_2}}\)

ExplanationStep 1: Understanding SHM and Maximum Speed

For a simple harmonic motion (SHM), the maximum speed Vmax​ of a particle is given by:

Vmax=Aω

where:

  • AA is the amplitude of oscillation.

  • ω\omega is the angular frequency, given by:

    ω=km\omega = \sqrt{\frac{k}{m}}​

Thus, the maximum speed becomes:

Vmax=Akm​

Step 2: Finding the Ratio of Maximum Speeds

Let the two particles have spring constants K1K_1​ and K2K_2 but the same mass mm and same amplitude AA.

For the first particle:

vmax⁡1=AK1mv_{\max1} = A \sqrt{\frac{K_1}{m}}​

For the second particle:

vmax⁡2=AK2mv_{\max2} = A \sqrt{\frac{K_2}{m}}

Taking the ratio:

vmax⁡1vmax⁡2=AK1mAK2m\frac{v_{\max1}}{v_{\max2}} = \frac{A \sqrt{\frac{K_1}{m}}}{A \sqrt{\frac{K_2}{m}}}​​

Canceling AAA and simplifying:

vmax⁡1vmax⁡2=K1K2\frac{v_{\max1}}{v_{\max2}} = \sqrt{\frac{K_1}{K_2}}

Step 3: Selecting the Correct Option

AK1K2\boxed{A} \quad \sqrt{\frac{K_1}{K_2}}

Q5

The measured value of the length of a simple pendulum is \(20 \mathrm{~cm}\) with \(2 \mathrm{~mm}\) accuracy. The time for 50 oscillations was measured to be 40 seconds with 1 second resolution. From these measurements, the accuracy in the measurement of acceleration due to gravity is \(\mathrm{N} \%\). The value of \(\mathrm{N}\) is:

[JEE Main 2024, 31 Jan (Shift 2)]

a

4

b

8

c

6

d

5

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

6

Explanation

\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
& \mathrm{T}=2 \pi \sqrt{\frac{\ell}{\mathrm{~g}}} \\
& \mathrm{~g}=\frac{4 \pi^2 \ell}{\mathrm{~T}^2} \\
& \text { Percentage error, } \frac{\Delta \mathrm{g}}{\mathrm{~g}}=\frac{\Delta \ell}{\ell}+\frac{2 \Delta \mathrm{~T}}{\mathrm{~T}} \\
& =\frac{0.2}{20}+2\left(\frac{1}{40}\right)=\frac{1.2}{20}
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}

percentage change = 1.220×100=6%

Q6

A spring-block system has a time period of 2 seconds. If D is the total distance traveled by the block and \(d\) is the displacement of the block in 12.5 seconds, find the ratio D/d.(Shift I - Memory Based)

a

12

b

25

c

20

d

10

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

25

Explanation

  1. Key concepts:

    • The total distance DD is the sum of all oscillations' displacements (absolute values of motion).
    • Displacement ddd is the shortest distance between the initial and final positions.
    • The time period T=2 sT = 2 \, \text{s}.

  2. Oscillation in 12.5 seconds:

    • Number of complete oscillations: n=Total timeTime period=12.52=6.25n = \frac{\text{Total time}}{\text{Time period}} = \frac{12.5}{2} = 6.25
    • This implies 6 full oscillations and 1/4 of another oscillation.

  3. Displacement (dd):

    • After 6 full oscillations, the block returns to its starting position.
    • During the remaining 0.25×T=0.5 s0.25 \times T = 0.5 \, \text{s}, the block moves from the mean position to the maximum displacement (amplitude AA).
    • Hence, d=Ad = A

  4. Total distance (DD):

    • In one complete oscillation, the block covers a distance of 4A4A(2A forward and 2A backward).
    • For 6 complete oscillations: D6 oscillations=6⋅4A=24AD_{\text{6 oscillations}} = 6 \cdot 4A = 24A
    • In the last quarter oscillation: Dlast quarter=AD_{\text{last quarter}} = A
    • Total distance: D=D6 oscillations+Dlast quarter=24A+A=25AD = D_{\text{6 oscillations}} + D_{\text{last quarter}} = 24A + A = 25A

  5. Ratio Dd\frac{D}{d}​:

    Dd=25AA=25\frac{D}{d} = \frac{25A}{A} = 25

Answer: Dd=25\frac{D}{d} = 25

A. parabolic path
B. elliptical path
C. periodic motion
D. simple harmonic motion

Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

[Re-NEET 2024]

a

B, C and D only

b

A, B and C only

c

A, C and D only

d

C and D only

✓ Correct answer: d)

Explanation

y = 2x. This is the equation of a straight line. Since x and y depend on cos(ωt), the motion is oscillatory (back and forth) along this line segment between (–A, –2A) and (A, 2A). This is Simple Harmonic Motion (SHM) and also Periodic Motion.
