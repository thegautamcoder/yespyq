Q4

For an ideal monoatomic gas undergoing an isobaric process, what is the ratio ΔQΔU\frac{\Delta Q}{\Delta U}? (Shift I - Memory Based)

a

53\frac{5}{3}

b

75\frac{7}{5}

c

43\frac{4}{3}

d

54\frac{5}{4}

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

53\frac{5}{3}

Explanation

From the first law of thermodynamics for an isobaric process: ΔQ=ΔU+ΔW.\Delta Q = \Delta U + \Delta W.

  • For a monoatomic gas:
    • ΔU=32nRΔT\Delta U = \frac{3}{2} n R \Delta T,
    • ΔW=nRΔT\Delta W = n R \Delta T.
  • Heat supplied: ΔQ=32nRΔT+nRΔT=52nRΔT.\Delta Q = \frac{3}{2} n R \Delta T + n R \Delta T = \frac{5}{2} n R \Delta T.
  • Ratio: ΔQΔU=5232=53.\frac{\Delta Q}{\Delta U} = \frac{\frac{5}{2}}{\frac{3}{2}} = \frac{5}{3}.

Thus, the ratio is 53\frac{5}{3}.

Q6

The temperature of a body of mass mm and specific heat capacity sss is raised slowly from T1T_1 to T2T_2​. The change in entropy of the system is:(Shift - II Memory Based)

a

msln⁡T2T1ms \ln \frac{T_2}{T_1}

b

msms

c

msln⁡T1T2ms \ln \frac{T_1}{T_2}

d

Zero

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

msln⁡T2T1ms \ln \frac{T_2}{T_1}

ExplanationEntropy change (ΔS\Delta S) is given by the formula:

ΔS=∫T1T2dQT\Delta S = \int_{T_1}^{T_2} \frac{dQ}{T}​

For a system where the temperature is slowly raised and the specific heat capacity is constant, the heat supplied (dQdQ) is:

dQ=ms dTdQ = m s \, dT

Substituting into the entropy formula:

ΔS=∫T1T2ms dTT\Delta S = \int_{T_1}^{T_2} \frac{ms \, dT}{T}​

This simplifies to:

ΔS=ms∫T1T2dTT\Delta S = ms \int_{T_1}^{T_2} \frac{dT}{T}​

The integral of 1T\frac{1}{T}​ is ln⁡T\ln T, so:

ΔS=ms[ln⁡T]T1T2\Delta S = ms \left[\ln T\right]_{T_1}^{T_2}

​​ ΔS=ms(ln⁡T2−ln⁡T1)\Delta S = ms \left(\ln T_2 - \ln T_1\right)

Using logarithmic properties:

ΔS=msln⁡T2T1\Delta S = ms \ln \frac{T_2}{T_1}​​

Thus, the change in entropy is:

msln⁡T2T1\boxed{ms \ln \frac{T_2}{T_1}}​​​

Q10

In a process, the pressure of a gas is directly proportional to its temperature. Choose the correct options:

A: The process is isochoric.
B: Work done in the process is zero.
C: Internal energy increases with an increase in temperature.

(Shift I - Memory Based)

a

A and B are correct.

b

A and C are correct.

c

A,B, and C are correct.

d

B and C are correct.

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

A,B, and C are correct.

ExplanationSolution:

  1. Condition:

    • If pressure P∝TP \propto T, this implies: P=kT(where k is constant).P = kT \quad \text{(where \( k \) is constant)}.
    • This relationship is only valid if the volume VV is constant, as per the ideal gas law:
    • PV=nRT  ⟹  P∝T(if V is constant).PV = nRT \quad \implies \quad P \propto T \quad \text{(if \( V \) is constant)}..
    • Thus, the process is isochoric (the statement AA is correct).

  2. Work Done:

    • In an isochoric process, the volume remains constant (ΔV=0\Delta V = 0), so the work done: W=PΔV=0.W = P \Delta V = 0. Hence, BB is also correct.

  3. Internal Energy:

    • The internal energy UU of an ideal gas depends on temperature: ΔU=nCvΔT.\Delta U = n C_v \Delta T.. Since temperature increases, internal energy also increases. Thus, CC is correct.

  4. Conclusion:

    • All statements AA, BB, and CC are correct.

Answer: (3) A,B,A, B, and CC are correct.

Q16

Given below are two statements. One is labelled as Assertion (A) and the other is labelled as Reason (R).
Assertion (A) : With the increase in the pressure of an ideal gas, the volume falls off more rapidly in an isothermal process in comparison to the adiabatic process.
Reason (R) : In isothermal process, PV = constant, while in adiabatic process PVγ= constant. Here γ is the ratio of specific heats, P is the pressure and V is the volume of the ideal gas.
In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below :

a

(A) is true but (R) is false

b

Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) is the correct explanation of (A)

c

Both (A) and (R) are true but (R) is NOT the correct explanation of (A)
