Q1 FREE PREVIEW

A problem is given to three students A,B and C, whose probabilities of solving the problem independently are 12,34 and p respectively. If the probability that the problem can be solved is 2932, then what is the value of p?

a

25

b

23

c

13

d

14

✓ Correct answer: d)

14

Explanation

Given that, P(A)=12,P(B)=34 and P(C)=p
Probability that the problem can not be solved

=P(A¯)⋅P(B¯)⋅P(C¯)

=1−121−34(1−p)

=12×14(1−p)=1−p8

∴ Probability that the problem can be solved

=1-Probability that the problem cannot be solved

⇒2932=1−(1−p)8

⇒1−p=34

∴p=14

Q2 FREE PREVIEW

If \(A\) and \(B\) are events such that P(A/B)=P(B/A)≠0, then :

a

A⊂B, but A≠B

b

A=B

c

A∩B=ϕ

d

P(A)=P(B)

✓ Correct answer: d)

P(A)=P(B)

Explanation

We know that, PA∣B=P(A∩B)P(B),

PB∣A=P(A∩B)P(A)

Now,

PA∣B=PB∣A⇒P(A∩B)P(B)=P(A∩B)P(A)

Since PA∣B≠0⇒PA∩B≠0.

Therefore,

PA∩B·PA=PA∩B·PB⇒PA=PB
