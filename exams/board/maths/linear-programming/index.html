Q1

A factory produces two products X and Y . The profit earned by selling X and \(Y\) is represented by the objective function \(Z=5 x+7 y\), where \(x\) and \(y\) are the number of units of X and Y respectively sold. Which of the following statement is correct?

a

The objective function maximizes the difference of the profit earned from products X and Y.

b

The objective function measures the total production of products X and Y

c

The objective function maximizes the combined profit earned from selling X and Y.

d

The objective function ensures the company produces more of product X than product Y .

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

The objective function maximizes the combined profit earned from selling X and Y.

Explanation

\(Z=5 x+7 y=(\) profit per X\() \times x+(\) profit per Y\() \times y\)

\(\Rightarrow Z\) is the combined profit to be maximized (in an LPP).

Q3

The number of corner points of the feasible region determined by constraints \(\mathrm{x} \geq 0, \mathrm{y} \geq 0, \mathrm{x}+\mathrm{y} \geq 4\) is :

a

0

b

1

c

2

d

3

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

2

Explanation

Boundaries: \(x=0, y=0, x+y=4\).
Intersections with feasibility:
\(x=0\) with \(x+y=4 \Rightarrow(0,4)\).
\(y=0\) with \(x+y=4 \Rightarrow(4,0)\).
\((0,0)\) not feasible since \(0 \not\ge 4\).
Unbounded region with vertices \((0,4),(4,0)\).
