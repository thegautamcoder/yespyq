Q1 FREE PREVIEW

If the first term of an arithmetic progression is 2

and the common difference is 3 , then the \({n}^{\text{th }}\) term will be :

a

\(3\mathrm{n}+2\)

b

\(3n-1\)

c

\(2n-1\)

d

\(3n-2\)

✓ Correct answer: b)

\(3n-1\)

Explanation

We know that formula for the \({n}^{th}\) term of an A.P.

\({a}_{n}=a+(n−1)d\)

Where:

a= first term

= common difference

Substitute the given values

\(a=2,\ d=3\)

\({a}_{n}=2+(n−1)⋅3\)

\({a}_{n}=2+3n−3\)

\({a}_{n}=3n−1\)
