Q2

Find the matrix A2, where A=aij is a 2×2 matrix whose elements are given by aij=maximum(i,j)-minimum(i,j) :

a

0000

b

0110

c

1001

d

1111

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

1001

Explanation

Given: aij=max(i,j)-min(i,j)

So,

a11=max(1,1)-min(1,1)=1-1=0,a12=max(1,2)-min(1,2)=2-1=1,a21=max(2,1)-min(2,1)=2-1=1,a22=max(2,2)-min(2,2)=2-2=0

The matrix A is: A=0110

Hence,

A2=A×A=0110×0110=1001

Q8

If \(F(x)=\left[\begin{array}{ccc}\cos x & -\sin x & 0 \\ \sin x & \cos x & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1\end{array}\right]\) and \([F(x)]^2=F(k x)\), then the value of \(k\) is :

a

1

b

2

c

0

d

-2

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

2

Explanation

Given: \({[\mathrm{F}(\mathrm{x})]^2=\mathrm{F}(\mathrm{kx})} \)
\( {\left[\begin{array}{ccc}
\cos x & -\sin x & 0 \\
\sin x & \cos x & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 1
\end{array}\right]\left[\begin{array}{ccc}
\cos x & -\sin x & 0 \\
\sin x & \cos x & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 1
\end{array}\right]=\left[\begin{array}{ccc}
\cos k x & -\sin k x & 0 \\
\sin k x & \cos k x & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 1
\end{array}\right]} \)
\(\Rightarrow {\left[\begin{array}{ccc}
\cos ^2 x-\sin ^2 x & -\cos x \sin x-\sin x \cos x & 0 \\ \sin x \cos x+\cos x \sin x & -\sin ^2 x+\cos ^2 x & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1
\end{array}\right]=\left[\begin{array}{ccc}
\cos k x & -\sin k x & 0 \\
\sin k x & \cos k x & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 1
\end{array}\right]} \)
\(\Rightarrow {\left[\begin{array}{ccc}
\cos 2 x & -2 \sin x \cos x & 0 \\
2 \sin x \cos x & \cos 2 x & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 1
\end{array}\right]=\left[\begin{array}{ccc}
\cos k x & -\sin k x & 0 \\
\sin k x & \cos k x & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 1
\end{array}\right]}\)

\((\because \cos 2 x=\cos ^2 x-\sin ^2 x\))

\(\Rightarrow\left[\begin{array}{ccc}
\cos 2 x & -\sin 2 x & 0 \\
\sin 2 x & \cos 2 x & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 1
\end{array}\right]=\left[\begin{array}{ccc}
\cos k x & -\sin k x & 0 \\
\sin k x & \cos k x & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 1
\end{array}\right]\)

\((\because \sin 2 x=2 \sin x \cos x)\)
The above matrix equation, we get \(\cos 2 x=\cos k x\) and \(\sin 2 x=\sin k x\)

\( \Rightarrow 2 x=k x \)
\( \Rightarrow k=2 \)
