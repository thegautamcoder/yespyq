Q1 FREE PREVIEW

Let the set of all values of r, for which the circles (x+1)2+(y+4)2=r2 and x2+y2−4x−2y−4=0

intersect at two distinct points be the interval α, β. Then αβ is equal to

[JEE Main 2026, 22 Jan (Shift 1)]

a

\(21\)

b

\(24\)

c

\(20\)

d

\(25\)

✓ Correct answer: d)

\(25\)

Explanation

Given circles:

(x−2)2+(y−1)2=32and  (x+1)2+(y+4)2=r2

Since the circles intersect each other at two different points.

So, r1−r2<c1c2<r1+r2

⇒r−3<(2+1)2+(1+4)2<r+3

⇒r−3<34  &  r+3>34

⇒−34<r−3<34  &  r>34−3

i.e. r=3−34,  3+34∩34−3,∞

i.e. r∈34−3,34+3

∴αβ=34−334+3

= 34 – 9=25
