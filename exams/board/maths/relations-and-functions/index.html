Q1

a

one-one but not onto

b

onto but not one-one

c

both one-one and onto

d

neither one-one nor onto

Explanation

Onto:

y=4x+3

x=y-34

Q4

The sum of absolute maximum and absolute minimum values of the function \(f(x)=\left|2 x^2+3 x-2\right|+\sin x \cos x\) in the interval [0,1] is:

[JEE Main 2022, 24 Jun (Shift 1)]

a

3+sin1cos2122

b

3+121+2cos1sin1

c

5+12sin1+sin2

d

2+sin12cos12

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

3+121+2cos1sin1

Explanation

\( f(x)=\left|2 x^2+3 x-2\right|+\sin x \cos x \)
\( =|(2 x-1)(x+2)|+\sin x \cos x \)
\( f(x)=\left\{\begin{array}{l}
-2 x^2-3 x+2+\sin x \cos x, \quad 0\leq x<\frac{1}{2} \\
2 x^2+3 x-2+\sin x \cos x, \quad \frac{1}{2} \leq x<1
\end{array}\right. \)
\( f^{\prime}(x)=\left\{\begin{array}{l}
-4 x-3+\cos 2 x, \quad 0\leq x<\frac{1}{2} \\
4 x+3+\cos 2 x, \quad \frac{1}{2} \leq x<1
\end{array}\right. \)

For \(0 \leq x<\frac{1}{2} \Rightarrow f^{\prime}(x)<0\)

\(\Rightarrow f(x)\) is decreasing

For \(\frac{1}{2}<x \leq 1 \Rightarrow f^{\prime}(x)>0\)

\(\Rightarrow f(x)\) is increasing

\(f(0)=2\)
\( f(1)=3+\sin 1 \cos 1 \text { and } f\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)=\sin \frac{1}{2} \cos \frac{1}{2}\)

Hence required sum

\( f(1)+f\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)=3+\frac{\sin 2}{2}+\frac{\sin 1}{2}=3+\frac{1}{2}(\sin 1+\sin 2) \)
\( =3+\frac{\sin 1}{2}(1+2 \cos 1)\)
