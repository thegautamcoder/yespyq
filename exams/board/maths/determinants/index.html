Q1

\(\left|\begin{array}{cc}\mathrm{x}+1 & \mathrm{x}-1 \\ \mathrm{x}^2+\mathrm{x}+1 & \mathrm{x}^2-\mathrm{x}+1\end{array}\right|\) is equal to :

a

\(2 x^3\)

b

2

c

0

d

\(2 x^3-2\)

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

2

Explanation

Given: x+1x-1x2+x+1x2-x+1

=x+1x2-x+1-x-1x2+x+1

\(=x^3-x^2+x+x^2-x+1-(x^3+x^2+x-x^2-x-1)\)

\(=\left(x^3+1\right)-\left(x^3-1\right)=2\)

Q3

If \(\left|\begin{array}{rrr}-a & b & c \\ a & -b & c \\ a & b & -c\end{array}\right|=k a b c\), then the value of \(k\) is :

a

0

b

1

c

2

d

4

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

4

Explanation

Let, Δ=-abca-bcab-c

Apply R1→R1+R2:

Δ=002ca-bcab-c

⇒Δ=2ca-bab

⇒Δ=2c(ab-(-b)a)=2c(2ab)=4abc

=kabc (given)

⇒k=4

Q5

22

Explanation

Q8

\(\left|\begin{array}{cc}\mathrm{x}+1 & \mathrm{x}-1 \\ \mathrm{x}^2+\mathrm{x}+1 & \mathrm{x}^2-\mathrm{x}+1\end{array}\right|\) is equal to :

a

\(2 x^3\)

b

2

c

0

d

\(2 x^3-2\)

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

2

Explanation

x+1x-1x2+x+1x2-x+1=(x+1)x2-x+1-(x-1)x2+x+1

\((x+1)\left(x^2-x+1\right)=x^3-x^2+x+x^2-x+1\)

=x3+1

\((x-1)\left(x^2+x+1\right)=x^3+x^2+x-x^2-x-1\)

=x3-1

\(\left(x^3+1\right)-\left(x^3-1\right)=2\)
