Q2 FREE PREVIEW

(i→+3j→-2k→)×(-i→+3k→)=

a

9i→-j→+3k→

b

9i→+j→-3k→

c

i→-j→+3k→

d

i→+j→-3k→

✓ Correct answer: a)

9i→-j→+3k→

Explanation

The given vectors are (i→+3j→-2k→) and (-i→+3k→).

(i→+3j→-2k→)×(-i→+3k→) represents the cross product of two vectors.

(i→+3j→-2k→)×(-i→+3k→)=i→j→k→13-2-103(i→+3j→-2k→)×(-i→+3k→)=i→3-203-j→1-2-13+k→13-10(i→+3j→-2k→)×(-i→+3k→)=i→(9-0)-j→(3-2)+k→(0+3)(i→+3j→-2k→)×(-i→+3k→)=9i→-j→+3k→

Hence, the value of (i→+3j→-2k→)×(-i→+3k→) is 9i→-j→+3k→.

Q3 FREE PREVIEW

Let \(\vec{p}\) and \(\vec{q}\) be two unit vectors and \(\alpha\) be the angle between them. Then \((\vec{p}+\vec{q})\) will be a unit vector for what value of \(\alpha\) ?

a

π4

b

π3

c

π2

d

2π3

✓ Correct answer: d)

2π3

Explanation

Given, p→+q→=p→=q→=1

Now,

p→+q→2=p→2+q→2+2p→·q→

⇒12=1+1+2cosα

⇒1=2+2cosα

⇒cosα=-12

⇒α=2π3
