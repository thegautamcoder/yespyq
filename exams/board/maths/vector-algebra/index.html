Q7

For any two vectors \(\vec{a}\) and \(\vec{b}\), which of the following statements is always true?

a

\(\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} \geq|\vec{a}||\vec{b}|\)

b

\(\quad \vec{a} \cdot \vec{b}=|\vec{a}||\vec{b}|\)

c

\(\overrightarrow{\mathrm{a}} \cdot \overrightarrow{\mathrm{b}} \leq|\overrightarrow{\mathrm{a}}||\overrightarrow{\mathrm{b}}|\)

d

\(\quad \vec{a} \cdot \vec{b}<|\vec{a}||\vec{b}|\)

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

\(\overrightarrow{\mathrm{a}} \cdot \overrightarrow{\mathrm{b}} \leq|\overrightarrow{\mathrm{a}}||\overrightarrow{\mathrm{b}}|\)

Explanation

\(\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b}=|\vec{a}||\vec{b}| \cos \theta\)

We know that: \(-1 \leq \cos \theta \leq 1\)

\(\Rightarrow -|\vec{a}||\vec{b}| \leq |\vec{a}||\vec{b}| \cos \theta \leq |\vec{a}||\vec{b}|\)

\(\Rightarrow-|\vec{a}||\vec{b}| \leq \vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} \leq|\vec{a}||\vec{b}|\)

\(\Rightarrow \vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} \leq|\vec{a}||\vec{b}|\)

Q11

The unit vector perpendicular to both vectors \(\hat{i}+\hat{k}\) and \(\hat{i}-\hat{k}\) is:

a

\(2 \hat{j}\)

b

\(\hat{j}\)

c

\(\frac{\hat{\mathrm{i}}-\hat{\mathrm{k}}}{\sqrt{2}}\)

d

\(\frac{\hat{\mathrm{i}}+\hat{\mathrm{k}}}{\sqrt{2}}\)

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

\(\hat{j}\)

Explanation

Let \(\vec{a}=\hat{i}+\hat{k}, \vec{b}=\hat{i}-\hat{k}\)

Now perpendicular vector \(\vec{c} = \vec{a} \times \vec{b}=(\hat{i}+\hat{k}) \times(\hat{i}-\hat{k})\)

\(=\left|\begin{array}{ccc}\hat{i} & \hat{j} & \hat{k} \\ 1 & 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 & -1\end{array}\right|=-\hat{j}(-1-1)=2 \hat{j}\)

Unit vector \(\hat{c}=\frac{\vec{a} \times \vec{b}}{|\vec{a} \times \vec{b}|}=\frac{2 \hat{j}}{2}=\hat{j}\)

Q17

Let \(\theta\) be the angle between two unit vectors \(\hat{\mathrm{a}}\) and \(\hat{\mathrm{b}} \operatorname{such}\) that \(\sin \theta=\frac{3}{5}\). Then, \(\hat{a} \cdot \hat{b}\) is equal to :

✓ Correct answer: c)

Explanation
