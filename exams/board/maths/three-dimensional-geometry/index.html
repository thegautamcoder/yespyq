Q1

Direction ratios of a vector parallel to line \(\frac{x-1}{2}=-y=\frac{2 z+1}{6}\) are :

a

\(2,-1,6\)

b

\(2,1,6\)

c

\(2,1,3\)

d

\(2,-1,3\)

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

\(2,-1,3\)

Explanation

Given equation of the line is

\( \frac{x-1}{2}=-y=\frac{2 z+1}{6} \)
\( \Rightarrow \frac{x-1}{2}=\frac{y-0}{-1}=\frac{2\left(z+\frac{1}{2}\right)}{6} \)
\( \Rightarrow \frac{x-1}{2}=\frac{y-0}{-1}=\frac{z+\frac{1}{2}}{3}\)

The direction ratios of the line are \((2,-1,3)\).

Q3

Assertion (A) : A line in space cannot be drawn perpendicular to \(\mathrm{x}, \mathrm{y}\) and \(z\) axes simultaneously.

Reason \((R)\) : For any line making angles, \(\alpha, \beta, \gamma\) with the positive directions of \(x, y\) and \(z\) axes respectively, then \(\cos ^2 \alpha+\cos ^2 \beta+\cos ^2 \gamma=1\).

a

Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A).

b

Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A).

c

Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false.

d

Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

✓ Correct answer: a)

Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A).

Explanation

Perpendicular to \(x, y, z \Rightarrow \alpha=\beta=\gamma=90^{\circ}\).

⇒cosα=cosβ=cosγ=0.

But for any line: \(\cos ^2 \alpha+\cos ^2 \beta+\cos ^2 \gamma=1\).
Here \(0+0+0 \neq 1 \Rightarrow\) impossible.
Thus Assertion is true.
Reason states the required identity and explains the impossibility.

Q7

The coordinates of the foot of the perpendicular drawn from the point \((0,1,2)\) on the \(x\)-axis are given by :

a

\((1,0,0)\)

b

\((2,0,0)\)

c

\((\sqrt{5}, 0,0)\)

d

\((0,0,0)\)

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

\((0,0,0)\)

Explanation

Vector equation for \(x\)-axis is \(\vec{r}=\lambda \hat{i}\) where \(x=\lambda\).

Let a point \(P(\lambda, 0,0)\) is on the \(x\)-axis, foot of perpendicular to the given point \(A(0,1,2)\).

then \(\overrightarrow{A P}\) is perpendicular to \(\lambda \hat{i}\).

\( \overrightarrow{A P} \cdot \lambda \hat{i}=0 \) where \( \overrightarrow{A P}=\lambda \hat{i}-\hat{j}-2 \hat{k} \)
\( (\lambda \hat{i}-\hat{j}-2 \hat{k}) \cdot(\lambda \hat{i}+0 \hat{j}+0 \hat{k})=0 \)
\(\Rightarrow \lambda^2=0\)

\(\Rightarrow \lambda=0\),

So the foot of the perpendicular drawn from the point \((0,1,2)\) is \((0,0,0)\).

Q8

If a line makes an angle of \(30^{\circ}\) with the positive direction of \(x\)-axis, \(120^{\circ}\) with the positive direction of \(y\)-axis, then the angle which it makes with the positive direction of \(z\)-axis is :

a

\(90^{\circ}\)

b

\(120^{\circ}\)

c

\(60^{\circ}\)

d

\(0^{\circ}\)

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

\(90^{\circ}\)

Explanation

Given, \(\alpha=30^{\circ}\), \(\beta=120^{\circ}\)

So, \(l=\cos \alpha=\cos 30^{\circ}=\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\)

\(m=\cos \beta=\cos 120^{\circ}=-\frac{1}{2}\)

Since, \(l^2+m^2+n^2=1\)

Therefore, \(\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)^2+\left(-\frac{1}{2}\right)^2+n^2=1\)

\(\Rightarrow \frac{3}{4}+\frac{1}{4}+n^2=1 \Rightarrow n^2=0 \Rightarrow n=0\)

\(\Rightarrow \cos \gamma=n=0 \Rightarrow \gamma=90^{\circ}\)
