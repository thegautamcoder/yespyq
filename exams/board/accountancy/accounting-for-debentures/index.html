Q1 FREE PREVIEW

Debenture is the part of :

a

Share Capital

b

Long term Borrowing

c

Owned Capital

d

None of these

✓ Correct answer: b)

Long term Borrowing

Explanation

Correct Answer:

(B) Long-term Borrowings

Why This Answer Is Correct?

Debentures represent long-term loans taken by the company. Debenture holders are creditors, not owners. Therefore, debentures are shown under Long-term Borrowings on the liabilities side of the Balance Sheet.

Why Other Options Are Incorrect?

(A) Share Capital — Incorrect
Share capital belongs to shareholders (owners), not to debenture holders.

(C) Owners’ Capital — Incorrect
Owners’ capital represents shareholders’ funds, whereas debentures are borrowed funds.

(D) None of these — Incorrect
Since debentures are part of long-term borrowings, this option is wrong.

Q2 FREE PREVIEW

Rate of Interest on debenture is :-

a

12% p.a.

b

20 % p.a.

c

Fixed rate

d

15 % p.a.

✓ Correct answer: c)

Fixed rate

Explanation

Correct Answer:

(C) Fixed rate

Why This Answer Is Correct?

Debentures carry a fixed rate of interest which is decided at the time of issue and remains the same throughout the life of the debentures, regardless of the company’s profit or loss.

Why Other Options Are Incorrect?

(A) 12% per annum — Incorrect
This is only an example of a rate. Debentures can be issued at different rates, not always at 12%.

(B) 20% per annum — Incorrect
This is also just a specific rate, not a general characteristic of debentures.

(D) 15% per annum — Incorrect
Like the other percentages, this is not universally applicable to all debentures.
