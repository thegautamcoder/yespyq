Q1 FREE PREVIEW

Which Sufi teacher was also known as the 'Gharib Nawaz'?"

a

Shaikh Nizamuddin Auliya

b

Khwaja Muinuddin

c

Amir Khusrau

d

Shaikh Qutubuddin Bakhtiyar Kaki

✓ Correct answer: b)

Khwaja Muinuddin

Explanation

Answer: (B) Khwaja Muinuddin

Explanation: Khwaja Muinuddin Chishti, the founder of the Chishti Order in India, is known as 'Gharib Nawaz,' which means 'Benefactor of the Poor.' He is revered for his teachings and service to the underprivileged.
