Q1 FREE PREVIEW

Match List-I with List-II:

List-I List-II
(A) Duarte Barbosa (I) Morocco
(B) Marco Polo (II) Spain
(C) Ibn Battuta (III) Portugal
(D) Antonio Monserrate (IV) Italy

Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

a

(A) - (I), (B) - (II), (C) - (IV), (D) - (III)

b

(A) - (III), (B) - (IV), (C) - (I), (D) - (II)

c

(A) - (II), (B) - (III), (C) - (IV), (D) - (I)

d

(A) - (IV), (B) - (I), (C) - (II), (D) - (III)

✓ Correct answer: b)

(A) - (III), (B) - (IV), (C) - (I), (D) - (II)

Explanation

Answer:
The correct answer is B (A) - (III), (B) - (IV), (C) - (I), (D) - (II).

Duarte Barbosa - Portugal.
Marco Polo - Italy.
Ibn Battuta - Morocco.
Antonio Monserrate - Spain.

Explanation:
  1. Duarte Barbosa: A Portuguese writer and explorer associated with the early explorations of Portugal.
  2. Marco Polo: A Venetian traveler from Italy, famous for his travels to Asia and his book detailing them.
  3. Ibn Battuta: A Moroccan scholar and traveler who journeyed extensively across the Islamic world and beyond.
  4. Antonio Monserrate: A Spanish Jesuit missionary known for his travels to India and accompanying Akbar on expeditions.
