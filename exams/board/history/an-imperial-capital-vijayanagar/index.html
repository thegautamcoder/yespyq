Q1 FREE PREVIEW

Which one of the following pairs is correctly matched?

Travellers of Medieval period — Native Region

a

Peter Mundy — Spain

b

Antonio Monserrate — Portugal

c

Abdal-Razzak — Morocco

d

Marco Polo — Italy

✓ Correct answer: d)

Marco Polo — Italy

Explanation

The correctly matched pair is:

(D) Marco Polo — Italy

Explanation:
Peter Mundy was from England, not Spain.
Antonio Monserrate was from Spain, not Portugal.
Abd al-Razzaq Samarqandi was from Persia (Iran), not Morocco.
Marco Polo was indeed from Italy.

Correct Answer: (D) Marco Polo — Italy
