Q1 FREE PREVIEW

The “Era of One-Party Dominance” in India refers to the period from :

a

1952 to1962

b

1977 to1984

c

1989 to 1996

d

1998 to 2004

✓ Correct answer: a)

1952 to1962

Explanation

The “Era of One-Party Dominance” in India refers to the period when the Indian National Congress dominated national and state politics after independence.

This began with the first general elections in 1952 and continued through the second (1957) and third (1962) general elections.

During this time, the Congress party had overwhelming majorities in Parliament and ruled most states.

Other options:
B) 1977 to 1984 – Period of political instability and rise of Janata Party, not one-party dominance.

C) 1989 to 1996 – Marked the beginning of coalition politics.

D) 1998 to 2004 – Era of NDA coalition government under BJP, not one-party dominance.
