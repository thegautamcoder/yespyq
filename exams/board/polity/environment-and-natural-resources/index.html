Q1 FREE PREVIEW

Match List-I with List-II and choose the correct option:

List-I List-II
The Energy Conservation Act, India (i) 1975
The Electricity Act, India (ii) 1992
Earth Summit (iii) 2001
World Council of Indigenous Peoples (iv) 2003

a

1-(iv), 2-(i), 3-(iii), 4-(ii)

b

1-(iii), 2-(iv), 3-(ii), 4-(i)

c

1-(ii), 2-(iii), 3-(i), 4-(iv)

d

1-(i), 2-(ii), 3-(iv), 4-(iii)

✓ Correct answer: b)

1-(iii), 2-(iv), 3-(ii), 4-(i)

Explanation

Answer: (B) 1-(iii), 2-(iv), 3-(ii), 4-(i)

The Energy Conservation Act, India (2001)

Enacted by the Government of India to promote efficient use of energy and reduce wastage.

Led to the establishment of the Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE).
→ Matches with (iii) 2001.

The Electricity Act, India (2003)

A comprehensive law replacing older electricity laws.

Aimed at improving electricity generation, transmission, and distribution in India.
→ Matches with (iv) 2003.

Earth Summit (1992)

Officially called the United Nations Conference on Environment and Development (UNCED).

Held in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil.

Focused on sustainable development and climate change.
→ Matches with (ii) 1992.

World Council of Indigenous Peoples (1975)

An international NGO established to represent indigenous people’s rights and issues globally.

First conference was held in Port Alberni, Canada.
→ Matches with (i) 1975.

Correct Match: 1-(iii), 2-(iv), 3-(ii), 4-(i)
Answer: (B)
