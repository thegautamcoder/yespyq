Q1 FREE PREVIEW

Choose the correct one-word substitution of:

"Ceremony of crowning a king"

a

Coquette

b

Demagogue

c

Coronation

d

Deprecate

✓ Correct answer: c)

Coronation

Explanation

The correct answer is C. Coronation

Coronation means the ceremony of crowning a king or queen. It is a formal event where the ruler is officially given the crown and authority.

Explanation for other options:

  • A. Coquette – A woman who flirts to attract attention.
  • B. Demagogue – A leader who gains support by appealing to emotions, often in a manipulative way.
  • D. Deprecate – To express disapproval or criticise something.
