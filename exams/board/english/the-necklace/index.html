Q1 FREE PREVIEW

Choose the correct option to answer the following questions :

Which of the following statements is correct ?

a

Matilda borrowed a necklace of real diamonds, but returned a necklace of artificial diamonds

b

Matilda borrowed a necklace of real diamonds and returned a necklace of real diamonds

c

Matilda borrowed a necklace of artificial diamonds and returned a necklace of artificial diamonds

d

Matilda borrowed a necklace of artificial diamonds, but returned a necklace of real diamonds

✓ Correct answer: d)

Matilda borrowed a necklace of artificial diamonds, but returned a necklace of real diamonds

Explanation

Option D

Matilda borrowed a necklace of artificial diamonds, but returned a necklace of real diamonds.

Explanation:
Matilda borrowed a necklace that she thought was real, lost it, and then replaced it with a real diamond necklace. Later, she discovered the original was artificial.
