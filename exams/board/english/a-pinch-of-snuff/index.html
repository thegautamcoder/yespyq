Q1 FREE PREVIEW

Nanukaka still wore

a

a knee length black coat

b

Aligarhi pajamas

c

a Jodhpur coat

d

an Aligarhi kurta

✓ Correct answer: c)

a Jodhpur coat

Explanation

Correct answer: C) a Jodhpur coat

Explanation:

  • In the story “The Minister’s Black Veil” (from The Minister’s Black Veil by R. K. Narayan, also known as “The Minister’s Black Veil / The Minister’s Black Coat” or The Minister’s Black Coat), Nanukaka still wore a Jodhpur coat, reflecting his traditional style and old-fashioned personality.
