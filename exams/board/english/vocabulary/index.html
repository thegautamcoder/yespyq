Q2 FREE PREVIEW

The meaning of 'Compulsory.'

a

Aphoristic

b

Religious

c

Obligatory

d

Permissible

✓ Correct answer: c)

Obligatory

Explanation

Explanation: 'Obligatory' means something required by rule or duty — compulsory. The passage uses it in 'Desirelessness is obligatory,' meaning it is a must.

(1) 'Aphoristic' means expressed in concise principles — unrelated to compulsory.

(2) 'Religious' relates to faith and worship — not compulsory.

(4) 'Permissible' means allowed, which is nearly the opposite of compulsory.

Q7 FREE PREVIEW

Choose the correct meaning of - "To cry wolf" :

a

To listen eagerly

b

To turn pale

c

To be hungry

d

To give a false alarm

✓ Correct answer: d)

To give a false alarm

Explanation

Correct Answer: (D) To give a false alarm
Explanation:
- "To cry wolf" means to raise a false alarm or to warn people about a danger that does not exist.
- This idiom comes from the famous fable "The Boy Who Cried Wolf."
