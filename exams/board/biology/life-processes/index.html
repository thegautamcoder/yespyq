✓ Correct answer: b)

Explanation

Correct Answer: B — Liver
Explanation: Bile juice is produced by the liver. It helps in the digestion of fats and is stored in the gall bladder.

Q2 FREE PREVIEW

Which type of reaction is respiration?

a

Combination

b

Reduction

c

Endothermic

d

Oxidation

✓ Correct answer: d)

Oxidation

Explanation

Respiration is a biochemical process in which organisms release energy byu breaking down of complex molecules. It is a type of Oxidation reaction. Oxidation because glucose is broken down and energy is released during the process.

While other options are:

Combination reaction: When two or more reactants reacts to form a single product.

Reduction: When electrons are gained then reduction takes places.

Endothermic Reaction: Reaction which absorbs heat from surrounding.
