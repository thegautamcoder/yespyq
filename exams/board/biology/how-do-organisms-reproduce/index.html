Q1 FREE PREVIEW

Buds on leaf develop in :

a

Mint

b

Potato

c

Bryophyllum

d

All of the above

✓ Correct answer: c)

Bryophyllum

Explanation

Correct answer: (C) Bryophyllum

Explanation:
In Bryophyllum, adventitious buds develop on the margins of leaves. These buds grow into new plants when they fall on the soil. Mint reproduces by stem (runner) and potato by tuber, not by leaf buds.
