Q1 FREE PREVIEW

In a large sized enterprise delegation is

a

Voluntary

b

Necessary

c

Compulsory

d

None of these

✓ Correct answer: c)

Compulsory

Explanation

Correct Answer: (C) Compulsory

Key Information:

In large-sized enterprises, the volume of work is very high and the top management alone cannot control all tasks and take all decisions. Therefore, the delegation of work and authority becomes compulsory. Delegation distributes work across different managerial levels, which speeds up both decision-making and execution.

When authority remains concentrated only at the top level, even small decisions require approval, which wastes time and delays the work. However, in large enterprises where operations are wide and complex, the organisation cannot function efficiently without delegation.

Hence, delegation is not considered an option, but a necessity in large organisations. Thus, delegation becomes essential for smooth functioning, proper work distribution, clear accountability, and timely execution in large enterprises.

Additional Information

(A) Voluntary — Incorrect
Voluntary means that delegation depends only on the manager’s preference. In large enterprises, delegation is not a matter of personal choice; it is a functional requirement. Without delegation, proper management cannot take place. Therefore, this option is incorrect.

(B) Necessary — Incorrect
Although delegation is indeed necessary in large enterprises, the word “necessary” does not fully convey the situation. In reality, a large-sized enterprise cannot operate effectively without delegation, which makes it compulsory rather than merely necessary. Therefore, this option is partially true but considered incorrect for examination purposes.

Q2 FREE PREVIEW

Match List-I with List-II on the basis of meaning and the term :

LIST I LIST II
A. Right to command I. Answerability
B.Obligation to perform task II. Delegation
C.Accountability III. Authority
D.Basis of management hierarchy IV. Responsibility

Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

a

(A) - (I), (B) - (II), (C) - (III), (D) - (IV)

b

(A) - (I), (B) - (III), (C) - (II), (D) - (IV)

c

(A) - (I), (B) - (II), (C) - (IV), (D) - (III)

d

(A) - (III), (B) - (IV), (C) - (I), (D) - (II)

✓ Correct answer: d)

(A) - (III), (B) - (IV), (C) - (I), (D) - (II)

Explanation

(A) - (III), (B) - (IV), (C) - (I), (D) - (II) is the correct match.

Right to command is possible when an individual has authority to do so, responsibility is the obligation to perform the assigned task, accountability means answerability & basis of management hierarchy is characterised through delegation.
