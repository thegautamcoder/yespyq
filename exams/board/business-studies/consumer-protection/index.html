Q1 FREE PREVIEW

When did Consumer Protection Act come into effect?

a

2018

b

2019

c

2020

d

None of these

✓ Correct answer: c)

2020

Explanation

The Consumer Protection Act, 2019 became effective on 20 July 2020. Though it was enacted and received Presidential assent in 2019, it was brought into force in 2020, replacing the Consumer Protection Act, 1986.
