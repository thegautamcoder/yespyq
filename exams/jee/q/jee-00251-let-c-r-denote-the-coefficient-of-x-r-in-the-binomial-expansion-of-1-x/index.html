Let \(C_r\) denote the coefficient of \(x^r\) in the binomial expansion of \((1+{x}^{n}),\text{  }n\in N,\text{  }0\leq r\leq n.\)

If \({P}_{n}={C}_{0}−{C}_{1}+\frac{{2}^{2}}{3}{C}_{2}−\frac{{2}^{3}}{4}{C}_{3}+...+\frac{{(−2)}^{n}}{n+1}{C}_{n},\) then the value of \(\sum _{n=1}^{25}\frac{1}{{P}_{2n}}\) equals

[JEE Main 2026, 22 Jan (Shift 2)]

a

\(675\)

b

\(650\)

c

\(580\)

d

\(525\)

✓ Correct answer: a)

\(675\)

Explanation

\({P}_{n}={\sum }_{r=0}^{n}\frac{{}^{n}{C}_{r}{(−2)}^{r}}{r+1}\)

\(={\sum }_{r=0}^{n}\frac{1}{\left(n+1\right)}{}^{n+1}{C}_{r+1}{(−2)}^{r}\)

\(=\frac{−1}{2\left(n+1\right)}{\sum }_{r=0}^{n}{}^{n+1}{C}_{r+1}{(−2)}^{r+1}\)

\(=\frac{−1}{2\left(\text{n}+1\right)}\left[{(1−2)}^{\text{n}+1}−1\right]\)

\({\text{⇒P}}_{\text{n}}=\frac{1}{2\left(\text{n}+1\right)}\left[1−{(−1)}^{\text{n}+1}\right]\)

\({P}_{2n}=\frac{1}{2\left(2n+1\right)}\left[1−{(−1)}^{2n+1}\right]\)

\({P}_{2n}=\frac{1}{2n+1}\)

\(\sum_{n=1}^{25} \frac{1}{P_{2 n}}=\sum_{n=1}^{25}(2 n+1)=3+5+\ldots . .+51=\frac{25}{2}[51+3]=25 \times 27=675\)
