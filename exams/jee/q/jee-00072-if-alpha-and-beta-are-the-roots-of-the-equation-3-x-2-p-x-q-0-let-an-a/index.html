If \(\alpha\) and \(\beta\) are the roots of the equation \(3 x^2-p x+q=0\) Let an A.P whose common diff. is \(\frac{3}{2}\) and \(10^{\text {th }}\) term is \(\alpha\) and \(11^{\text {th }}\) term is \(\beta\) and \(S_{11}=88\). Find the value of \(q-2 p\).

a

400

b

450

c

474

d

350

✓ Correct answer: c)

474

Explanation

\(\begin{array}{rlrl}
d=3 / 2 & a_{10}=\alpha =a+9 d \\
a_{11} & =\beta=a+10 d \\
3_{11}=\frac{11}{2}(2 a+10 d) & =88 \\
(a+5 d) & =8 \\
a+\frac{15}{2} & =8 \Rightarrow a=1 / 2
\end{array}\)

\(\begin{gathered}
\alpha=\frac{1}{2}+9 \times \frac{3}{2}=14 \\
\beta=\frac{1}{2}+10 \times \frac{3}{2}=\frac{31}{2} \\
3 x^2-p x+q=0 \\
\alpha+\beta=\frac{p}{3} \\
\frac{14+81}{2}=\frac{p}{3} \Rightarrow \frac{28+31}{2}=\frac{p}{3} \\
\frac{59}{2}=\frac{p}{3} \Rightarrow p=\frac{59 \times 3}{2}=\frac{177}{2}
\end{gathered}\)

\(\begin{aligned}
& \alpha \beta=q / 3 \\
& 14 \times \frac{31}{2}=\frac{q}{3} \\
& 217 \times 3=q \\
& q=651 \\
& q-2 p \\
& 651-2\left(\frac{177}{2}\right)=651-177=474
\end{aligned}\)
