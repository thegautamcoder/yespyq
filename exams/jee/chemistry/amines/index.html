Q4

An amine \((X)\) is prepared by ammonolysis of benzyl chloride. On adding p-toluenesulphonyl chloride to it the solution remains clear. Molar mass of the amine \((X)\) formed is ................\(g mol ^{-1}\).
(Given molar mass in \(gmol ^{-1} C : 12, H : 1, O : 16, N : 14\) )

[JEE Main 2024, 6 Apr (Shift 2)]

a

108

b

287

c

330

d

111

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

287

Explanation

Ammonolysis of benzyl chloride can give primary, secondary, and tertiary amines.

In the Hinsberg test:

Primary amine → forms soluble sulphonamide (solution clear in base)
Secondary amine → forms insoluble product (solution turbid)
Tertiary amine → does not react and remains insoluble

Here, the statement says solution remains clear, which in this context indicates tertiary amine (no reaction, remains dissolved).

Thus, X = tribenzylamine = (C₆H₅CH₂)₃N

C₆H₅CH₂ = C₇H₇

So, (C₇H₇)₃N = C₂₁H₂₁N

Molar mass = 21×12 + 21×1 + 14
= 252 + 21 + 14
= 287 g/mol

Q5

Given below are two statements

Statement I: Aniline reacts with conc. \(\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{SO}_4\), followed by heating at 453 - 473 K gives p-aminobenzene sulphonic acid, which gives blood red colour in the 'Lassaigne's test'.
Statement II: In Friedel - Craft's alkylation and acylation reactions, aniline forms salt with the \(\mathrm{AlCl}_3\) catalyst. Due to this, nitrogen of aniline acquires a positive charge and acts as deactivating group.
In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below:

[JEE Main 2024, 31 Jan (Shift 2)]

a

Both statement I and statement II are false

b

Statement I is true but statement II is false

c

Statement I is false but statement II is true

d

Both statement I and statement II are true

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

Both statement I and statement II are true

Explanation

Statement I:

Aniline reacts with conc. H₂SO₄ and on heating forms p-aminobenzenesulphonic acid — this part is correct.

However, this compound does not give blood red colour in Lassaigne’s test.
Lassaigne’s test is for detection of nitrogen, sulphur, and halogens, and blood red colour is associated with Fe³⁺–thiocyanate complex, not this compound.

So, Statement I is false.

Statement II:

Aniline reacts with AlCl₃ (Lewis acid) forming a complex/salt.
This gives positive charge on nitrogen, reducing electron density on ring, making it deactivating.

Thus, Friedel–Crafts reactions do not occur with aniline.

So, Statement II is true.

Q8

Which of the following will give azo dye test?

Memory Based Question 29/Jan/25 Evening shift

a

Aniline

b

Anisole

c

Benzene

d

Benzaldehyde

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

Aniline

Explanation

The azo dye test determines whether an organic compound contains a primary aromatic amine or not.In this test, a primary aromatic amine reacts with nitrous acid, which is generated in situ by the reaction of sodium nitrite with hydrochloric acid (HCl), at 0–5°C to produce a diazonium salt. The diazonium salt couples with 2-naphthol (also called beta naphthol) to make a scarlet red dye, which is slightly soluble in water.

Among the given options, only Aniline will show an azo dye test.

Q16

4-nitrotoluene is treated with Br2 to get compound P which is reduced with Sn and HCl to get Compound Q, then Q is diazotized and the product is treated with phosphinic acid to get R is oxidized with alkaline KMnO4 to get the final product

(JEE mains memory based shift-1 23/01/2025)

a

2-Bromo-4-hydroxy Benzoic acid

b

Benzoic acid

c

4-Bromo Benzoic acid

d

2-Bromo Benzoic acid

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

2-Bromo Benzoic acid

Explanation

1.4-Nitrotoluene + Br₂: Bromination at the meta position forms 2-Bromo-4-nitrotoluene (Compound P).

2.P + Sn/HCl: Reduction of the nitro group forms 2-Bromo-4-aminotoluene (Compound Q).

3.Q + NaNO₂/HCl: Diazotization forms a diazonium salt.

4.Diazonium salt + H₃PO₄: Forms 2-Bromo-4-hydroxybenzene (Compound R).

5.R + KMnO₄ (alkaline): Oxidation forms 2-Bromo-4-carboxybenzoic acid (final product).
