Statement (II) : s-block elements can be found in pure form in nature.

[JEE Main 2024, 27 Jan (Shift 1)]

b

Statement I is true but Statement II is false

Explanation

Lanthanides (4f) and Actinides (5f) are placed separately to keep the periodic table compact and to preserve its periodicity and group structure.

  • Most s-block elements (like Na, K, Ca) are highly reactive and do not occur in pure form in nature.

  • They are usually found as compounds (e.g., NaCl, K₂CO₃).

  • Only a few like beryllium (Be) or magnesium (Mg) may exist in less reactive or near-pure forms, but not commonly.
