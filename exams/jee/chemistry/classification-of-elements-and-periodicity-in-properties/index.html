Q26

In case of isoelectronic species the size of \(F ^{-}, Ne\) and \(Na ^{+}\)is affected by :

[JEE Main 2024, 1 Feb (Shift 1)]

a

Principal quantum number (n)

b

Ionic radius

c

Electron-electron interaction in the outer orbitals

d

Nuclear charge (z)

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

Nuclear charge (z)

Explanation

For the isoelectronic species F⁻, Ne, Na⁺, all have 10 electrons.

So, their size cannot depend on:

  • (A) Principal quantum number (n) → same n

  • (B) Ionic radius → this is what we are trying to compare, not the cause

  • (C) Electron–electron interaction → same number of electrons → nearly same e–e repulsion

The only factor that changes is:

✔ Nuclear charge (Z)

More nuclear charge pulls electrons inward → smaller radius.

Order of Z:
F (Z=9) < Ne (Z=10) < Na (Z=11)

Thus radius order:
F⁻ > Ne > Na⁺

Which matches the observed trend.

✅ Correct Answer: D — Nuclear charge (Z)
