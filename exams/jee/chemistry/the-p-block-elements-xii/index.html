The species formed on fluorination of phosphorus pentachloride in a polar organic solvent are

a

[PF4]+ [PF6]– and [PCl4]+ [PF6]–

b

[PCl4]+ [PCl4F2]– and [PCl4]+ [PF6]–

c

\({\mathrm{PF}}_{3}\) and \({\mathrm{PCl}}_{3}\)

✓ Correct answer: b)

[PCl4]+ [PCl4F2]– and [PCl4]+ [PF6]–

Explanation

In a polar solvent, PCl5 undergoes auto-ionization to form ionic species [PCl4]+ [PCl6]–.
