Q1 FREE PREVIEW

Identify X, Y and Z in the following reaction. (Equation not balanced)

\(\mathrm{Cl}\overset{˙}{\mathrm{O}}+{\mathrm{NO}}_{2}\to \underset{¯}{\mathrm{X}}\overset{{\mathrm{H}}_{2}\mathrm{O}}{\to }\underset{¯}{\mathrm{Y}}+\underset{¯}{\mathrm{Z}}\)

[JEE Main 2023, 31 Jan (Shift 1)]

a

\(\mathrm{X}={\mathrm{ClONO}}_{2},\mathrm{Y}=\mathrm{HOCl},\mathrm{Z}={\mathrm{NO}}_{2}\)

b

\(\mathrm{X}={\mathrm{ClNO}}_{2},\mathrm{Y}=\mathrm{HCl},\mathrm{Z}={\mathrm{HNO}}_{3}\)

c

\(\mathrm{X}={\mathrm{ClONO}}_{2},\mathrm{Y}=\mathrm{HOCl},\mathrm{Z}={\mathrm{HNO}}_{3}\)

d

\(\mathrm{X}={\mathrm{ClNO}}_{3},\mathrm{Y}={\mathrm{Cl}}_{2},\mathrm{Z}={\mathrm{NO}}_{2}\)

✓ Correct answer: c)

\(\mathrm{X}={\mathrm{ClONO}}_{2},\mathrm{Y}=\mathrm{HOCl},\mathrm{Z}={\mathrm{HNO}}_{3}\)

Explanation

(c) The reaction occurs as:

\(\mathrm{Cl}\overset{˙}{\mathrm{O}}+{\mathrm{NO}}_{2}⟶\underset{(\mathrm{X})}{\mathrm{ClO}}\overset{+{\mathrm{H}}_{2}\mathrm{O}}{\to }\underset{(\mathrm{Y})}{\mathrm{HOCl}}+\underset{(\mathrm{Z})}{{\mathrm{HNO}}_{3}}\)

Q2 FREE PREVIEW

Dinitrogen, a stable compound, reacts at high altitudes to form oxides. Which nitrogen oxide can damage plant leaves andhinder photosynthesis

a

\(\mathrm{NO}\)

b

\({\mathrm{NO}}_{3}^{-}\)

c

\({\mathrm{NO}}_{2}\)

d

\({\mathrm{NO}}_{2}^{-}\)

✓ Correct answer: c)

\({\mathrm{NO}}_{2}\)

Explanation

Nitrogen dioxide (NO2) is a toxic gas that reacts with water in plant cells, forming acids that damage leaves and disrupt photosynthesis.

Q3 FREE PREVIEW

[JEE Main 2023, 10 Apr (Shift 2)]

a

Burning of Coal

b

Deforestation

c

Burning of petroleum

d

Respiration

Respiration

Explanation

Respiration, is a natural process. So, balance of \({\mathrm{CO}}_{2}\) and \({\mathrm{O}}_{2}\) is not disturbed by respiration. While other processes disturb this balance.
