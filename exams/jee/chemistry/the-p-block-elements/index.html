Q10

Given below are two statements

Statement I : The electronegativity of group 14 elements from \(\mathrm{Si}\) to \(\mathrm{Pb}\), gradually decreases.

Statement II : Group 14 contains non-metallic, metallic, as well as metalloid elements.

In the light of the above statements, choose the most appropriate answer from the options given below.

[JEE Main 2024, 29 Jan (Shift 1)]

a

Statement I is false but Statement II is true

b

Both Statement I and Statement II are false

c

Statement I is true but Statement II is false

d

Both Statement I and Statement II are true

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

Statement I is false but Statement II is true

Explanation

Statement I is false because electronegativity in group 14 does not decrease regularly from Si to Pb; there is an irregular trend due to poor shielding of d and f electrons. Statement II is true because group 14 contains non-metals (C), metalloids (Si, Ge), and metals (Sn, Pb). Hence, the correct answer is that Statement I is false but Statement II is true, making option A correct.

Statement II : AlCl3 upon hydrolysis in acidified aqueous solution forms octahedral AlH2O63+ ion.

[JEE Main 2024, 31 Jan (Shift 2)]

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

Both statement I and statement II are true

Explanation

Statement I is true: Group 13 halides are largely covalent, so they undergo hydrolysis easily in water. Due to electron deficiency, these compounds readily react with water molecules.

Statement II is also true: AlCl₃ in aqueous acidic medium forms the octahedral complex [Al(H₂O)₆]³⁺. Aluminium accepts lone pairs from six water molecules, completing its coordination.

Therefore, both statements are correct, and the correct option is that both I and II are true.

Explanation

Assertion is true: Boron has an unusually high melting point in group 13. Reason is also true: solid boron forms a very strong crystalline lattice. The correct statements from the following are

(A) The decreasing order of atomic radii of group 13 elements is Tl>In>Ga>Al>B
(B) Down the group 13 electronegativity decreases from top to bottom.
(C) Al dissolves in dil. HCl and liberates H2 but conc. HNO3 renders Al passive by forming a protective oxide layer on the surface.
(D) All elements of group 13 exhibits highly stable + 1 oxidation state.
(E) Hybridisation of Al in Al(H2O)63+ ion is sp3d2.

Choose the correct answer from the options given below

[JEE Main 2024, 5 Apr (Shift 2)]

a

(C) and (E) only

b

(A) and (C) only

c

(A), (C) and (E) only

d

(A), (B), (C) and (E) only

✓ Correct answer: a)

(C) and (E) only

Explanation

(A) Given: Tl > In > Ga > Al > B

Actual trend:

Size generally increases down the group
But Ga is smaller than Al due to d-block contraction

Correct order:
Tl > In > Al > Ga > B

So (A) is incorrect

(B) Electronegativity trend

General trend: decreases down the group

But in group 13:

Al → Ga → slight increase due to poor shielding of d-electrons

So trend is irregular, not strictly decreasing

Hence (B) is incorrect

(C) Reaction of Al
With dilute HCl → Al dissolves and releases H₂
With concentrated HNO₃ → forms oxide layer → becomes passive

So (C) is correct

(D) Stability of +1 oxidation state
+1 becomes stable only down the group (In, Tl)
Not for all elements

So (D) is incorrect

(E) Hybridisation of Al in [Al(H₂O)₆]³⁺
Coordination number = 6
Geometry = octahedral
Hybridisation = sp³d²

So (E) is correct

Q17

Given below are two statements:

Statement I : In group 13, the stability of +1 oxidation state increases down the group.

Statement II : The atomic size of gallium is greater than that of aluminium.

In the light of the above statements, choose the most appropriate answer from the options given below :

[JEE Main 2024, 05 Apr (Shift 1)]

a

Statement I is correct but Statement II is incorrect.

b

Statement I is incorrect but Statement II is correct.

c

Both Statement I and Statement II are correct.

d

Both Statement I and Statement II are incorrect.

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

Statement I is correct but Statement II is incorrect.

Explanation

Group 13 elements:
B, Al, Ga, In, Tl

Electronic configuration:
ns² np¹

As we move down the group:

The ns² electrons become more tightly held
Due to poor shielding by d and f orbitals
These electrons do not participate in bonding easily

Thus:

+3 oxidation state → involves ns² + np¹
+1 oxidation state → involves only np¹

Down the group:

ns² pair becomes inert (inactive)

So +1 oxidation state becomes more stable

Example:

Al → +3 stable
Ga → +3 dominant
In → +1 starts appearing
Tl → +1 most stable

Statement II: Incorrect
Expected trend:

Atomic size should increase down the group

So ideally: Ga > Al

But actual observation: Ga ≈ Al (slightly smaller or similar)

Reason: d-block contraction
Ga has 3d¹⁰ electrons
These electrons do not shield effectively

Result: Nuclear charge increases, Electrons are pulled closer, Atomic size decreases

Thus: Ga is not larger than Al

Statement I → Correct
Statement II → Incorrect

Statement - 2: First ionization energy Pb is greater than Sn

Answer & explanation — Premium

✓ Correct answer: d)

Explanation
