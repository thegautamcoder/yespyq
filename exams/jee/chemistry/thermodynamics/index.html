Energy supplied to 1 mole of monoatomic gas is 48 J and changes its temperature by 2°C. Find the work done by gas.

a

23 J

b

28 J

c

46 J

d

20 J

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

23 J

Explanation

  • Energy supplied (heat), Q=48 JQ = 48 \, \text{J}Q=48J

  • Gas: monoatomic, n=1 moln = 1 \, \text{mol}n=1mol

  • Temperature change: \Delta T = 2\,^\circ \text{C} = 2 \, \text{K}

We are asked to find work done, W.

Step 1: Recall relations

For any process:

Q=ΔE+WQ = \Delta E + WQ=ΔE+W

Where:

  • ΔE=nCVΔT\Delta E = n C_V \Delta TΔE=nCV​ΔT (for a monoatomic ideal gas, CV=32RC_V = \frac{3}{2} RCV​=23​R)

  • W=W =W= work done by gas

Step 2: Compute change in internal energy

  • R=8.314 J/mol\cdotpKR = 8.314 \, \text{J/mol·K}R=8.314J/mol\cdotpK

CV=32R=32⋅8.314≈12.471 J/mol\cdotpKC_V = \frac{3}{2} R = \frac{3}{2} \cdot 8.314 \approx 12.471 \, \text{J/mol·K}CV​=23​R=23​⋅8.314≈12.471J/mol\cdotpK ΔE=nCVΔT=1⋅12.471⋅2≈24.942 J≈25 J\Delta E = n C_V \Delta T = 1 \cdot 12.471 \cdot 2 \approx 24.942 \, \text{J} \approx 25 \, \text{J}ΔE=nCV​ΔT=1⋅12.471⋅2≈24.942J≈25J

Step 3: Compute work done

W=Q−ΔE=48−24.942≈23 JW = Q - \Delta E = 48 - 24.942 \approx 23 \, \text{J}W=Q−ΔE=48−24.942≈23J

✅ Answer: 23 J (Option A)

ΔHfθH2O= Bond enthalpies of reactants - Bond enthalpies of products

-242.0=D(H-H)+12D(O=O)-2D(O-H)

Substituting Known Values

 -242.0=440+12(500)-2D(O-H) -242.0=440+250-2D(O-H) -242.0=690-2D(O-H)

Solving for D ( O - H )

2D(O-H)=690+242 2D(O-H)=932 D(O-H)=9322=466kJ/mol
