Q5

Let us consider an endothermic reaction which is non-spontaneous at the freezing point of water. However, the reaction is spontaneous at boiling point of water. Choose the correct option

a

Both ΔH and ΔS are (+ve)

b

ΔH is (−ve) but ΔS is (+ve)

c

Both ΔH and ΔS are (–ve)

d

ΔH is (+ve) but ΔS is (–ve)

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

Both ΔH and ΔS are (+ve)

Explanation

The given reaction is an endothermic process that is non-spontaneous at the freezing point of water but becomes spontaneous at the boiling point of water. This indicates that the process follows Gibbs free energy equation

ΔG=ΔH-TΔS

It is only possible when ΔH and ΔS both are positive.

Q6

Consider the given data:
(a) HCl(g)+10H2O(l)→HCl.10H2O   ΔH=-69.01 kJ mol-1
(b) HCl(g)+40H2O(l)→HCl.40H2O  ΔH=-72.79 kJ mol-1
Choose the correct statement :

[JEE Main 2025, 4 Apr (Shift 2)]

a

Dissolution of gas in water is an endothermic process

b

The heat of solution depends on the amount of solvent.

c

The heat of dilution for the HClHCl·10H2O to HCl.40H2O ) is 3.78 kJ mol-1.

d

The heat of formation of HCl solution is represented by both (a) and (b)

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

The heat of solution depends on the amount of solvent.

Explanation

ΔH < 0 ⇒ dissolution of HCl(g) is exothermic.

HCl(g) + 10H₂O(l) → HCl·10H2O
ΔH₁ = −69.01 kJ mol⁻¹

HCl(g) + 40H2O(l) → HCl·40H2O
ΔH₂ = −72.79 kJ mol⁻¹

Heat of dilution
= ΔH₂ − ΔH₁
= −72.79 − (−69.01)
= −3.78 kJ mol⁻¹

⇒ Heat of solution depends on amount of solvent.
⇒ Option (3) is incorrect.

For heat of formation, reactants must be in elemental state ⇒ Option (4) is incorrect.

Q7

If C (diamond) →C (graphite)+X kJ mol-1
C( diamond )+O2(g)→CO2(g)+YkJmol-1
C (graphite) +O2(g)→CO2(g)+ZkJmol-1

at constant temperature. Then

[JEE Main 2025, 29 Jan (Shift 2)]

a

X = Y - Z

b

X = Y + Z

c

-X=Y+Z

d

X= -Y+Z

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

X = Y - Z

Explanation

(a) Using Hess's Law, we sum the given reactions:
1. C (diamond) →C (graphite) + X
2. C (diamond) +O2→CO2+Y
3. C (graphite) +O2→CO2+Z

Reversing (3):

CO2→C( graphite )+O2-Z

Adding to (2):
C(diamond) → C(graphite) + (Y–Z)
Comparing with (1): X = Y – Z

a

23 J

b

28 J

c

46 J

d

20 J

✓ Correct answer: a)

Explanation

  • Energy supplied (heat), Q=48 JQ = 48 \, \text{J}Q=48J

  • Gas: monoatomic, n=1 moln = 1 \, \text{mol}n=1mol

  • Temperature change: \Delta T = 2\,^\circ \text{C} = 2 \, \text{K}

We are asked to find work done, W.

Step 1: Recall relations

For any process:

Q=ΔE+WQ = \Delta E + WQ=ΔE+W

Where:

  • ΔE=nCVΔT\Delta E = n C_V \Delta TΔE=nCV​ΔT (for a monoatomic ideal gas, CV=32RC_V = \frac{3}{2} RCV​=23​R)

  • W=W =W= work done by gas

Step 2: Compute change in internal energy

  • R=8.314 J/mol\cdotpKR = 8.314 \, \text{J/mol·K}R=8.314J/mol\cdotpK

CV=32R=32⋅8.314≈12.471 J/mol\cdotpKC_V = \frac{3}{2} R = \frac{3}{2} \cdot 8.314 \approx 12.471 \, \text{J/mol·K}CV​=23​R=23​⋅8.314≈12.471J/mol\cdotpK ΔE=nCVΔT=1⋅12.471⋅2≈24.942 J≈25 J\Delta E = n C_V \Delta T = 1 \cdot 12.471 \cdot 2 \approx 24.942 \, \text{J} \approx 25 \, \text{J}ΔE=nCV​ΔT=1⋅12.471⋅2≈24.942J≈25J

Step 3: Compute work done

W=Q−ΔE=48−24.942≈23 JW = Q - \Delta E = 48 - 24.942 \approx 23 \, \text{J}W=Q−ΔE=48−24.942≈23J
