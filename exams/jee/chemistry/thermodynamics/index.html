a

23 J

b

28 J

c

46 J

d

20 J

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

23 J

Explanation

  • Energy supplied (heat), Q=48 JQ = 48 \, \text{J}Q=48J

  • Gas: monoatomic, n=1 moln = 1 \, \text{mol}n=1mol

  • Temperature change: \Delta T = 2\,^\circ \text{C} = 2 \, \text{K}

We are asked to find work done, W.

Step 1: Recall relations

For any process:

Q=ΔE+WQ = \Delta E + WQ=ΔE+W

Where:

  • ΔE=nCVΔT\Delta E = n C_V \Delta TΔE=nCV​ΔT (for a monoatomic ideal gas, CV=32RC_V = \frac{3}{2} RCV​=23​R)

  • W=W =W= work done by gas

Step 2: Compute change in internal energy

  • R=8.314 J/mol\cdotpKR = 8.314 \, \text{J/mol·K}R=8.314J/mol\cdotpK

CV=32R=32⋅8.314≈12.471 J/mol\cdotpKC_V = \frac{3}{2} R = \frac{3}{2} \cdot 8.314 \approx 12.471 \, \text{J/mol·K}CV​=23​R=23​⋅8.314≈12.471J/mol\cdotpK ΔE=nCVΔT=1⋅12.471⋅2≈24.942 J≈25 J\Delta E = n C_V \Delta T = 1 \cdot 12.471 \cdot 2 \approx 24.942 \, \text{J} \approx 25 \, \text{J}ΔE=nCV​ΔT=1⋅12.471⋅2≈24.942J≈25J

Step 3: Compute work done

W=Q−ΔE=48−24.942≈23 JW = Q - \Delta E = 48 - 24.942 \approx 23 \, \text{J}W=Q−ΔE=48−24.942≈23J

✅ Answer: 23 J (Option A)

Q32

500 J of energy is transferred as heat to 0.5 mol of Argon gas at 298 K and 1.00 atm . The final temperature and the change in internal energy respectively are
(Given : R=8.3JK-1mol-1)

[JEE Main 2025, 29 Jan (Shift 1)]

a

378 K and 300 J

b

348 K and 300 J

c

368 K and 500 J

d

378 K and 500 J

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

348 K and 300 J

Explanation

Argon is a monoatomic ideal gas

At constant pressure:

Q = nCₚΔT

For monoatomic gas:

Cₚ = (5/2)R

Q = n × (5/2)R × ΔT

500 = 0.5 × (5/2) × 8.3 × ΔT

(5/2) × 8.3 = 20.75

500 = 0.5 × 20.75 × ΔT
500 = 10.375 × ΔT

ΔT = 500 / 10.375 ≈ 48 K

T₂ = 298 + 48 = 346 K ≈ 348 K

For monoatomic gas:

ΔU = n × (3/2)R × ΔT

= 0.5 × (3/2 × 8.3) × 48

= 0.5 × 12.45 × 48
≈ 300 J

Final Answer: 348 K and 300 J

✓ Correct answer: a)

Explanation

Option A is correct. The combustion reaction of benzene is:

C6H6(l) + 15/2 O2(g) → 6CO2(g) + 3H2O(l)

Using Hess’s law:

ΔHcombustion = ΣΔHf(products) − ΣΔHf(reactants)

Substituting the given values:

−3267 = [6(−393.5) + 3(−286)] − [ΔHf(C6H6)]

Therefore, the heat of formation of benzene is 48 kJ mol⁻¹.
