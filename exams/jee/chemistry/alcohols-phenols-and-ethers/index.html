Q1 FREE PREVIEW
The difference in the reaction of phenol with bromine in chloroform and bromine in water medium is due to [JEE Main 2022, 26 Jul (Shift 1)]
aHyperconujugation in substrate
bPolarity of solvent
cFree radical formation
dElectromeric effect of the substrate
✓ Correct answer: b) Polarity of solvent
ExplanationThe difference in phenol’s reaction with bromine in chloroform vs. water is due to the polarity of the solvent.In water (polar), phenol ionizes to phenoxide, which is highly reactive and gives tribromo product.In chloroform (non-polar), less ionization occurs, giving monobromo product.Correct answer: Option B – Polarity of solvent.
Q2 FREE PREVIEW

Which of the following ether react with HBr to form phenol?

(PYQ-2025)

a

\(\mathrm{Ph}-{\mathrm{CH}}_{2}-\mathrm{O}-{\mathrm{CH}}_{2}-{\mathrm{CH}}_{3}\)

b

\(\mathrm{Ph}-{\mathrm{CH}}_{2}-{\mathrm{OCH}}_{3}\)

c

\(\\ {\mathrm{CH}}_{3}\\ \mathrm{Ph}-\mathrm{O}-\overset{|}{\underset{|}{\mathrm{C}}}-{\mathrm{CH}}_{3}\\ {\mathrm{CH}}_{3}\)

d

\(\mathrm{Ph}-{\mathrm{CH}}_{2}-\mathrm{O}-{\mathrm{CH}}_{2}-\mathrm{Ph}\)

✓ Correct answer: c)

\(\\ {\mathrm{CH}}_{3}\\ \mathrm{Ph}-\mathrm{O}-\overset{|}{\underset{|}{\mathrm{C}}}-{\mathrm{CH}}_{3}\\ {\mathrm{CH}}_{3}\)

Explanation

The ether that reacts with HBr to form phenol must have a phenyl (-Ph) group directly attached to oxygen, making cleavage difficult via SN1 or SN2 .

Option C (Ph-O-C(CH₃)₃) forms phenol due to cleavage at the C-O bond.
