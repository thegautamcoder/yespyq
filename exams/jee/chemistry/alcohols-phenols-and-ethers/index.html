Q8

Given below are two statements

Statement-I: The boiling points of alcohols and phenols increase with increase in number of carbon atoms

Statement-II: The boiling points of alcohols and phenols are higher in comparison to other class of compounds such as ethers, haloalkanes

In the light of above statements, choose the correct answer from options given below.

[JEE Main 2025, 23 Jan (Shift 2)]

a

Statement-I is true but statement-II is false

b

Both statement-I and statement-II are true

c

Statement-I is false but statement-II is true

d

Both statement-I and statement-II are false

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

Both statement-I and statement-II are true

Explanation

Statement-I is true because as the number of carbon atoms increases, molecular weight and van der Waals forces increase, leading to higher boiling points in alcohols and phenols.

Statement-II is also true since alcohols and phenols form strong hydrogen bonds, making their boiling points higher than ethers and haloalkanes, which only have weaker dipole-dipole or van der Waals interactions. Hence, both statements are correct.

[JEE Main 2024, 5 Apr (Shift 1)]

a

Assertion is true but Reason is false

b

Both Assertion and Reason are true

d

Assertion is false but Reason is true

Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

✓ Correct answer: d)

Assertion is false but Reason is true

Explanation

Phenol is highly activated due to the –OH group. Even in non-polar solvents, it can undergo bromination without a catalyst. So, Assertion is false. Lewis acids polarize Br₂ and generate Br⁺, which is true for electrophilic substitution. So, Reason is true.

Q13

What is the rate of reaction of releasing carbon dioxide gas with aqueous sodium bicarbonate among the following? (memory based jee mains shift-1 28/01/2025)

(1) Picric acid

(2) Benzoic acid

(3) o-toluic acid

a

1 > 2 > 3

b

3 > 2 > 1

c

1 > 3 > 2

d

2 > 3 > 1

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

1 > 3 > 2

Explanation

The reaction of acid with aqueous sodium bicarbonate is known as an acid-base reaction, Stronger the acid , the higher will be the rate of reaction.

The electron-withdrawing group increases the acidic strength and the electron-donating group decreases the acidic strength.

Picric acid (2,4,6-Trinitrophenol ) is the strongest acid among all due to the presence of strong EWG (-NO2).

o-toluic acid is stronger than benzoic acid due to the ortho effect. Hence, the order will be Picric acid >o-Toluic> Benzoic acid
