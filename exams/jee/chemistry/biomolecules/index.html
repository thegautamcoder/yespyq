Q14

A linear octasaccharide (molar mass \(=1024 ~g mol ^{-1}\) ) on complete hydrolysis produces three monosaccharides: ribose, 2-deoxyribose and glucose. The amount of 2-deoxyribose formed is \(58.26 \%( w / w )\) of the total amount of the monosaccharides produced in the hydrolyzed products. The number of ribose unit(s) present in one molecule of octasaccharide is________.

Use: Molar mass \(\left(\right.\) in g \(\left.mol ^{-1}\right)\) : ribose \(=150,2\)-deoxyribose \(=134\), glucose \(=180\);
Atomic mass (in amu): \(H =1, O =16\)

[JEE Advanced 2025, Paper-2]

a

2

b

6

c

5

d

9

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

2

Explanation

Let numbers of units be: ribose = n₁, 2-deoxyribose = n₂, glucose = n₃.
Octasaccharide ⇒ n₁ + n₂ + n₃ = 8.

Total mass of monomers after complete hydrolysis:
150 n₁ + 134 n₂ + 180 n₃ = 1024 + 7×18 = 1150 g

Given 2-deoxyribose is 58.26% (w/w) of total monomers:
134 n₂ = 0.5826 × 1150 ≈ 670 ⇒ n₂ = 5

Then n₁ + n₃ = 3 and
150 n₁ + 180 n₃ = 1150 − 670 = 480

Put n₁ = 3 − n₃:
150(3 − n₃) + 180 n₃ = 480 ⇒ 450 + 30 n₃ = 480 ⇒ n₃ = 1, n₁ = 2.

Number of ribose units = 2.

Q20

Given below are two statements:

Statement I: Sucrose is dextrorotatory. However, sucrose upon hydrolysis gives a solution having mixture of products. This solution shows laevorotation.

Statement II: Hydrolysis of sucrose gives glucose and fructose. Since the laevorotation of glucose is more than the dextrorotation of fructose, the resulting solution becomes laevorotatory.

In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below

[JEE Main 2026, 22 Jan (Shift 1)]

a

Both Statement I and Statement II are true

b

Statement I is false but Statement II is true

c

Statement I is true but Statement II is false

d

Both Statement I and Statement II are false

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

Statement I is true but Statement II is false

Explanation

Sucrose is dextrorotatory.
On hydrolysis, it gives glucose and fructose. The mixture is called invert sugar and shows laevorotation because fructose has stronger optical activity.
Therefore, Statement I is true.

Statement II: It says laevorotation of glucose is more than dextrorotation of fructose.

This is incorrect because:

Glucose is dextrorotatory (+)
Fructose is laevorotatory (−)
Magnitude of fructose rotation is greater than glucose

So the mixture becomes laevorotatory due to fructose dominance, not glucose. Thus, Statement II is false.
