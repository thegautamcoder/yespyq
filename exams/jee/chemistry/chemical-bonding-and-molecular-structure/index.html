Paramagnetic species contain one or more unpaired electrons.

Q15

The correct increasing order for bond angles among BF3,PF3 and ClF3 is :

[JEE Main 2024, 9 Apr (Shift 2)]

a

ClF3<PF3<BF3

b

BF3=PF3<ClF3

c

BF3<PF3<ClF3

d

PF3<BF3<ClF3

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

ClF3<PF3<BF3

Explanation

BF₃ has a trigonal planar structure with no lone pairs on the central atom. The bond angle is 120°.

PF₃ has a trigonal pyramidal structure with one lone pair on phosphorus. Lone pair–bond pair repulsion reduces the bond angle from the ideal tetrahedral value, making it less than 109.5°.

ClF₃ has a T-shaped structure with three lone pairs on chlorine. Due to strong lone pair–lone pair and lone pair–bond pair repulsions, the bond angles are further reduced and become less than 90°.

Thus, the bond angle decreases in the order:
BF₃ > PF₃ > ClF₃

So, the increasing order is:
ClF₃ < PF₃ < BF₃

Given below are two statements :
Statement (I): Experimentally determined oxygen-oxygen bond lengths in the O3 are found to be same and the bond length is greater than that of a O = O (double bond) but less than that of a single (O − O) bond.
Statement (II) : The strong lone pair-lone pair repulsion between oxygen atoms is solely responsible for the fact that the bond length in ozone is smaller than that of a double bond (O = O) but more than that of a single bond (O − O).
In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below :

[JEE Main 2025, 24 Jan (Shift 2)]

Statement I is false but Statement II is true

Both Statement I and Statement II are true

Q26

A diatomic molecule has a dipole moment of 1.2 D . If the bond distance is 1 Å, then fractional charge present on each atom is____ ×10–11 esu.
(Given 1 D = 10–18 esu cm)

[JEE Main 2024, 31 Jan (Shift 2)]

a

10

b

11

c

12

d

13

Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

12

Explanation

Dipole moment is given by
μ = q × r

Given:
μ = 1.2 D = 1.2 × 10⁻¹⁸ esu·cm
r = 1 Å = 10⁻⁸ cm

So,
q = μ / r
q = (1.2 × 10⁻¹⁸) / (10⁻⁸)
q = 1.2 × 10⁻¹⁰ esu

This can be written as
q = 12 × 10⁻¹¹ esu

(D) IF7   (IV) Linear

(A)-(I), (B)-(IV), (C)-(III), (D)-(II)
