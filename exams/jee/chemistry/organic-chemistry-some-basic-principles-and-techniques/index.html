Q6

CORRECT order of stability for the following is

CH2=CH−,CH3−CH2−,CH≡C−

[JEE Main 2026, 28 Jan (Shift 2)]

a

CH≡C−>CH2=CH−>CH3−CH2−

b

CH3−CH2−>CH2=CH−>CH≡C−

c

CH2=CH−>CH≡C−>CH3−CH2−

d

CH≡C−>CH3−CH2−>CH2=CH−

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

CH≡C−>CH2=CH−>CH3−CH2−

Explanation

These are carbanions, so stability depends mainly on hybridization.

Rule: Greater s-character stabilizes the negative charge more.

sp → 50% s-character
sp² → 33% s-character
sp³ → 25% s-character

So:

CH≡C⁻ (sp) → most stable
CH₂=CH⁻ (sp²) → moderately stable
CH₃–CH₂⁻ (sp³) → least stable

Final order:

CH≡C⁻ > CH₂=CH⁻ > CH₃–CH₂⁻

In Dumas’ method for estimation of nitrogen 0.4 g of an organic compound gave 60 mL of nitrogen collected at 300 K temperature and 715 mm Hg pressure. The percentage composition of nitrogen in the compound is
(Given : Aqueous tension at 300 K = 15 mm Hg)

[JEE Main 2025, 3 Apr (Shift 2)]

a

15.71%

b

20.95%

c

17.46%

d

7.85%

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

15.71%

Explanation

Pressure of N₂ gas evolved

=715−15=700 mm Hg= 715 - 15 = 700 \text{ mm Hg} =700760 atm= \frac{700}{760} \text{ atm}

Moles of N₂ evolved:

n=PVRTn = \frac{PV}{RT} =700×60×10−3760×0.0821×300= \frac{700 \times 60 \times 10^{-3}}{760 \times 0.0821 \times 300} =0.0022 mol= 0.0022 \text{ mol}

Mass of N₂ evolved:

=0.0022×28=0.063 g= 0.0022 \times 28 = 0.063 \text{ g}

Weight percentage of nitrogen in the compound:

%N=wt. of nitrogenwt. of compound×100\%N = \frac{\text{wt. of nitrogen}}{\text{wt. of compound}} \times 100% =0.0630.4×100= \frac{0.063}{0.4} \times 100 = 15.71%​​
