A metal chloride contains 55.0% of chlorine by weight. 100 mL vapours of the metal chloride at STP weigh 0.57 g. The molecular formula of the metal chloride is (Given: Atomic mass of chlorine is 35.5 u)
aMCl2
bMCl4
cMCl3
dMCl
✓ Correct answer: a) MCl2
ExplanationGiven: Mass = 0.57 g, Volume = 100 mL = 0.1 L At STP, molar volume = 22.4 L Moles = 0.1 / 22.4 = 0.004464 mol Molar mass = 0.57 / 0.004464 ≈ 127.7 g/mol Let metal chloride be MClₓ %Cl = (35.5x / (M + 35.5x)) × 100 = 55 Putting M + 35.5x = 127.7, solve: 35.5x = 70.24 ⇒ x = 2 So formula = MCl₂ Answer: Option A

Q4 FREE PREVIEW

0.1 M solution of KI reacts with excess of \({H}_{2}S{O}_{4}\) and \(KI{O}_{3}\) solutions. According to equation

\(5{I}^{-}+I{O}_{3}^{-}+6{H}^{+}\to 3{I}_{2}+3{H}_{2}O\)

Identify the correct statements :
(A) 200 mL of KI solution reacts with 0.004 mol of \(KI{O}_{3}\)
(B) 200 mL of KI solution reacts with 0.006 mol of \({H}_{2}S{O}_{4}\)
(C) 0.5 L of KI solution produced 0.005 mol of \({I}_{2}\)
(D) Equivalent weight of \(KI{O}_{3}\) is equal to (\(\frac{\text{ Molecular weight }}{5}\))

Choose the correct answer from the options given below

a

(A) and (B) only

b

(A) and (D) only

c

(B) and (C) only

d

(C) and (D) only

✓ Correct answer: b)

(A) and (D) only

Explanation

\(200mL(0.2L)\) of KI solution \((0.1M)\)
\(\to\) Moles of Kl (I–)

\(=0.1\times 0.2=0.02\text{ moles }.\)

From reaction: 5 moles of \({I}^{-}\)react with 1 mole of \(I{O}_{3}^{-}\).
Required \(I{O}_{3}^{-}:0.02\times 1/5=0.004\) moles.

Equivalent weight formula:

\(\text{ Equivalent weight }=\frac{\text{ Molecular weight }}{n-\text{ factor }}\)

For \(I{O}_{3}^{-}\)in this reaction, the change in oxidation state ( n -factor) is 5 .
Hence, Equivalent weight =( Molecular weight)/5.

Q8 FREE PREVIEW

The hydrocarbon (𝑋) with molar mass 80 g \(mol{}^{-1}\) and 90% carbon has ______ degree of unsaturation.

[JEE Main 2025, 24 Jan (Shift 2)]

a

1

b

2

c

3

d

4

✓ Correct answer: c)

3

Explanation

First calculate no. of Carbon and H atoms as:
Mass of carbon \(=\frac{80\times 90}{100}=72g\)
Number of C-atoms \(=\frac{72}{12}=6\)
Mass of hydrogen =80-72=8 g
Number of H -atoms \(=\frac{8}{1}=8\)
Molecular formula of hydrocarbon

\(={C}_{6}{H}_{8}\)

Degree of unsaturation =12+2-8 / 2=3

Q9 FREE PREVIEW

Choose the Incorrect Statement about Dalton's Atomic Theory

[JEE Main 2024, 4 Apr (Shift 2)]

a

All the atoms of a given element have identical properties including identical mass.

b

Chemical reactions involve reorganization of atoms

c

Compounds are formed when atoms of different elements combine in any ratio.

d

Matter consists of indivisible atoms.

✓ Correct answer: c)

Compounds are formed when atoms of different elements combine in any ratio.

Explanation

According to Dalton's Atomic Theory,
compounds are formed when atoms of different elements combine in a fixed (definite) ratio by mass and number.
So, saying they combine in "any" ratio is incorrect.
