Q4

For \(10\) observations x1,x2,…,x10, if ∑i=110xi+22=180 and ∑i=110xi-12=90, then their standard deviation is:

[JEE Main 2026, 4 Apr (Shift 2)]

a

\(2\)

b

3

c

22

d

\(3\)

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

\(3\)

Explanation

\(\sum_{\mathrm{i}=1}^{10}\left(\mathrm{x}_{\mathrm{i}}+2\right)^2=180 \)
\( \sum_{\mathrm{i}=1}^{10} \mathrm{x}_{\mathrm{i}}^2+4 \sum_{\mathrm{i}=1}^{10} \mathrm{x}_{\mathrm{i}}+\sum_{\mathrm{i}=1}^{10} 4=180 \)
\( \sum_{\mathrm{i}=1}^{10} \mathrm{x}_{\mathrm{i}}^2+4 \sum_{\mathrm{i}=1}^{10} \mathrm{x}_{\mathrm{i}}=180-40=140 \ldots\ (1)\)
\( \text { Also } \sum_{\mathrm{i}=1}^{10}\left(\mathrm{x}_{\mathrm{i}}-1\right)^2=90 \)
\( \sum_{i=1}^{10} x_i^2-2 \sum_{i=1}^{10} x_i+\sum_{i=1}^{10} 1=90 \)
\( \sum_{i=1}^{10} x_i^2-2 \sum_{i=1}^{10} x_i=90-10=80 \ldots\ (2)\)
From (1) and (2) we get:
\( \sum_{i=1}^{10} x_i^2=100 \text { and } \sum_{i=1}^{10} x_i=10 \)
\( \sigma^2=\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{10} x_i^2}{N}-\left(\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{10} x_i}{N}\right)^2\)
\( =\frac{100}{10}-\left(\frac{10}{10}\right)^2\)
\( \sigma^2=10-1=9 \)
\( \Rightarrow \sigma=3\)

Q9

For a statistical data x1,x2,…,x10 of 10 values, a student obtained the mean as \(5.5\) and ∑i=110xi2=371. He later found that he had noted two values in the data incorrectly as 4 and 5, instead of the correct values 6 and 8, respectively. The variance of the corrected data is

[JEE Main 2025, 24 Jan (Shift 1)]

a

7

b

4

c

9

d

5

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

7

Explanation

Given,
Number of terms \(=10\)
Mean \(=5.5\)

\(\sum_{i=1}^{10} x_i^2=371\)

\(\begin{aligned} & \mu=\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{10} \mathrm{x}_{\mathrm{i}}}{n} \\ & 5.5=\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{10} \mathrm{x}_{\mathrm{i}}}{10} \\ & \sum_{i=1}^{10} \mathrm{x}_{\mathrm{i}}=55\end{aligned}\)

Corrected \(\sum^{10} x_i=55-(4+5)+(6+8)=60\)

Corrected Mean \(\mu=\frac{60}{10}=6\)

Corrected \(\sum_{i=1}^{10} x_i^2=371-\left(4^2+5^2\right)+\left(6^2+8^2\right)=430\)

Now, corrected variance \(=\frac{1}{n} \text { Corrected } \sum_{i=1}^{10} x_i^2-(\text { Corrected Mean } \mu)^2\)

\(=\frac{1}{10} \times 430-(6)^2=43-36=7\)

Q12

Let \(a_1, a_2, \ldots a_{10}\) be 10 observations such that \(\sum_{k=1}^{10} a_k=50\) and ∑∀k<jak·aj=1100. Then the standard deviation of \(a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_{10}\) is equal to :

[JEE Main 2024, 27 Jan (Shift 1)]

a

\(\sqrt{115}\)

b

5

c

10

d

\(\sqrt{5}\)

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

\(\sqrt{5}\)

Explanation

Given \(a_1+a_2+\cdots+a_{10}=50\)

\(\left(a_1+a_2+\ldots+a_{10}\right)^2=2500\)

\(\sum_{i=1}^{10} a_i^2+2 \sum_{\forall k<j} a_k \cdot a_j=2500\)

\(\sum_{i=1}^{10} a_i^2=2500-2(1100)=300\)

Standard Deviation \(=\sqrt{\frac{\sum a_k^2}{10}-\left(\frac{\sum a_k}{10}\right)^2} \)
Standard Deviation \(=\sqrt{\frac{300}{10}-\left(\frac{50}{10}\right)^2}=\sqrt{5}\)

Q16

A data consists of \(20\) observations x1,x2,…,x20. If ∑i=120xi+52=2500 and ∑i=120xi-52=100, then the ratio of mean to standard deviation of this data is:

[JEE Main 2026, 6 Apr (Shift 1)]

a

2:1

b

3:1

c

3:2

d

4:1

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

3:1

Explanation

Let the mean of the \(20\) observations be \(\bar{x}\) and the standard deviation be \(\sigma\).

\(\sum_{i=1}^{20}\left(x_i+5\right)^2=2500\)\(\ldots(1)\)

\(\sum_{i=1}^{20}\left(x_i-5\right)^2=100\)\(\ldots(2)\)

\((1)-(2)\)

\(\Rightarrow 20 \sum x_i=2400 \Rightarrow \sum x_i=120\)

mean, \(\bar{x}=6\)

\((1)+(2)\)

\(\Rightarrow 2 \sum x_{i}^2+40\left(5^2\right)=2600\)

\(\sum x_i^2=800\)

\(\sigma^2=\frac{\sum x_{i}^2}{\mathrm{n}}-(\bar{x})^2=\frac{800}{20}-36=4\)

\(\bar{x}: \sigma=6: 2=3: 1\)
