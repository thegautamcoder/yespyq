Q3

The mean and variance of a data of \(10\) observations are \(10\) and \(2\) , respectively. If an observations \(\alpha\) in this data is replaced by \(\beta\), then the means and variance becomes \(10.1\) and \(1.99\), respectively. Then \(\alpha+\beta\) equals

a

\(10\)

b

\(15\)

c

\(20\)

d

\(5\)

\(20\)

Explanation

Let, in first case, \(10\) numbers are \(x_1, x_2, \ldots \ldots x_9, \alpha\)

Then, ∑i=19xi+α10=10

⇒α+∑i=19xi=100⇒∑i=19xi=100−α

And σ2=∑xi2+α2n−∑xi+αn2

⇒2=∑xi2+α210−102

⇒∑xi2+α2n=102

⇒x12+x22+……x92+α2=1020

⇒∑xi2=1020−α2

In second case, let number are

x1,x2,……x9,β

Now \(\frac{\sum_{i=1}^9 x_i+\beta}{10}=\frac{100-\alpha+\beta}{10}\)

\(\Rightarrow 10.1=\frac{100-\alpha+\beta}{10}\)

\(\Rightarrow \alpha-\beta=-1\)

Now, σ2=∑xi2+β2n−∑xi+βn2

⇒∑xi2+β210−(10.1)2=1.99

⇒1.99=1020-α2+β210-102.01⇒1020-α2+β2=1040

⇒β2−α2=20

\(\Rightarrow \alpha^2-\beta^2=-20\)

\(\Rightarrow (\alpha-\beta)(\alpha+\beta)=-20 \)

\(\Rightarrow (-1)(\alpha+\beta)=-20\)

Hence, \(\alpha+\beta=20\)
