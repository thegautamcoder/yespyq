& {\left[\begin{array}{ll}
1 & 1 \\

\end{aligned}

0100

If the probability that the random variable X takes the value x is given by P(X=x)=k(x+1)3-x, x=0,1,2,3….., where k is a constant, then PX≥3 is equal to

[JEE Main 2025, 3 Apr (Shift 2)]

a

727

b

49

c

827

d

19

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

19

Explanation

We have

P(X=x)=k(x+1)3−x,x=0,1,2,…P(X=x)=k(x+1)3^{-x}, \quad x=0,1,2,\dots

Use ∑x=0∞(x+1)rx=1(1−r)2\sum_{x=0}^{\infty}(x+1)r^x=\frac{1}{(1-r)^2} (for ∣r∣<1|r|<1) with r=13r=\frac13:

∑x=0∞(x+1)(13)x=1(1−13)2=1(23)2=94\sum_{x=0}^{\infty}(x+1)\left(\frac13\right)^x = \frac{1}{\left(1-\frac13\right)^2} = \frac{1}{\left(\frac23\right)^2} = \frac94

Since total probability is 1:

k⋅94=1⇒k=49k\cdot \frac94 = 1 \Rightarrow k = \frac49

Now

P(X≤2)=∑x=02k(x+1)3−x=49[1+23+39]=49⋅2=89P(X\le2) = \sum_{x=0}^{2} k(x+1)3^{-x} = \frac49\left[1 + \frac{2}{3} + \frac{3}{9}\right] = \frac49 \cdot 2 = \frac89

So

P(X≥3)=1−P(X≤2)=1−89=19.

P(A∩B)=610·59=3090=13

Explanation

Q6

One die has two faces marked \(1\), two faces marked \(2\), one face marked \(3\) and one face marked \(4\). Another die has one face marked \(1,\) two faces marked \(2\), two faces marked \(3\) and one face marked \(4\). The probability of getting the sum of numbers to be \(4\) or \(5\), when both the dice are thrown together, is

[JEE Main 2025, 23 Jan (Shift 1)]

a

12

b

35

c

23

d

49

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

12

Explanation

outcomes for one dice =1,1,2,2,3,4

outcomes for another dice =1,2,2,3,3,4

Identifying Favorable Outcomes for a Sum of \(4\):

\( (1, 3) \): Probability = 13×13=19

\( (2, 2) \): Probability =13×13=19

\( (3, 1) \): Probability =16×16=136

Identifying Favorable Outcomes for a Sum of \(5\):

\( (1, 4) \): Probability =13×16=118

\( (2, 3) \): Probability = 13×13=19

\( (3, 2) \): Probability =16×13=118

\( (4, 1) \): Probability =16×16=136

Calculating the Total Probability:

P(Sum=4 or Sum=5)=P(Sum=4)+P(Sum=5)

=19+19+136+ 118+19+118+136
=12

Q11

Let the sum of two positive integers be \(24.\) If the probability, that their product is not less than \(\frac{3}{4}\) times their greatest possible product, is \(\frac{m}{n}\), where \(\operatorname{gcd}(m, n)=1\), then \(n-m\) equals

[JEE Main 2024, 8 Apr (Shift 1)]

a

9

b

10

c

11

d

8

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

10

Explanation

\( \mathrm{x}+\mathrm{y}=24, \mathrm{x}, \mathrm{y} \in \mathrm{~N} \)
\( \mathrm{AM}>\mathrm{GM} \Rightarrow \mathrm{xy} \leq 144 \)
\( x y \geq 108\)

Favorable pairs of \((x, y)\) are

{(13,11),(12,12),(14,10),(15,9),(16,8),(17,7),(18,6),(6,18),(7,17),(8,16),(9,15),(10,14),(11,13)}
i.e. \(13\) cases

\(n-m=10\)
