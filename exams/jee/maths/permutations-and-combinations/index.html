The largest n∈N such that 3n divides 50 ! is:

[JEE Main 2025, 2 Apr (Shift 1)]

a

21

b

22

c

20

d

23

✓ Correct answer: b)

22

Explanation

To find the exponent of a prime pp in n!n!, we use:

Exponent of p in n!=∑k=1∞⌊npk⌋\text{Exponent of } p \text{ in } n! = \sum_{k=1}^{\infty} \left\lfloor \frac{n}{p^k} \right\rfloor

Here, p=3p = 3, and n=50n = 50. So,

∑k=1∞⌊503k⌋=⌊503⌋+⌊509⌋+⌊5027⌋+⌊5081⌋+…\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} \left\lfloor \frac{50}{3^k} \right\rfloor = \left\lfloor \frac{50}{3} \right\rfloor + \left\lfloor \frac{50}{9} \right\rfloor + \left\lfloor \frac{50}{27} \right\rfloor + \left\lfloor \frac{50}{81} \right\rfloor + \dots

Now compute:

  • ⌊503⌋=16\left\lfloor \frac{50}{3} \right\rfloor = 16
  • ⌊509⌋=5\left\lfloor \frac{50}{9} \right\rfloor = 5
  • ⌊5027⌋=1\left\lfloor \frac{50}{27} \right\rfloor = 1
  • ⌊5081⌋=0\left\lfloor \frac{50}{81} \right\rfloor = 0

Adding up:

16+5+1=2216 + 5 + 1 = 22

Answer: 22
