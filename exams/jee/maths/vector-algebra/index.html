Write \(\vec{X}, \vec{Y}, \vec{Z}\) in terms of \(\vec{x}, \vec{y}, \vec{z}\):

\(\vec{X}=\alpha\vec{x}+\beta\vec{y}-\vec{z},\quad \vec{Y}=-\vec{x}+\alpha\vec{y}+\beta\vec{z},\quad \vec{Z}=\beta\vec{x}-\vec{y}+\alpha\vec{z}\).

\(\det(B)=\begin{vmatrix}1&2&3\\2&3&1\\3&1&2\end{vmatrix}=1(6-1)-2(4-3)+3(2-9)=-18\neq 0\).

\(=\alpha(\alpha^2+\beta)+(\alpha\beta-1)+\beta(\beta^2+\alpha)\)

\(=\alpha^3+\beta^3+3\alpha\beta-1\).

Hence,

\(\alpha^3+\beta^3+3\alpha\beta-1=0\).

\(\alpha^3+\beta^3+(-1)^3-3\alpha\beta(-1)=0\).

Using \(a^3+b^3+c^3-3abc=(a+b+c)(a^2+b^2+c^2-ab-bc-ca)\), with \(a=\alpha,\ b=\beta,\ c=-1\), we get

\((\alpha+\beta-1)(\alpha^2+\beta^2+1-\alpha\beta+\alpha+\beta)=0\).
