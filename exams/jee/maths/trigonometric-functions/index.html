\(\frac{10-\sqrt{10}}{12}\)

\(\frac{\sqrt{10}-10}{12}\)

\(\frac{\sqrt{10}-10}{6}\)

\(\frac{10-\sqrt{10}}{6}\)

✓ Correct answer: b)

\(\frac{\sqrt{10}-10}{12}\)

Explanation

\(4\cos\alpha+5\sin\alpha=1\)

\(-\frac{\pi}{2}<\alpha<\frac{\pi}{2}\),

\(\cos\alpha>0\).

\(t=\tan\alpha\).

\(\sin\alpha=t\cos\alpha\)

Substituting, \((4+5t)\cos\alpha=1\)

\(\cos\alpha=\frac{1}{4+5t}\)

\(\sec^2\alpha=1+\tan^2\alpha\),

\((4+5t)^2=1+t^2\)

\(16+40t+25t^2=1+t^2\)

\(24t^2+40t+15=0\)

\(t=\frac{-40\pm\sqrt{1600-1440}}{48}\)

\(=\frac{-40\pm4\sqrt{10}}{48}\)

\(=\frac{-10\pm\sqrt{10}}{12}\)

\(\cos\alpha=\frac{1}{4+5t}>0\)

\(t=\frac{-10-\sqrt{10}}{12}\),

\(4+5t<0\),

which gives \(\cos\alpha<0\), not possible.

Therefore \(t=\frac{-10+\sqrt{10}}{12}\)

\(=\frac{\sqrt{10}-10}{12}\)
