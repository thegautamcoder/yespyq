The area enclosed by the curves \(y=x^3\) and \(y=\sqrt{x}\) is:

\(\frac{5}{3}\) sq. units

b

\(\frac{5}{4}\) sq. units

c

\(\frac{5}{12}\) sq. units

d

\(\frac{12}{5}\) sq. units

Q5

\(\text { The area of region enclosed by the curves } y=e^x, y=\left|e^x-1\right| \text { and } y \text {-axis is (in sq. units) }\)

a

\(1\)

b

\(1-ln 2\)

c

\(1+ln 2\)

d

\(ln 2\)

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

\(1-ln 2\)

Explanation

\(\begin{aligned}
& \mathrm{e}^x=1-\mathrm{e}^x \\
& \mathrm{e}^x=\frac{1}{2} \\
& \mathrm{x}=-\ln 2 \\
& \text { required area }=\int_{-\ln 2}^0\left(e^x-\left(1-e^x\right)\right) d x=\left[2 e^x-x\right]_{-\ln 2}^0 \\
& =2-\left(2 e^{-\ln 2}+\ln 2\right)=2-1-\ln 2=1-\ln 2
\end{aligned}\)
