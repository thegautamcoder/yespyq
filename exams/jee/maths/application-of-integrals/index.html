The area enclosed by the curves \(y=x^3\) and \(y=\sqrt{x}\) is:

a

\(\frac{5}{3}\) sq. units

b

\(\frac{5}{4}\) sq. units

c

\(\frac{5}{12}\) sq. units

d

\(\frac{12}{5}\) sq. units

✓ Correct answer: c)

\(\frac{5}{12}\) sq. units

Explanation

The point of intersection of these curves will bex=x3⇒x=0,1

Then, the area is given by

A=∫01x-x3dx=23x32-x4401=23(1)-14=512
