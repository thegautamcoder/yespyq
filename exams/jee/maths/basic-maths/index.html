Q6

The product of all real solutions of the equation e5logex2+3=x8,x>0, is:

[JEE Main 2025, 22 Jan (Shift 1)]

a

e85

b

e65

c

e2

d

e

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

e85

Explanation

Given: \(e^{5(\ln x)^2+3}=x^8\)

Taking \(\log\) on both sides:

\(\begin{aligned} & \Rightarrow \ln e^{5(\ln x)^2+3}=\ln x^8 \\ & \Rightarrow 5(\ln x)^2+3=8 \ln x\end{aligned}\)

Let \(\ln x=t\)

\(\begin{aligned} & \Rightarrow 5 t^2-8 t+3=0 \\ & t_1+t_2=\frac{8}{5} \\ & \Rightarrow \ln x_1+\ln x_2=\frac{8}{5} \\ & \Rightarrow \ln x_1 x_2=\frac{8}{5} \\ & \Rightarrow x_1 x_2=e^{8 / 5}\end{aligned}\)

Q10

The number of distinct real solutions of the equation xx+4+3x+2+10=0 is

[JEE Main 2026, 22 Jan (Shift 1)]

a

\(2\)

b

\(0\)

c

\(1\)

d

\(3\)

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

\(1\)

Explanation

Given: \(x|x+4|+3|x+2|+10=0\)

Case 1: \(x \geq-2\)

\(|x+4|=x+4, |x+2|=x+2\)

\(\therefore x(x+4)+3(x+2)+10=0\)

\(\Rightarrow x^2+7 x+16=0\)

\(D=49-64=-15<0 \Rightarrow\) no real solution

Case 2: \(-4 \leq x<-2\)

\(|x+4|=x+4, |x+2|=-(x+2)\)

\(\therefore x(x+4)+3(-(x+2))+10=0\)

\(\Rightarrow x^2+x+4=0\)

\(D=1-16=-15<0 \Rightarrow\) no real solution

Case 3: \(x<-4\)

\(|x+4|=-(x+4), |x+2|=-(x+2)\)

\(\therefore x(-(x+4))+3(-(x+2))+10=0\)

\(\Rightarrow x^2+7 x-4=0\)

\(\Rightarrow x=\frac{-7 \pm \sqrt{65}}{2}\)

Only \(\frac{-7-\sqrt{65}}{2}<-4\) is valid.

Number of distinct real solutions \(=1\)
