Q1 FREE PREVIEW

If the set of all solutions of |x2+x-9|=|x|+|x2-9| is [α,β]∪[γ,∞), then α2+β2+γ2 is equal to:

[JEE Main 2026, 4 Apr (Shift 1)]

a

\(9\)

b

\(18\)

c

\(36\)

d

\(72\)

✓ Correct answer: b)

\(18\)

Explanation

We know if \( |\mathrm{a}|+|\mathrm{b}|=|\mathrm{a}+\mathrm{b}|\)
Then \( \mathrm{a} \cdot \mathrm{~b} \geq 0 \)
\( \Rightarrow \mathrm{x}\left(\mathrm{x}^2-9\right) \geq 0\)
\( \mathrm{x} \in[-3,0] \cup[3, \infty)\)
\( \alpha^2+\beta^2+\gamma^2=18\)

Q2 FREE PREVIEW

The sum of the squares of the roots of |x-2|2+|x-2|-2=0 and the squares of the roots of x2-2|x-3|-5=0, is

[JEE Main 2025, 8 Apr (Shift 1)]

a

26

b

36

c

30

d

24

✓ Correct answer: b)

36

Explanation

|x−2|2+|x−2|−2=0

(|x−2|+2)(|x−2|−1)=0

|x−2|+2=0, |x−2|−1=0

(not possible), |x−2|=1

x=2±1

x=3,1

and x2−2(x−3)−5=0

Case I:-  x ⩾ 3

x2−2x+6−5=0

(x−1)2=0

x=1 (not possible)

Case II:-  x < 3

x2+2x−6−5=0

x2+2x−11=0

Let α, β be the roots of this equation then

α2+β2=(α+β)2−2αβ

=(−2)2−2(−11)=4+22=26

Sum of the squares of the roots =32+12+26=36
