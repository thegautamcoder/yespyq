[JEE Main 2024, 6 Apr (Shift 1)]

a

\(0\)

b

\(4\alpha +2\beta\)

c

\(2n\)

d

✓ Correct answer: b)

\(4\alpha +2\beta\)

Explanation

\(A_r=\left|$$\begin{array}{ccc}r & 1 & \frac{n^2}{2}+\alpha \\ 2 r & 2 & n^2-\beta \\ 3 r-2 & 3 & \frac{n(3 n-1)}{2}\end{array}$$\right|\)

\(2 A_{10}-A_8=\left|$$\begin{array}{ccc}20 & 1 & \frac{n^2}{2}+a \\ 40 & 2 & n^2-\beta \\ 56 & 3 & \frac{n(3 n-1)}{2}\end{array}$$\right|-\left|$$\begin{array}{ccc}8 & 1 & \frac{n^2}{2}+a \\ 16 & 2 & n^2-\beta \\ 22 & 3 & \frac{n(3 n-1)}{2}\end{array}$$\right|\)

\(=\left|$$\begin{array}{ccc}12 & 1 & \frac{n^2}{2}+a \\ 24 & 2 & n^2-\beta \\ 34 & 3 & \frac{n(3 n-1)}{2}\end{array}$$\right|\)

\(=\left|$$\begin{array}{ccc}0 & 1 & \frac{n^2}{2}+a \\ 0 & 2 & n^2-\beta \\ -2 & 3 & \frac{n(3 n-1)}{2}\end{array}$$\right|\)
