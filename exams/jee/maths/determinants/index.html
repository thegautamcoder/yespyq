[JEE Main 2024, 6 Apr (Shift 1)]

\(0\)

b

2n

d

2α+4β

✓ Correct answer: b)

4α+2β

Explanation

\(A_r=\left|\begin{array}{ccc}r & 1 & \frac{n^2}{2}+\alpha \\ 2 r & 2 & n^2-\beta \\ 3 r-2 & 3 & \frac{n(3 n-1)}{2}\end{array}\right|\)

\(2 A_{10}-A_8=\left|\begin{array}{ccc}20 & 1 & \frac{n^2}{2}+a \\ 40 & 2 & n^2-\beta \\ 56 & 3 & \frac{n(3 n-1)}{2}\end{array}\right|-\left|\begin{array}{ccc}8 & 1 & \frac{n^2}{2}+a \\ 16 & 2 & n^2-\beta \\ 22 & 3 & \frac{n(3 n-1)}{2}\end{array}\right|\)

\(=\left|\begin{array}{ccc}12 & 1 & \frac{n^2}{2}+a \\ 24 & 2 & n^2-\beta \\ 34 & 3 & \frac{n(3 n-1)}{2}\end{array}\right|\)

\(=\left|\begin{array}{ccc}0 & 1 & \frac{n^2}{2}+a \\ 0 & 2 & n^2-\beta \\ -2 & 3 & \frac{n(3 n-1)}{2}\end{array}\right|\)

\(=-2\left(\left(n^2-\beta\right)-\left(n^2+2 \alpha\right)\right)\)

\(=-2(-\beta-2 \alpha)\)

\(=4 \alpha+2 \beta\)

If det((a+1)adj((a)A)) is 2m3n,m,n∈ {0,1,2,…..20}, then m+n is equal to :

17

15

✓ Correct answer: d)

16

Explanation

Since A+I=[1a1210a12]A + I = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & a & 1 \\ 2 & 1 & 0 \\ a & 1 & 2 \end{bmatrix},

we subtract the identity matrix II to get AA:

A=[1a1210a12]−[100010001]=[0a1200a11]A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & a & 1 \\ 2 & 1 & 0 \\ a & 1 & 2 \end{bmatrix} - \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & a & 1 \\ 2 & 0 & 0 \\ a & 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix}

We compute the determinant of AA:

det⁡(A)=∣0a1200a11∣\det(A) = \begin{vmatrix} 0 & a & 1 \\ 2 & 0 & 0 \\ a & 1 & 1 \end{vmatrix}

−2a+2=−4  ⟹  −2a=−6  ⟹  a=3-2a + 2 = -4 \implies -2a = -6 \implies a = 3

det⁡((a+1)adj(aI)A)=[(a+1)a2]3det⁡(A)\det((a + 1) \text{adj}(aI) A) = \left[(a + 1)a^2\right]^3 \det(A)

Substitute a=3a = 3 and det⁡(A)=−4\det(A) = -4:

(a+1)a2=(3+1)⋅32=4⋅9=36(a + 1)a^2 = (3 + 1) \cdot 3^2 = 4 \cdot 9 = 36 [36]3⋅(−4)=363⋅(−4)\left[36\right]^3 \cdot (-4) = 36^3 \cdot (-4)

363⋅(−4)=26⋅36⋅(−22)=−28⋅3636^3 \cdot (-4) = 2^6 \cdot 3^6 \cdot (-2^2) = -2^{8} \cdot 3^6

∣det⁡((a+1)adj(aI)A)∣=28⋅36|\det((a + 1) \text{adj}(aI) A)| = 2^8 \cdot 3^6

Hence, m=8m = 8 and n=6n = 6.
