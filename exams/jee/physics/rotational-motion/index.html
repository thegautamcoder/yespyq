✓ Correct answer: a)

Given position vector of particle in circular motion.
r→ = acosωti^ + asinωtj^

v→ = dr→ dt = −aωsinωti^ + aωcosωtj^

Q9

A wheel is rolling on a plane surface. The speed of a particle on the highest point of the rim is 8 m/s. The speed of the particle on the rim of the wheel at the same level as the centre of wheel, will be :

a

42 m/s

b

8 m/s

c

4 m/s

d

82 m/s

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

42 m/s

Explanation

For a wheel in pure rolling, the speed at the highest point is twice the translational speed of its center of mass (vcm).

vtop=2vcm=8 m/s

vcm=4 m/s

For a particle on the rim at the same level as the center, its velocity has two perpendicular components:

Translational velocity: vcm (horizontal)

Tangential (rotational) velocity: ωR=vcm (vertical)

The resultant speed is the vector sum of these two perpendicular components:

v=vcm2+vcm2=2vcm

Substitute vcm=4 m/s:

v=42 m/s

Q12

A solid sphere (A) of mass 5m and a spherical shell (B) of mass m, both having same radius, are placed on a rough surface. When a force of same magnitude is applied tangentially at the highest points of A and B, they start rolling without slipping with an acceleration of aA and aB, respectively. The ratio of aA and aB is ____________.

[JEE Main 2026, 6 Apr (Shift 2)]

a

5 : 21

b

6 : 10

c

21 : 25

d

1 : 5

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

5 : 21

Explanation

Let's first derive a general formula for the linear acceleration (\(a\)) of a round body of mass \(M\), radius \(R\), and moment of inertia Icm when a tangential force \(F\) is applied at its highest point.

Assume a static friction force \(f\) acts backward at the point of contact with the rough surface.

Translational Motion (Newton's Second Law):

\[F - f = Ma\]

Rotational Motion (about the Center of Mass):

Both the applied force \(F\) and the friction \(f\) produce a clockwise torque.

\[\tau_{net} = F \cdot R + f \cdot R = I_{cm} \alpha\]

Since the body is rolling without slipping on a stationary surface, we use the constraint a=αR, which gives α=aR. Substituting this in:

\[R(F + f) = I_{cm} \left(\frac{a}{R}\right)\] \[F + f = \frac{I_{cm} a}{R^2}\]

Solving for Acceleration (\(a\)):

Add Equation 1 and Equation 2 to eliminate \(f\):

\[(F - f) + (F + f) = Ma + \frac{I_{cm} a}{R^2}\] \[2F = a \left(M + \frac{I_{cm}}{R^2}\right)\] \[a = \frac{2F}{M + \frac{I_{cm}}{R^2}}\]

Applying to Solid Sphere (A)

Mass:MA=5m

Moment of Inertia: For a solid sphere, I=25MR2.

\[I_A = \frac{2}{5}(5m)R^2 = 2mR^2\]

Acceleration (aA):

\[a_A = \frac{2F}{5m + \frac{2mR^2}{R^2}}\] \[a_A = \frac{2F}{5m + 2m} = \frac{2F}{7m}\]

Applying to Spherical Shell (B)

Mass: MB=m

Moment of Inertia: For a spherical shell (hollow sphere), I=23MR2.

\[I_B = \frac{2}{3}mR^2\]

Acceleration (aB):

\[a_B = \frac{2F}{m + \frac{\frac{2}{3}mR^2}{R^2}}\] \[a_B = \frac{2F}{m + \frac{2}{3}m} = \frac{2F}{\frac{5}{3}m} = \frac{6F}{5m}\]

Finding the Ratio

Now, we find the ratio of aA to aB:

\[\frac{a_A}{a_B} = \frac{\left(\frac{2F}{7m}\right)}{\left(\frac{6F}{5m}\right)}\] \[\frac{a_A}{a_B} = \frac{2F}{7m} \times \frac{5m}{6F}\] \[\frac{a_A}{a_B} = \frac{10}{42} = \frac{5}{21}\]

The ratio of their accelerations is 5 : 21.

ExplanationGiven:
  • Mass of the cylinder: mm
  • Radius of the cylinder: rr
  • Inclined plane angle: θ=45∘\theta = 45^\circ
Steps:

  1. Forces acting on the cylinder:

    • Component of gravitational force along the incline: mgsin⁡θm g \sin \theta
    • Frictional force: ff
    • Normal force: N=mgcos⁡θN = m g \cos \theta

  2. Equations of motion: For rolling without slipping:

    • Translational motion: ma=mgsin⁡θ−fm a = m g \sin \theta - f
    • Rotational motion about the center of mass: fr=Iαf r = I \alpha For a solid cylinder, I=12mr2I = \frac{1}{2} m r^2, and rolling without slipping implies α=ar\alpha = \frac{a}{r}.
      Substituting II and α\alpha: fr=12mr2⋅arf r = \frac{1}{2} m r^2 \cdot \frac{a}{r},​ f=12maf = \frac{1}{2} m a

  3. Substitute ff into the translational motion equation:

    ma=mgsin⁡θ−12mam a = m g \sin \theta - \frac{1}{2} m a

    Simplify:

    ma+12ma=mgsin⁡θm a + \frac{1}{2} m a = m g \sin \theta, 32ma=mgsin⁡θ\frac{3}{2} m a = m g \sin \theta, a=23gsin⁡θa = \frac{2}{3} g \sin \theta

  4. Substitute θ=45∘\theta = 45^\circ:

    a=23gsin⁡45∘a = \frac{2}{3} g \sin 45^\circ

    Since sin⁡45∘=12\sin 45^\circ = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}

    a=23g⋅12=2g32a = \frac{2}{3} g \cdot \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} = \frac{2g}{3\sqrt{2}}​
Final Answer:

The acceleration of the axis of the cylinder is:

a=2g32a = \frac{2g}{3\sqrt{2}}​
