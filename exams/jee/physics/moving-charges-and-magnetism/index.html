A current of 30 A each flows in opposite directions in two conducting wires, placed parallel to each other at a distance of 8 cm. The magnetic field at the mid point between the two wires is ____ μT. μ04π=10-7N/A2

[JEE Main 2026, 6 Apr (Shift 2)]

a

30

b

300

c

150

d

0

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

300

Explanation

Given Data

Current (\(I\)) = 30 A

Total distance between wires (\(d\)) = 8 cm

Distance from each wire to the midpoint (\(r\)) = 4 cm = 0.04 m

Concept & Formula

The magnetic field produced by a long straight wire at a distance \(r\) is:

\[B = \frac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi r}\]

Since the currents flow in opposite directions, applying the Right-Hand Grip Rule shows that the magnetic fields generated by both wires point in the same direction at the midpoint between them. Therefore, their magnetic fields add up:

\[B_{net} = B_1 + B_2 = 2 \left( \frac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi r} \right) = \frac{\mu_0 I}{\pi r}\]

Calculation

From the given constant μ04π=10-7, we can determine that μ0π=4×10-7.

Substitute the values into the net magnetic field formula:

\[B_{net} = \frac{4 \times 10^{-7} \times 30}{0.04}\] \[B_{net} = \frac{120 \times 10^{-7}}{4 \times 10^{-2}}\] \[B_{net} = 30 \times 10^{-5}\text{ T}\]

To convert this to microteslas (μT), shift the decimal:

\[B_{net} = 300 \times 10^{-6}\text{ T}\]

Final Answer: 300 μT

Correct Option: B

Explanation

To find the magnetic field at the centroid of an equilateral triangle frame of side l carrying a current i, we use the Biot-Savart law.

Step 1: Magnetic Field Due to One Side of Triangle

For a current-carrying finite wire, the magnetic field at a perpendicular distance d from the center of the wire is:

B=μ0i4πd[sin⁡θ1+sin⁡θ2]B = \frac{\mu_0 i}{4 \pi d} \left[ \sin \theta_1 + \sin \theta_2 \right]

For an equilateral triangle, the perpendicular distance from the centroid to any side is:

d=l23​

Each side of the equilateral triangle subtends an angle θ = 60° at the centroid.

So,

sin⁡60∘=32\sin 60^\circ = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}​

Thus, the field due to one side:

Bone side=μ0i4π(l/23)(2×32)

​​Bone side=μ0i2π×3l×3

​ Bone side=μ0i2πl×3

Bone side=3μ0i2πl​

Step 2: Net Magnetic Field at Centroid

Since all three sides contribute equally to the field at the centroid and add up vectorially:

Btotal=3×Bone sideB_{\text{total}} = 3 \times B_{\text{one side}}

​ Btotal=3×3μ0i2πl

Btotal=9μ0i2πl​

Step 3: Comparing with Options

From the given options:

  • The correct answer is Option A: 9μ0i2πl​
Final Answer:

Option C: 9μ0i2πl​

Explanation

To find the magnetic field at the centroid of an equilateral triangle frame of side l carrying a current i, we use the Biot-Savart law.

Step 1: Magnetic Field Due to One Side of Triangle

For a current-carrying finite wire, the magnetic field at a perpendicular distance d from the center of the wire is:

B=μ0i4πd[sin⁡θ1+sin⁡θ2]B = \frac{\mu_0 i}{4 \pi d} \left[ \sin \theta_1 + \sin \theta_2 \right]

For an equilateral triangle, the perpendicular distance from the centroid to any side is:

d=l23​

Each side of the equilateral triangle subtends an angle θ = 60° at the centroid.

So,

sin⁡60∘=32\sin 60^\circ = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}​

Thus, the field due to one side:

Bone side=μ0i4π(l/23)(2×32)

​​Bone side=μ0i2π×3l×3

​ Bone side=μ0i2πl×3

Bone side=3μ0i2πl​

Step 2: Net Magnetic Field at Centroid

Since all three sides contribute equally to the field at the centroid and add up vectorially:

Btotal=3×Bone sideB_{\text{total}} = 3 \times B_{\text{one side}}

​ Btotal=3×3μ0i2πl

Btotal=9μ0i2πl​

Step 3: Comparing with Options

From the given options:

  • The correct answer is Option A: 9μ0i2πl​
Final Answer:

Option C: 9μ0i2πl​

Q32

Given below are two statements: one is labelled as Assertion A and the other is labelled as Reason R.
Assertion A : If oxygen ion O-2 and Hydrogen ion H+enter normal to the magnetic field with equal momentum, then the path of O-2 ion has a smaller curvature than that of H+.
Reason R : A proton with same linear momentum as an electron will form a path of smaller radius of curvature on entering a uniform magnetic field perpendicularly.
In the light of the above statement, choose the correct answer from the options given below

[JEE Main 2025, 3 Apr (Shift 2)]

a

A is true but R is false

b

Both A and R are true but R is NOT the correct explanation of A

c

A is false but R is true

d

Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

A is true but R is false

Explanation

Assertion A:

  • The formula for the radius of a charged particle in a uniform magnetic field is R=pqB.

  • Since momentum (\(p\)) and the magnetic field (\(B\)) are constant here, the radius is inversely proportional to the magnitude of the charge: R∝1|q|.

  • Charge magnitude of the O-2 ion is \(2e\). Charge magnitude of the H+ ion is 1e.

  • Comparing the two: RO=p2eB and RH=peB.

  • The oxygen ion clearly has a smaller radius (RO<RH).

  • Assertion A is True.

Reason R:

  • Now consider a proton and an electron entering the field with the same linear momentum \(p\).

  • Charge magnitude of a proton is \(e\). Charge magnitude of an electron is also \(e\).

  • Because \(p\), \(|q|\), and \(B\) are identical for both particles, their radii of curvature will be exactly the same (Rproton=Relectron).

  • Reason R incorrectly claims the proton has a smaller radius, making Reason R False.

Final Answer:

Option A: A is true but R is false.
