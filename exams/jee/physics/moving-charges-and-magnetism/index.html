A small cube of side 1 mm is placed at the centre of a circular loop of radius 10 cm carrying a current of 2 A.The magnetic energy stored inside the cube is α×10-14 J. The value of α is ______ .

μo=4π×10-7Tm/A,π=3.14

[JEE Main 2026, 6 Apr (Shift 1)]

a

6.28

b

6.28×10-6

c

628

d

6.28×10-4

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

6.28

Explanation

The magnetic field at the center of a circular loop :

B=μ0I2R


The energy stored per unit volume is:

uB=B22μ0


The total energy stored inside the cube is the product of the energy stored per unit volume to the volume of the cube.
U=uB× Volume


U=B22μ0×a3=μ0I28R2×a3
⇒U=4π×10-7×(2)28×(0.1)2×10-9
U=6.28×10-14J


The value of α is 6.28 .
