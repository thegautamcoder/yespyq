Q3

Statement-I : Velocity of sound in solids in more compared to that in gases.

Statement-II : Bulk modulus of gas is more than that of solids.

a

Statement-I is correct statement-II is correct

b

Statement-I is correct statement-II is incorrect

c

Statement-I is incorrect statement-II is correct

d

Statement-l is incorrect statement-II is incorrect

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

Statement-I is correct statement-II is incorrect

ExplanationStatement-I: Velocity of Sound in Solids is Greater than in Gases

  • The velocity of sound is given by:

    v=Bρv = \sqrt{\frac{B}{\rho}}

    where:

    • BB is the bulk modulus (measure of elasticity),
    • ρ\rho is density.

  • Solids have a much higher bulk modulus than gases, which increases the sound velocity.

  • In gases, BB is small, leading to a lower sound velocity.

Statement-I is Correct.

Statement-II: Bulk Modulus of Gas is More than that of Solids
  • The bulk modulus (BB) quantifies the resistance to compression.
  • Solids have a significantly higher bulk modulus than gases because they are less compressible.
  • Gases are highly compressible, so their bulk modulus is much smaller.

Statement-II is Incorrect.

Q7

A sinusoidal wave of wavelength  7.5 cm travels a distance of  1.2 cm along the x -direction in 0.3 sec . The crest P is at x = 0 at t=0sec and maximum displacement of the wave is 2 cm . Which equation correctly represents this wave ?

[JEE Main 2025, 2 Apr (Shift 2)]

a

y=2cos(0.83x-3.35t)cm

b

y=2sin(0.83x-3.5t)cm

c

y=2cos(3.35x-0.83t)cm

d

y=2cos(0.13x-0.5t)cm

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

y=2cos(0.83x-3.35t)cm

Explanation

Amplitude (\(A\)): The maximum displacement is given as 2 cm, so A = 2.

Wave Number (\(k\)):

\[k = \frac{2\pi}{\lambda} = \frac{2\pi}{7.5} \approx 0.83\text{ cm}^{-1}\]

Wave Velocity (\(v\)):

\[v = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Time}} = \frac{1.2}{0.3} = 4\text{ cm/s}\]

Angular Frequency (ω):

\[\omega = v \cdot k = 4 \times 0.837 \approx 3.35\text{ rad/s}\]

Wave Function: Since the crest (maximum positive displacement) is at x = 0 and t = 0, the wave starts at its peak. This corresponds perfectly to a cosine function with zero initial phase: y=Acos(kx-ωt).

Substituting the values into the standard equation:

\[y = 2 \cos(0.83x - 3.35t)\text{ cm}\]

Correct Option: A

Q15

In the resonance experiment, two air columns (closed at one end) of 100 cm and 120 cm long, give 15 beats per second when each one is sounding in the respective fundamental modes. The velocity of sound in the air column is :

a

335 m/s

b

370 m/s

c

340 m/s

d

360 m/s

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

360 m/s

Explanation

Fundamental frequency in close/organ pipe

$$
\begin{aligned}
& (f)=\frac{v}{4 \ell} \\
& f_1=\frac{v}{4 \ell_1} \& f_2=\frac{v}{4 \ell_2} \\
& \text { Beat }=\left(f_1-f_2\right)=\frac{v}{4}\left(\frac{1}{\ell_1}-\frac{1}{\ell_2}\right) \\
& 15=\frac{v}{4}\left(\frac{1}{1}-\frac{1}{1.2}\right) \\
& v=\left(\frac{15 \times 4 \times 1.2}{0.2}\right)=60 \times 6=360 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}
\end{aligned}
$$

Q16

Two harmonic waves moving in the same direction superimpose to form a wave x=acos(1.5t)cos(50.5t) where t is in seconds. Find the period with which they beat (close to nearest integer)

a

6 s

b

4 s

c

1 s

d

2 s

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

2 s

Explanation

Given displacement as product of two cosine functions representing beats.
x = a cos (1.5t) cos (50.5t)

Use trigonometric identity for product of cosines.
x = a 2 [ cos (49t) + cos (52t) ]

Angular frequencies of the two waves are identified.
Δω = 52 − 49 = 3 rad/s

Beat frequency is obtained from angular frequency difference.
Δf = 3 2π

Time period of beats is reciprocal of beat frequency.
Δt = 2π 3

Numerical evaluation of beat time period.
Δt ≈ 2 s

Q23

Consider the sound wave travelling in ideal gases of He,CH4, and CO2. All the gases have the same ratio Pρ, where P is the pressure and ρ is the density. The ratio of the speed of sound through the gases VHe:VCH4:VCO2 is given by

a

75:53:43

b

53:43:75

c

53:43:43

d

43:53:75

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

53:43:43

Explanation

Speed of sound in an ideal gas is given by the standard relation.
v = γ P ρ
Given that all gases have the same ratio of pressure to density, the speed depends only on the adiabatic index.
v ∝ γ
The adiabatic indices of the gases are identified from molecular structure.
γHe=53
γCH4=43
γCO2=75
Using proportionality, the ratio of speeds is written directly.
vHe:vCH4:vCO2=53:43:43
This corresponds to the required final answer.
53:43:43
