Q4

A convex lens made of glass (refractive index = 1.5 ) has focal length 24 cm in air. When it is totally immersed in water (refractive index = 1.33 ), its focal length changes to:

a

24 cm

b

72 cm

c

96 cm

d

48 cm

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

96 cm

Explanation

The focal length in air is given by the lens formula:

1f=μl-11R1-1R2

where μ, is the refractive index of the lens material ( 1.5 for glass), and R1 and R2 are the radii of curvature of the lens surfaces. For air, the focal length f = 24 cm, So, 124=(1.5-1)·2R

When immersed in water.

1f'=μlμs-11R1-1R2

Substituting the values for μl=1.5, μs=1.33 and the same R1,R2 from before:

1f'=1.51.33-12R

Dividing the two equations for the focal lengths in air and in water, we get:

f'24=1.5-11.51.33-1f' = 96 cm

Thus, the new focal length in water is:

f'=96cm

What is the relative decrease in focal length of a lens for an increase in optical power by 0.1 D from 2.5D? ['D' stands for dioptre]

[JEE Main 2025, 24 Jan (Shift 1)]

a

0.1

b

0.04

c

0.01

d

0.40

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

0.04

Explanation

The focal length F is the reciprocal of optical power P :

F=1P

When P =2.5 D,

F=12.5=25

When P increases to 2.6 D ,

F'=12.6=513

Relative decrease in focal length:

F-F'F=25-51325

Simplifying:

=26-2526=126≈0.04

the correct answer is 0.04 .
