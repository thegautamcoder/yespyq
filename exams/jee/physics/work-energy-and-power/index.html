Q2 FREE PREVIEW

Two bodies A and B of equal mass are suspended from two massless springs of spring constant k1 and k2, respectively. If the bodies oscillate vertically such that their amplitudes are equal, the ratio of the maximum velocity of A to the maximum velocity of B is

[JEE Main 2025, 29 Jan (Shift 2)]

a

k2k1

b

k1k2

c

k1k2

d

k2k1

✓ Correct answer: c)

k1k2

Explanation

the maximum velocity for a simple harmonic oscillator is given by:

V=Aω

The angular frequency ω of a mass-spring system is related to the spring constant k and the mass m by:

ω=km

Since the bodies have equal amplitudes, therefore,

V1V2=ω1ω2 V1V2=k1mk2m V1V2=k1k2
Option (c) is correct.

Q5 FREE PREVIEW

A small mirror of mass m is suspended by a massless thread of length l. Then the small angle through which the thread will be deflected when a short pulse of laser of energy E falls normal on the mirror
(c = speed of light in vacuum and g = acceleration due to gravity)

[JEE Main 2025, 4 Apr (Shift 1)]

a

θ=3E4mcgI

b

θ=Emcgl

c

θ=E2mcgI

d

θ=2Emcgl

✓ Correct answer: d)

θ=2Emcgl

Explanation

A laser pulse of energy E is incident normally and reflected from the mirror, so momentum change is twice that of incident light.
p=Ec
Change in momentum transferred to mirror is:
Δp=2Ec
Using impulse–momentum relation for the mirror:
mv=2Ec
Horizontal velocity acquired by the mirror is:
v=2Emc
The mirror then behaves as a pendulum of length l performing small oscillations.
Maximum angular deflection for small angles is given by:
θ=vgl
Substituting the value of v:
θ=2Emcgl
Final answer:
θ=2Emcgl
