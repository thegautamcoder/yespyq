Q2

Two bodies A and B of equal mass are suspended from two massless springs of spring constant k1 and k2, respectively. If the bodies oscillate vertically such that their amplitudes are equal, the ratio of the maximum velocity of A to the maximum velocity of B is

[JEE Main 2025, 29 Jan (Shift 2)]

a

k2k1

b

k1k2

k1k2

d

k2k1

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

✓ Correct answer: c)

k1k2

Explanation

the maximum velocity for a simple harmonic oscillator is given by:

V=Aω

The angular frequency ω of a mass-spring system is related to the spring constant k and the mass m by:

ω=km

Since the bodies have equal amplitudes, therefore,

V1V2=ω1ω2 V1V2=k1mk2m V1V2=k1k2
Option (c) is correct.

Q5

A small mirror of mass m is suspended by a massless thread of length l. Then the small angle through which the thread will be deflected when a short pulse of laser of energy E falls normal on the mirror
(c = speed of light in vacuum and g = acceleration due to gravity)

[JEE Main 2025, 4 Apr (Shift 1)]

a

θ=3E4mcgI

b

θ=Emcgl

c

θ=E2mcgI

d

θ=2Emcgl

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

θ=2Emcgl

Explanation

A laser pulse of energy E is incident normally and reflected from the mirror, so momentum change is twice that of incident light.
p=Ec
Change in momentum transferred to mirror is:
Δp=2Ec
Using impulse–momentum relation for the mirror:
mv=2Ec
Horizontal velocity acquired by the mirror is:
v=2Emc
The mirror then behaves as a pendulum of length l performing small oscillations.
Maximum angular deflection for small angles is given by:
θ=vgl
Substituting the value of v:
θ=2Emcgl
Final answer:
θ=2Emcgl

A force F⃗=x2yi^+y2j^\vec{F} = x^2 y \hat{i} + y^2 \hat{j}​ acts on a body. The body is moved from the point (0,0)(0, 0) to (4,2)(4, 2) along the line x+y=10x + y = 10. Find the work done by this force.

a

112 J112 \, \text{J}

b

−112 J-112 \, \text{J}

c

200 J200 \, \text{J}

d

−200 J-200 \, \text{J}

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

−112 J-112 \, \text{J}

Explanation

The work done by the force is given by:

W=∫F⃗⋅dr⃗,W = \int \vec{F} \cdot d\vec{r},

where dr⃗=dxi^+dyj^d\vec{r} = dx \hat{i} + dy \hat{j}​. Along x+y=10x + y = 10, we use y=10−xy = 10 - x and dy=−dxdy = -dx, resulting in:

F⃗⋅dr⃗=[x2(10−x)−(10−x)2]dx.\vec{F} \cdot d\vec{r} = \big[x^2(10 - x) - (10 - x)^2\big] dx.

Integrating from x=0x = 0 to x=4x = 4:

W=∫04[9x2−x3+20x−100]dx.W = \int_0^4 \big[9x^2 - x^3 + 20x - 100\big] dx.

After solving, we find:

W=−112 J.W = -112 \, \text{J}.

Thus, the work done by the force is −112 J-112 \, \text{J}

Q16

A projectile is projected at an angle of 60 degree with the horizontal with kinetic energy K. Find the kinetic energy of the projectile at the highest point.

(Shift - II Memory Based)

a

K/3

b

K/2

c

K/4

d

K/8

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

K/4

Explanation

  1. Initial Kinetic Energy:

    • The total initial kinetic energy (KKK) of the projectile is given as: K=12mv2K = \frac{1}{2} m v^2
    • where:
      • mm is the mass of the projectile.
      • vv is the initial velocity of the projectile.

  2. Velocity Components:

    • The initial velocity (vvv) can be resolved into horizontal and vertical components: vx=vcos⁡θ,vy=vsin⁡θv_x = v \cos \theta, \quad v_y = v \sin \theta
    • Here, θ=60∘\theta = 60^\circ

  3. At the Highest Point:

    • At the highest point, the vertical velocity (vyv_y) becomes zero due to gravity, but the horizontal velocity (vxv_x) remains constant throughout the motion because there is no acceleration in the horizontal direction.

    • The velocity at the highest point is purely horizontal:

      vhighest=vx=vcos⁡60∘v_\text{highest} = v_x = v \cos 60^\circ

      Since cos⁡60∘=12\cos 60^\circ = \frac{1}{2}

      vhighest=v2v_\text{highest} = \frac{v}{2}​

  4. Kinetic Energy at the Highest Point:

    • The kinetic energy at the highest point is due only to the horizontal velocity: Khighest=12mvhighest2K_\text{highest} = \frac{1}{2} m v_\text{highest}^2
    • Substituting vhighest=v2v_\text{highest} = \frac{v}{2}
    • Khighest=12m(v2)2K_\text{highest} = \frac{1}{2} m \left(\frac{v}{2}\right)^2
    • Khighest=12m⋅v24K_\text{highest} = \frac{1}{2} m \cdot \frac{v^2}{4}
    • ​ Khighest=14(12mv2)K_\text{highest} = \frac{1}{4} \left(\frac{1}{2} m v^2\right)
    • Since K=12mv2K = \frac{1}{2} m v^2,
    • we have: Khighest=K4K_\text{highest} = \frac{K}{4}​
Final Answer:

The kinetic energy of the projectile at the highest point is:

K4\boxed{\frac{K}{4}}​​

Correct Option: C. K/4K/4
