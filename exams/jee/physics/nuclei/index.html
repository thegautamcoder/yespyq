Two nuclei of mass number 3 combine with another nucleus of mass number 4 to yield a nucleus of mass number 10. If the binding energy per nucleon for the mass numbers 3,4 and 10 are 5.6 MeV, 7.4 MeV and 6.1 MeV, respectively, then in the process, ∆Mc2=____ MeV.

[04 April, 2026 (Shift-I)]

a

7.9

2.2

4.3

Explanation

2×3×5.6+4×7.4=33.6+29.6=63.2MeV

Q4

Energy released when two deuterons (H12) fuse to form a helium nucleus (He24) is :
(Given : Binding energy per nucleon of H12=1.1 MeV and binding energy per nucleon of He24=7.0 MeV )

[JEE Main 2025, 2 Apr (Shift 2)]

a

8.1 MeV

b

5.9 MeV 

c

23.6 MeV

d

26.8 MeV

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

23.6 MeV

Explanation


Binding energy per nucleon of deuteron \( \left(^2_1\text{H}\right) = 1.1\, \text{MeV} \)
Binding energy per nucleon of helium-4 \( \left(^4_2\text{He}\right) = 7.0\, \text{MeV} \)

Two deuterons \( \Rightarrow \) total nucleons = 2 × 2 = 4

\[
\text{BE}_{\text{reactants}} = 2 \times (1.1 \times 2) = 4.4\, \text{MeV}
\]

\[
\text{BE}_{\text{product}} = 4 \times 7.0 = 28.0\, \text{MeV}
\]

Q=BEproduct −BEreactants =28.0−4.4=23.6MeV

Q5

A radioactive nucleus \(\mathrm{n}_2\) has 3 times the decay constant as compared to the decay constant of another radioactive nucleus \(n_1\). If initial number of both nuclei are the same, what is the ratio of number of nuclei of \(n_2\) to the number of nuclei of \(n_1\), after one half-life of \(n_1\) ?

[JEE Main 2025, 23 Jan (Shift 1)]

a

8

b

4

c

18

d

14

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

14

Explanation

The number of remaining nuclei after time t can be given by:

N(t)=N0e−λt

For half-life t1/2 of n1, we know that:

t1/2=ln2λ1

At the end of one half-life, the number of n1 nuclei left will be:

N1=N02

For nucleus n2, which has a decay constant 3 times that of n1 the remaining number of nuclei will be:

N2=N0e-3λ1t

Since t=t1/2=ln2λ1, we substitute it into the equation for N2 :

N2=N0e-3λ1×ln2λ1=N0e-3ln2=N0123=N08


Now, the ratio of the number of nuclei of n2 to n1 after one half-life of n1 is:

N2N1=N08N02=14

Q6

The energy released if hydrogen atoms are combined to form He24 is _______ MeV.

(Take binding energies per nucleon of H12 and He24 as 1.1 MeV and 7.2 MeV, respectively)

[JEE Main 2026, 6 Apr (Shift 2)]

a

6.1

b

24.4

c

26.6

d

5

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

24.4

Explanation

Two deuterium atoms (H12) fuse to form one helium atom (He24):

\[2 \ ^2_1\text{H} \rightarrow \ ^4_2\text{He}\]

Calculate Total Binding Energies

Energy released (ΔE) is the difference between the total binding energy of the products and the reactants.

Total BE of Reactants (\(2\) deuterium atoms):

Each H12 has \(2\) nucleons.

\[BE_{\text{reactants}} = 2 \text{ atoms} \times (2 \text{ nucleons} \times 1.1\text{ MeV/nucleon}) = 4.4\text{ MeV}\]

Total BE of Product (\(1\) helium atom):

Each He24 has \(4\) nucleons.

\[BE_{\text{product}} = 1 \text{ atom} \times (4 \text{ nucleons} \times 7.2\text{ MeV/nucleon}) = 28.8\text{ MeV}\]

Calculate Energy Released (ΔE)

\[\Delta E = BE_{\text{product}} - BE_{\text{reactants}}\] \[\Delta E = 28.8\text{ MeV} - 4.4\text{ MeV}\] \[\Delta E = 24.4\text{ MeV}\]

Correct Option: (b) 24.4

Q13

The energy equivalent of 1 g of substance is :

[JEE Main 2024, 9 Apr (Shift 1)]

a

11.2×1024 MeV

b

5.6×1026 MeV

c

5.6×1012 MeV

d

5.6 eV

✓ Correct answer: b)

Explanation

Convert mass to kilograms:\(m=1\,\text{g}=1\times 10^{-3}\,\text{kg}\)

Calculate the energy:\(E=m\times c^{2}=(1\times 10^{-3}\,\text{kg})\times (9\times 10^{16}\,\text{m}^{2}/\text{s}^{2})=9\times 10^{13}\,\text{J}\) Other Units The energy can also be expressed in other units:

Approximately \(2.5\times 10^{7}\) kilowatt-hours (kWh).Approximately \(5.6\times 10^{26}\) mega-electron volts (MeV).
