A block of mass 1 kg , moving along x with speed vi=10 m/s enters a rough region ranging from x=0.1 m to x=1.9 m. The retarding force acting on the block in this range is Fr=-kxN, with k=10 N/m. Then the final speed of the block as it crosses rough region is

10 m/s

Explanation

3

4

Explanation

\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
&\begin{aligned}

\end{aligned}
\end{equation}

Q5

A body projected vertically upwards with a certain speed from the top of a tower reaches the ground in \(t_1\). If it is projected vertically downwards from the same point with the same speed, it reaches the ground in \(t_2\). Time required to reach the ground, if it is dropped from the top of the tower, is :

[JEE Main 2024, 06 Apr (Shift 2)]

a

t1t2

b

t1+t2

c

t1t2

d

t1-t2

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

t1t2

Explanation

Case 1: h=-ut1+12gt12  ( downward taken positive)

Case 2 h=ut2+12gt22

\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
& \text { } t_1=\frac{u+\sqrt{u^2+2 g h}}{g} \\
& t_2=\frac{-u+\sqrt{u^2+2 g h}}{g} \\
& \text{Case 3}\\ t=\frac{\sqrt{2 g h}}{g} \\
& t_1 t_2=\frac{\left(u^2+2 g h\right)-u^2}{g^2}=\frac{2 g h}{g^2}=t^2 \\
& \Rightarrow t=\sqrt{t_1 t_2}
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}

Q10

Two cars are travelling towards each other at speed of \(20 m s ^{-1}\) each. When the cars are \(300 m\) apart, both the drivers apply brakes and the cars retard at the rate of \(2 m s ^{-2}\). The distance between them when they come to rest is :

[JEE Main 2024, 09 Apr (Shift 2)]

a

50 m

b

25 m

c

100 m

d

200m

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

100 m

Explanation

Given:
- Speed of each car: \(u=20 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}\)
- Retardation: \(a=2 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}^2\)
- Initial separation: 300 m

Step 1: Find distance travelled by one car before stopping
Using:

v2=u2+2as⇒0=202-2·2·s400=4s⇒s=100 m

Each car travels 100 m , so total distance covered before stopping:

100+100=200 m

Step 2: Remaining distance between them:

\[
300-200=100 \mathrm{~m}
\]


Answer: 100 m

Q17

A satellite is nine times closer to earth compared to moon. Time period of moon is 27 days then period of satellite is

(Shift - II Memory Based)

a

3 days

b

9 days

c

1 days

d

33 days

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

1 days

Explanation

Sol. The time period of an orbiting body is related to its distance from the central body (Earth in this case) by Kepler’s third law, which states:

T2∝R3T^2 \propto R^3

Where:

  • TT = time period
  • RR = distance from the central body

Given:

RsatelliteRmoon=19(satellite is nine times closer to Earth)\frac{R_{\text{satellite}}}{R_{\text{moon}}} = \frac{1}{9} \quad \text{(satellite is nine times closer to Earth)}

Let TmoonT_{\text{moon}} and TsatelliteT_{\text{satellite}}​ be the time periods of the Moon and the satellite, respectively. Using Kepler's law:

(TsatelliteTmoon)2=(RsatelliteRmoon)3\left( \frac{T_{\text{satellite}}}{T_{\text{moon}}} \right)^2 = \left( \frac{R_{\text{satellite}}}{R_{\text{moon}}} \right)^3

Substitute values:

(Tsatellite27)2=(19)3\left( \frac{T_{\text{satellite}}}{27} \right)^2 = \left( \frac{1}{9} \right)^3

Simplify:

(Tsatellite27)2=1729\left( \frac{T_{\text{satellite}}}{27} \right)^2 = \frac{1}{729} ​ Tsatellite27=127  ⟹  Tsatellite=27×127=1 day

Correct answer: (a) 1 day

Q22

Two cars A and B are moving in the same direction along a straight line with speeds 100 km/h and 80 km/h, respectively such that car A is moving ahead of car B. A person in car B throws a stone with a speed v so that it hits the car A with a speed of 5 m/s. The value of v is _____ km/h.

[JEE Main 2026, 4 Apr (Shift 2)]

a

18

b

28

c

38

d

48

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

38

Explanation

Given:

vA=100 km/h

vB=80 km/h

Relative speed of hit

vsA=5 m/s=5×185=18 km/h

Relative Velocity Equations:
Let vs be the velocity of the stone relative to the ground and v be its velocity relative to car B.

vs=vB+v=80+v

The speed with which the stone hits car Ais:

vsA=vs-vA

18 = (80 + v) – 100

18 = v – 20

v = 38km / h
