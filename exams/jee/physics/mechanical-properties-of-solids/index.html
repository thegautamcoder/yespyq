Young's modulus is determined by the equation given by \(Y =49000 \frac{ M }{ l } \frac{\text { dyne }}{ cm ^2}\) where \(M\) is the mass and \(l\) is the extension of wire used in the experiment. Now error in Young modulus \((Y)\) is estimated by taking data from \(M-l\) plot in graph paper. The smallest scale divisions are \(5 g\) and \(0.02 cm\) along load axis and extension axis respectively. If the value of \(M\) and \(l\) are \(500 g\) and \(2 cm\) respectively then percentage error of \(Y\) is :

[JEE Main 2024, 08 Apr (Shift 1)]

a

\(0.5 \%\)

b

\(0.02 \%\)

c

\(0.2 \%\)

d

\(2\%\)

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

\(2\%\)

Explanation

Given Values

Mass (\(M\)): 500 g

Extension (\(l\)): 2 cm

Least Count of \(M\) (ΔM): 5 g (smallest scale division along load axis)

Least Count of \(l\) (Δl): 0.02 cm (smallest scale division along extension axis)

Calculation of Percentage Error

\[\text{Percentage Error} = \left( \frac{\Delta M}{M} + \frac{\Delta l}{l} \right) \times 100\%\] \[\text{Percentage Error} = \left( \frac{5}{500} + \frac{0.02}{2} \right) \times 100\%\] \[\text{Percentage Error} = (0.01 + 0.01) \times 100\%\] \[\text{Percentage Error} = 0.02 \times 100\% = 2\%\]

Correct Option: D (2%)

Q8

A cube of side 10 cm having bulk modulus of \(1.4 \times 10^{11} \mathrm{~Pa}\) is placed in atmosphere. Now it is subjected to extra pressure of \(7 \times 10^6 \mathrm{~Pa}\) then magnitude of change in volume of cube is

(Shift - II Memory Based)

a

\(\quad 0.03 \mathrm{~mL}\)

b

\(\quad 0.3 \mathrm{~mL}\)

c

\(\quad 0.05 \mathrm{~mL}\)

d

\(\quad 0.2 \mathrm{~mL}\)

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

\(\quad 0.05 \mathrm{~mL}\)

Explanation

We use the formula for bulk modulus (B):

B=Pressure Change(ΔP)ΔVVB = \frac{\text{Pressure Change} (\Delta P)}{\frac{\Delta V}{V}}​

where:

  • B = 1.4 × 10¹¹ Pa (Bulk modulus)
  • ΔP = 7 × 10⁶ Pa (Applied pressure)
  • V = a³ = (10 cm)³ = (0.1 m)³ = 10⁻³ m³ (Initial volume of cube)

Rearranging for ΔV:

ΔV=ΔPB×V\Delta V = \frac{\Delta P}{B} \times V

Substituting the values:

ΔV=(7×106)(1.4×1011)×(10−3)\Delta V = \frac{(7 \times 10^6)}{(1.4 \times 10^{11})} \times (10^{-3})

ΔV=71.4×10−8\Delta V = \frac{7}{1.4} \times 10^{-8}

ΔV=5×10−8 m³\Delta V = 5 \times 10^{-8} \text{ m³}

Converting to mL:

ΔV=0.05 mL\Delta V = 0.05 \text{ mL}

Final Answer:

Correct option: (C) 0.05 mL

Q16

When a force of 5N is applied extension in the spring is found to be x1, extension becomes x2 when the force applied is 7N. Calculate the tension in spring when extension is 5x1-2x2

(Shift - II Memory Based)

a

11N

b

12N

c

22N

d

24N

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

11N

Explanation

The relationship between force (FF) and extension (xx) in a spring follows Hooke’s law:

F=kxF = kx

Where:

  • kk is the spring constant.
  • FF is the applied force.
  • xx is the extension.
  1. From the given data:
    For F=5 NF = 5 \, \text{N}:

5=kx1⇒k=5x15 = kx_1 \quad \Rightarrow \quad k = \frac{5}{x_1}​

For F=7 NF = 7 \, \text{N}:

7=kx2⇒k=7x27 = kx_2 \quad \Rightarrow \quad k = \frac{7}{x_2}​

Equating kk:

5x1=7x2⇒x2=75x1\frac{5}{x_1} = \frac{7}{x_2} \quad \Rightarrow \quad x_2 = \frac{7}{5}x_1

  1. Find the tension for the extension 5x1−2x25x_1 - 2x_2
    Substitute x2=75x1x_2 = \frac{7}{5}x_1

5x1−2x2=5x1−2(75x1)=5x1−145x1=25x1−14x15=11x155x_1 - 2x_2 = 5x_1 - 2 \left(\frac{7}{5}x_1\right) = 5x_1 - \frac{14}{5}x_1 = \frac{25x_1 - 14x_1}{5} = \frac{11x_1}{5}​​

The corresponding force (tension) is:

F=kx=k⋅11x15F = kx = k \cdot \frac{11x_1}{5}​​

Substitute k=5x1k = \frac{5}{x_1}:

F=5x1⋅11x15=11 NF = \frac{5}{x_1} \cdot \frac{11x_1}{5} = 11 \, \text{N}

Correct answer: (a) 11N

Q19

Given below are two statements : one is labelled as Assertion (A) and the other is labelled as Reason (R).
Assertion (A) : The property of body, by virtue of which it tends to regain its original shape when the external force is removed, is Elasticity.

Reason (R) : The restoring force depends upon the bonded inter atomic and inter molecular force of solid.

In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below :

[JEE Main 2024, 27 Jan (Shift 2)]

a

Both (A) and (R) are true but (R) is not the correct explanation of (A)

b

Both \(( A )\) and \(( R )\) are true and \(( R )\) is the correct explanation of \(( A )\)

c

(A) is false but (R) is true

d

(A) is true but (R) is false

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

Both (A) and (R) are true but (R) is not the correct explanation of (A)

Explanation

Assertion (A) is a definition of elasticity (a macroscopic property).
Reason (R) describes the microscopic origin of restoring force, not the definition of elasticity itself.
Elasticity refers to the ability to regain original shape, whereas the restoring force is a consequence of elasticity, not its explanation.
So, although both statements are true, R does not directly explain A.

5x1−2x2=5x1−2(75x1)=5x1−145x1=25x1−14x15=11x155x_1 - 2x_2 = 5x_1 - 2 \left(\frac{7}{5}x_1\right) = 5x_1 - \frac{14}{5}x_1 = \frac{25x_1 - 14x_1}{5} = \frac{11x_1}{5}​​

The corresponding force (tension) is:

F=kx=k⋅11x15F = kx = k \cdot \frac{11x_1}{5}​​

Substitute k=5x1k = \frac{5}{x_1}:

F=5x1⋅11x15=11 NF = \frac{5}{x_1} \cdot \frac{11x_1}{5} = 11 \, \text{N}
