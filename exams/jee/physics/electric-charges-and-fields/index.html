The electric flux is ϕ=ασ+βλ where \(\lambda\) and \(\sigma\) are linear and surface charge density, respectively. αβ represents

✓ Correct answer: b)

displacement

Explanation

The electric flux \(\phi\) has dimensions of charge per unit area, i.e.,:

[ϕ]=Q Area 

a is associated with surface charge density s , which has the dimensions:

[σ]=Q Area 

Thus, the dimensions of a will be:

[α]=ϕσ=Q/ Area Q/ Area =1

Next, for 1 , the linear charge density, we have:

[λ]=Q Length

ExplanationStep 1: Understanding the System
  • A charged particle of charge Q and mass mmm is suspended from a string of length ll.
  • It is placed in a uniform electric field E​.
  • Gravity is ignored.
  • The particle will undergo small oscillations due to the torque exerted by the electric field.
Step 2: Torque and Restoring Force

  • The electric force acting on the charge is:

    F=QE​

  • The torque about the hinge when displaced by an angle θ\theta is:

    τ=QE⋅lsin⁡θ

  • For small angles, using sin⁡θ≈θ\sin \theta \approx \theta

  • Comparing with the standard equation for simple harmonic motion:

    τ=−kθ\tau = - k \theta

    k=QEl
Step 3: Equation of Motion

  • The equation for angular motion:

    Iα=−kθI \alpha = - k \thetaI

    ml2d2θdt2=−QElθ

    This is the standard form of SHM with angular frequency ω\omega:

    ω=QEml​
Step 4: Finding Period

The period is given by:

T=2πω=2πmlQE​

Step 5: Selecting the Correct Option

The correct option is:

AT=2πmlQE\boxed{A} \quad T = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{m l}{Q E}}​
