Q1 FREE PREVIEW

The electric flux is ϕ=ασ+βλ where \(\lambda\) and \(\sigma\) are linear and surface charge density, respectively. αβ represents

[JEE Main 2025, 23 Jan (Shift 1)]

a

charge

b

displacement

c

area

d

electric field

✓ Correct answer: b)

displacement

Explanation

The electric flux \(\phi\) has dimensions of charge per unit area, i.e.,:

[ϕ]=Q Area 

a is associated with surface charge density s , which has the dimensions:

[σ]=Q Area 

Thus, the dimensions of a will be:

[α]=ϕσ=Q/ Area Q/ Area =1

Next, for 1 , the linear charge density, we have:

[λ]=Q Length 

Since b is associated with 1 , the dimensions of b are:

[β]=ϕλ=Q/ Area Q/ Length = Length  Area 

Now, considering the ratio αβ, we find:

αβ=1 Length  Area = Length 

Thus, the ratio αβ represents length.
