Q1

A car of \(800 kg\) is taking turn on a banked road of radius \(300 m\) and angle of banking \(30^{\circ}\). If coefficient of static friction is 0.2 then the maximum speed with which car can negotiate the turn safely : \(\left( g =10 m / s ^2, \sqrt{3}=1.73\right)\)

[JEE Main 2024, 06 Apr (Shift 2)]

a

\(264 m / s\)

b

\(51.4 m / s\)

c

\(102.8 m / s\)

d

\(70.4 m / s\)

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

\(51.4 m / s\)

Explanation

m=800 kg
r=300 m
θ=30°
μs=0.2
Vmax=Rgtanθ+μ1-μtanθ
=300×g×tan30°+0.21-0.2×tan30°
=300×10×0.57+0.21-0.2×0.57
Vmax=51.4 m/s

Q7

An artillery piece of mass \(M_1\) fires a shell of mass \(M_2\) horizontally. Instantaneously after the firing, the ratio of kinetic energy of the artillery and that of the shell is:

[JEE Main 2024, 31 Jan (Shift 1)]

a

\(\frac{M_1}{M_2}\)

b

\(\frac{M_2}{M_1}\)

c

\(M_1 /\left(M_1+M_2\right)\)

d

\(M_2 /\left(M_1+M_2\right)\)

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

\(\frac{M_2}{M_1}\)

Explanation

\(\begin{aligned} & \left|\overrightarrow{p_1}\right|=\left|\overrightarrow{p_2}\right| \\ & K E=\frac{P^2}{2 M} ; p \text { same } \\ & K E \propto \frac{1}{m} \\ & \frac{K E_1}{K E_2}=\frac{p^2 / 2 M_1}{p^2 / 2 M_2}=\frac{M_2}{M_1}\end{aligned}\)

Q10

An object with mass 500 g moves along x -axis with speed v=4x m/s. The force acting on the object is :

[JEE Main 2025, 7 Apr (Shift 2)]

a

8 N

b

5 N

c

6 N

d

4 N

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

4 N

Explanation

$$
\begin{aligned}
& \mathrm{F}=\mathrm{M} \times \mathrm{a} \\
& v=4 \sqrt{x} \\
& v^2=16 x \\
& 2 v \frac{d v}{d x}=16 \\
& \frac{\mathrm{vdv}}{\mathrm{dx}}=\frac{16}{2}=8 \\
& \mathrm{~F}=0.5 \times 8=4 \mathrm{~N}
\end{aligned}
$$

Q11

A balloon of mass M is ascending with an acceleration a. What fraction of its mass should be dropped so that it starts moving upward with an acceleration of 3 a ?

(Shift - II Memory Based)

a

\(\frac{2 a}{g+3 a}\)

b

\(\frac{a}{g+2 a}\)

c

\(\frac{2 a M}{g+3 a}\)

d

\(\frac{a M}{g+2 a}\)

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

\(\frac{2 a M}{g+3 a}\)

Explanation

Let:

  • MM be the initial mass of the balloon.
  • TT be the tension (upward force) acting on the balloon.
Step 1: Initial Equation of Motion

Using Newton’s second law for the initial state:

T−Mg=MaT - Mg = Ma

T=M(g+a)T = M(g + a)

Step 2: After Dropping Mass

Let mm be the mass that is dropped. The new mass of the balloon becomes (M−m)(M - m). The equation of motion after dropping mass:

T−(M−m)g=(M−m)⋅3aT - (M - m)g = (M - m) \cdot 3a

Substituting T=M(g+a)T = M(g + a):

M(g+a)−(M−m)g=(M−m)⋅3aM(g + a) - (M - m)g = (M - m) \cdot 3a

Expanding:

Mg+Ma−Mg+mg=3a(M−m)Mg + Ma - Mg + mg = 3a(M - m)

Ma+mg=3aM−3amMa + mg = 3aM - 3am

Rearrange terms:

Ma−3aM=−mg−3amMa - 3aM = - mg - 3am

Ma−3aM=−m(g+3a)Ma - 3aM = -m(g + 3a)

Step 3: Solve for mm

m=2aMg+3am = \frac{2aM}{g + 3a}​

Answer:

The mass that should be dropped is 2aMg+3a\frac{2aM}{g+3a}.
