Q26

The energy in a system varies with position and time as E(x, t) = x3  e–βt , where β = 0.3 sec–1. Given that the P% error in x = 1.2 % and that the % error in t = 1.6%. Find the maximum % error in E at t = 5 sec.

(Shift - II Memory Based)

a

1%

b

2%

c

3%

d

6%

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: d)

6%

Explanation

The formula for percentage error propagation in a function is:

ΔE%=∣∂E∂x∣Δx%+∣∂E∂t∣Δt%\Delta E_{\%} = \left| \frac{\partial E}{\partial x} \right| \Delta x_{\%} + \left| \frac{\partial E}{\partial t} \right| \Delta t_{\%}​

Given:

E(x,t)=x3e−βtE(x, t) = x^3 e^{-\beta t} Step 1: Find percentage errors due to xx and tt

  1. For x3x^3: The percentage error for x3x^3 is:

    ΔE% x=3⋅Δx%\Delta E_{\% \, x} = 3 \cdot \Delta x_{\%}​

    Substituting Δx%=1.2%\Delta x_{\%} = 1.2\%

    ΔE% x=3⋅1.2=3.6%\Delta E_{\% \, x} = 3 \cdot 1.2 = 3.6\%

  2. For e−βte^{-\beta t}: The percentage error due to ttt comes from the exponential term. Using:

    ΔE% t=β⋅t⋅Δt%\Delta E_{\% \, t} = \beta \cdot t \cdot \Delta t_{\%}​

    Substituting β=0.3 s−1,t=5 s,Δt%=1.6%\beta = 0.3 \, \text{s}^{-1}, t = 5 \, \text{s}, \Delta t_{\%} = 1.6\%

    ΔE% t=0.3⋅5⋅1.6=2.4%\Delta E_{\% \, t} = 0.3 \cdot 5 \cdot 1.6 = 2.4\%
Step 2: Combine percentage errors

The total percentage error in EEE is:

ΔE%=ΔE% x+ΔE% t\Delta E_{\%} = \Delta E_{\% \, x} + \Delta E_{\% \, t}​

Substituting the values:

ΔE%=3.6+2.4=6.0%\Delta E_{\%} = 3.6 + 2.4 = 6.0\%

Correct answer: (d) 6%
