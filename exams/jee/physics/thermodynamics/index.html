Q1
500 J of energy is transferred as heat to 0.5 mol of Argon gas at 298 K and 1.00 atm . The final temperature and the change in internal energy respectively are (Given : ) [JEE Main 2025, 29 Jan (Shift 1)]
a378 K and 300 J
b348 K and 300 J
c368 K and 500 J
d378 K and 500 J
🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

✓ Correct answer: b) 348 K and 300 J
ExplanationArgon is a monoatomic ideal gas At constant pressure: Q = nCₚΔT For monoatomic gas: Cₚ = (5/2)R Q = n × (5/2)R × ΔT 500 = 0.5 × (5/2) × 8.3 × ΔT (5/2) × 8.3 = 20.75 500 = 0.5 × 20.75 × ΔT 500 = 10.375 × ΔT ΔT = 500 / 10.375 ≈ 48 K T₂ = 298 + 48 = 346 K ≈ 348 K For monoatomic gas: ΔU = n × (3/2)R × ΔT = 0.5 × (3/2 × 8.3) × 48 = 0.5 × 12.45 × 48 ≈ 300 J Final Answer: 348 K and 300 J

Q4

In a process, the pressure of a gas is directly proportional to its temperature. Choose the correct options:

A: The process is isochoric.
B: Work done in the process is zero.
C: Internal energy increases with an increase in temperature.

(Shift I - Memory Based)

a

A and B are correct.

b

A and C are correct.

c

A,B, and C are correct.

d

B and C are correct.

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

A,B, and C are correct.

ExplanationSolution:

  1. Condition:

    • If pressure P∝TP \propto T, this implies: P=kT(where k is constant).P = kT \quad \text{(where \( k \) is constant)}.
    • This relationship is only valid if the volume VV is constant, as per the ideal gas law:
    • PV=nRT  ⟹  P∝T(if V is constant).PV = nRT \quad \implies \quad P \propto T \quad \text{(if \( V \) is constant)}..
    • Thus, the process is isochoric (the statement AA is correct).

  2. Work Done:

    • In an isochoric process, the volume remains constant (ΔV=0\Delta V = 0), so the work done: W=PΔV=0.W = P \Delta V = 0. Hence, BB is also correct.

  3. Internal Energy:

    • The internal energy UU of an ideal gas depends on temperature: ΔU=nCvΔT.\Delta U = n C_v \Delta T.. Since temperature increases, internal energy also increases. Thus, CC is correct.

  4. Conclusion:

    • All statements AA, BB, and CC are correct.

Answer: (3) A,B,A, B, and CC are correct.

Q7

Pressure of an ideal gas, contained in a closed vessel, is increased by 0.4% when heated by 1°C. Its initial temperature must be ;

[JEE Main 2025, 3 Apr (Shift 2)]

a

25°C

b

2500 K

c

250 K

d

250°C

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

250 K

Explanation

For a closed vessel, the volume is constant. According to the ideal gas law (PV = nRT), at constant volume, pressure is directly proportional to temperature in Kelvin (P∝T).

Therefore, the fractional change is:

\[ \frac{\Delta P}{P} = \frac{\Delta T}{T} \]

Given the values from the problem:

Percentage change in pressure: ΔPP=0.4%=0.4100

Change in temperature: ΔT=1°C=1 K (Note: A change of 1°C is exactly equal to a change of 1 K)

Substituting these values into the equation:

\[ \frac{0.4}{100} = \frac{1}{T} \] \[ T = \frac{100}{0.4} \] \[ T = 250\text{ K} \]

Correct Option: C
