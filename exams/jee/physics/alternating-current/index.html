35

c

78

34

✓ Correct answer: c)

Explanation

(c) Current in series (RC) circuit is at frequency (ω):

Q13

In a series LCR circuit, inductance L = 10 mH and capacitance C = 10 nF. The angular frequency of the source when current has maximum amplitude in the circuit is

(Shift - II Memory Based)

a

1042πrad/s

b

1052πrad/s

c

105 rad/s

d

105 rad/s

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

105 rad/s

Explanation

The current has maximum amplitude in an LCR circuit at resonance frequency (ω0\omega_0), where:

ω0=1L⋅C\omega_0 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{L \cdot C}}​

Given:

L=10 mH=10×10−3 H, C=10 nF=10×10−9 FL = 10 \, \text{mH} = 10 \times 10^{-3} \, \text{H}, \, C = 10 \, \text{nF} = 10 \times 10^{-9} \, \text{F}

Substitute the values into the formula:

ω0=1(10×10−3)(10×10−9)\omega_0 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{(10 \times 10^{-3})(10 \times 10^{-9})}}​

Simplify:

ω0=110×10−12=110−11=105 rad/s\omega_0 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{10 \times 10^{-12}}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{10^{-11}}} = 10^5 \, \text{rad/s}

Hence, the angular frequency of the source is:
105 rad/s10^5 \, \text{rad/s}

Correct option: (c).

Q16

A series L.R circuit connected with an ac source E=(25sin1000t)V has a power factor of12. If the source of emf is changed to

E =(20sin2000t)V, the new power factor of the circuit will be :

a

17

b

15

c

12

d

13

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

15

Explanation

Initial power factor (cosϕ1):

RR2+(ω1L)2=12

Squaring both sides:

R2R2+(ω1L)2=12

2R2=R2+(ω1L)2⇒R=ω1L

New frequency (ω2):

ω1=1000, ω2=2000⇒ω2=2ω1

New inductive reactance (XL2):

XL2=ω2L=2ω1L=2R

New power factor (cosϕ2):

cosϕ2=RR2+XL22

cosϕ2=RR2+(2R)2=RR2+4R2

cosϕ2=RR5=15
