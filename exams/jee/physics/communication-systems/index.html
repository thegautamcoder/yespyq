Q1 FREE PREVIEW

A transmitting antenna is kept on the surface of the earth. The minimum height of receiving antenna required to receive the signal in line of sight at 4km distance from it is x×10-2 m. The value of x is (Let. radius of earth R= 6400 km )

[JEE Main 2023, 11 Apr (Shift 1)]

a

125

b

12.5

c

1.25

d

1250

✓ Correct answer: a)

125

Explanation

Given, dr=4 km
For receiving antenna. dr=2hrR∴hr=dr22R
 R=6400 km=6.4×106 m=4×103226.4×106 m=10.8m=1.25 m=125×10-2 m

Q2 FREE PREVIEW

The modulation index of an A.M. wave having maximum and minimum peak to peak voltages of \(14 mV\) and \(6 mV\) respectively is:

[JEE Main 2023, 29 Jan (Shift 2)]

a

1.4

b

0.4

c

0.2

d

0.6

✓ Correct answer: b)

0.4

Explanation

\(\begin{aligned} & \because \mu=\frac{A_{\max }-A_{\min }}{A_{\max }+A_{\min }} \\ & \Rightarrow \mu=\frac{14-6}{14+6}=0.4\end{aligned}\)
