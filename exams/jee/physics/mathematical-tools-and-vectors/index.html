[JEE Main 2021, 24 Feb (Shift 1)]

\(|\vec{A}-\vec{B}|=2 R \cos \left(\frac{\theta}{2}\right)\)

b

\(|\vec{A}+\vec{B}|=2 R \cos \left(\frac{\theta}{2}\right)\)

c

\(|\vec{A}-\vec{B}|=\sqrt{2} R \sin \left(\frac{\theta}{2}\right)\)

d

\(|\vec{A}+\vec{B}|=2 R \sin \left(\frac{\theta}{2}\right)\)
