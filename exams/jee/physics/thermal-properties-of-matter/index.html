k=213tk = \frac{2}{13t}​ Second Case: Cooling from 80∘C80^\circ C to 60∘C60^\circ C

Explanation

The total energy required for the bullet to reach its melting point and completely melt is given by:

Q=mcΔT+mL,Q = mc \Delta T + mL,

where:

  • Q=625 JQ = 625 \, \text{J}
  • c=0.442 J/g⋅∘Cc = 0.442 \, \text{J/g} \cdot ^{\circ}\text{C}
  • T = 600 - 300 = 300 K
  • L=247 J/gL = 247 \, \text{J/g}.

Substitute these values:

625=m(0.442⋅300)+m(247).625 = m (0.442 \cdot 300) + m (247).

625=m(132.6+247).625 = m (132.6 + 247).

625=m⋅379.6.625 = m \cdot 379.6.

Solve for mm:

m=625379.6≈1.65 g.m = \frac{625}{379.6} \approx 1.65 \, \text{g}.
