Q3

A wire of resistance 9 Ω9 \, \Omega is bent into the shape of an equilateral triangle. What is the equivalent resistance between any two vertices of the triangle?

(Shift I Memory Based)

a

1 Ω1 \, \Omega

b

2 Ω2 \, \Omega

c

3 Ω3 \, \Omega

d

4 Ω4 \, \Omega

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

2 Ω2 \, \Omega

Explanation
  • Total resistance of the wire: Rtotal=9 ΩR_{\text{total}} = 9 \, \Omega
  • Resistance of each side: Rside=Rtotal3=3 ΩR_{\text{side}} = \frac{R_{\text{total}}}{3} = 3 \, \Omega
  • Between any two vertices, there are two paths:
    1. Direct resistance R1=3 ΩR_1 = 3 \, \Omega.
    2. The other two sides (R2R_2) add up: R2=6 ΩR_2 = 6 \, \Omega
  • These are in parallel: Req=R1R2R1+R2=(3)(6)3+6=2 Ω.R_{\text{eq}} = \frac{R_1 R_2}{R_1 + R_2} = \frac{(3)(6)}{3 + 6} = 2 \, \Omega.

Thus, the equivalent resistance is 2 Ω2 \, \Omega.

Statement I: When non-ideal batteries are connected in parallel, the resultant EMF is less than either of the batteries.

Statement II: When non-ideal batteries are connected in parallel, the resultant resistance of their internal resistances is smaller than either of the individual resistances.

Choose the correct option which is given below:(JEE Mains - 21 Jan 2025 - Shift I Memory Based)

a

Statement I is true and Statement II is false.

b

Statement I is false and Statement II is true.

c

Both the Statements are true.

d

Both the Statements are false.

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: b)

Statement I is false and Statement II is true.

Explanation

Sol.

  • Statement I: This is false. When non-ideal batteries are connected in parallel, the resultant EMF is a weighted average of the EMFs of the individual batteries depending on their internal resistances. It does not necessarily become less than either battery's EMF.

  • Statement II: This is true. When internal resistances are connected in parallel, the resultant resistance is always smaller than the smallest individual resistance, as per the formula for parallel resistance:

    1Rtotal=1R1+1R2.\frac{1}{R_{\text{total}}} = \frac{1}{R_1} + \frac{1}{R_2}.

Thus, the correct answer is (b)\text{(b)}.

Q20

A wire of resistance \(R\) and length \(L\) is cut into 5 equal parts. If these parts are joined parallely, then resultant resistance will be :

a

\(25 R\)

b

\(5 R\)

c

\(\frac{1}{25} R\)

d

\(\frac{1}{5} R\)

🔒
Answer & explanation — PYQ Pass

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: c)

\(\frac{1}{25} R\)

Explanation

When resistors are connected in parallel, the reciprocal of the equivalent resistance is the sum of the reciprocals of the individual resistances. For 5 equal resistors in parallel, the formula is:


\(\frac{1}{R_{e q}}=\frac{1}{R_1}+\frac{1}{R_2}+\frac{1}{R_3}+\frac{1}{R_4}+\frac{1}{R_5}\)


Since all 5 parts have the same resistance \(\boldsymbol{R}^{\prime}\), the formula becomes:


\(\frac{1}{R_{e q}}=\frac{1}{R^{\prime}}+\frac{1}{R^{\prime}}+\frac{1}{R^{\prime}}+\frac{1}{R^{\prime}}+\frac{1}{R^{\prime}}=\frac{5}{R^{\prime}}\)

\(R_{e q}=\frac{R^{\prime}}{5}\)

\(\begin{aligned}
&R_{e q}=\frac{R / 5}{5}=\frac{R}{25}
\end{aligned}\)

✓ Correct answer: a)

Explanation

When a galvanometer is converted to an ammeter, a shunt resistance SS is connected in parallel with the galvanometer. The shunt allows most of the current to bypass the galvanometer.

  1. Full-scale current through galvanometer (IgI_g):

    Ig=20 mA=0.02 AI_g = 20 \, \text{mA} = 0.02 \, \text{A}

  2. Maximum current through the ammeter (II):

    I=3 AI = 3 \, \text{A}I=3A

  3. Current through the shunt (IsI_s​):

    Is=I−Ig=3−0.02=2.98 AI_s = I - I_g = 3 - 0.02 = 2.98 \, \text{A}

  4. Relationship between shunt resistance and galvanometer resistance:
    Since SS and GG are in parallel, the voltage across both is the same:

    IsS=IgGI_s S = I_g G

    Substituting the known values:

    2.98S=0.02×302.98 S = 0.02 \times 302.98S=0.62.98 S = 0.6

  5. Solve for SS:

    S=0.62.98≈0.2 ΩS = \frac{0.6}{2.98} \approx 0.2 \, \Omega

Thus, the value of the shunt resistance is:

0.2 Ω\boxed{0.2 \, \Omega}​

    2.98S=0.02×302.98 S = 0.02 \times 302.98S=0.62.98 S = 0.6

  5. Solve for SS:

    S=0.62.98≈0.2 ΩS = \frac{0.6}{2.98} \approx 0.2 \, \Omega

Thus, the value of the shunt resistance is:

0.2 Ω\boxed{0.2 \, \Omega}​

Explanation

Let the internal resistance of the voltmeter be x.

Suppose its full-scale current is I.

Then, 20=I x

To increase the range to 30 V , let additional resistance R be connected in series. Then, 30=I(x+R)

using I=20x

30=20x(x+R)

R=x2

This extra resistance must be connected in series.
