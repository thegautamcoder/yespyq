✓ Correct answer: a)

λe=h2mc1λi-1λ0

Explanation

For photoemission,
\[
hf_i=\phi+K_{\max}, \qquad \phi=\frac{hc}{\lambda_0}, \qquad f_i=\frac{c}{\lambda_i}.
\]
Hence the kinetic energy of emitted electrons is
\[
K_{\max}=hc\!\left(\frac{1}{\lambda_i}-\frac{1}{\lambda_0}\right).
\]

Using de Broglie and non-relativistic relations,
\[
K_{\max}=\frac{p^2}{2m}, \qquad \lambda_e=\frac{h}{p}.
\]
Therefore
\[
p=\sqrt{2mK_{\max}}
=\sqrt{2mhc\!\left(\frac{1}{\lambda_i}-\frac{1}{\lambda_0}\right)},
\]
and
\[
\boxed{\;\lambda_e
=\frac{h}{p}
=\sqrt{\frac{h}{2mc\!\left(\dfrac{1}{\lambda_i}-\dfrac{1}{\lambda_0}\right)}}\;}
\]

Explanation

Wave theory of light successfully explains:

  • Reflection: Bouncing of light off surfaces.
  • Refraction: Bending of light when it passes from one medium to another.
  • Diffraction: Bending and spreading of waves around obstacles.
