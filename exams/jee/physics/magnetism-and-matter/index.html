Q2

The relationship between the magnetic susceptibility (χ) and the magnetic permeability (μ) is given by :
( μ0 is the permeability of free space and μr is relative permeability)

[JEE Main 2025, 2 Apr (Shift 1)]

a

χ=μμ0-1

b

χ=μrμ0+1

c

χ=μr+1

d

χ=1-μμ0

🔒
Answer & explanation — Premium

Unlock this and thousands more solved PYQs.

Unlock · ₹149
✓ Correct answer: a)

χ=μμ0-1

Explanation

Relation between relative permeability and magnetic susceptibility:
μr = (1+χ)
Expressing susceptibility in terms of relative permeability:
χ = μr − 1
Relation between permeability and relative permeability:
μ = μ0 μr
Substituting for relative permeability:
μr = μ μ0
Final Answer:
χ = ( μ μ0 − 1 )

(A) is correct but (R) is not correct
