aLaw of diminishing marginal product
bLaw of variable proportions
cThe Short Run
dThe Long Run
✓ Correct answer: b) Law of variable proportions
ExplanationThe law of variable proportions explains that when one factor of production is increased while others are held constant, the marginal product of that variable factor will initially increase.However, as more of the variable factor is employed, there comes a point where the marginal product begins to decline.This occurs due to factors such as limited space, equipment, or other fixed resources that can't be easily expanded.The law illustrates the idea that there's an optimal level of input usage beyond which efficiency decreases.This phenomenon is crucial for understanding production decisions and resource allocation in the short run.Hence, the correct answer is "Law of variable proportions."

Which of the following is represented by 'The Lorenz Curve'?
aEmployment
bInflation
cDeflation
dIncome Distribution
✓ Correct answer: d) Income Distribution
ExplanationThe Lorenz curve is a graphical tool used to represent the distribution of income or wealth within a population.It was created by Max O. Lorenz in 1905 to show economic inequality.The curve plots the cumulative percentage of total income received against the cumulative percentage of recipients, starting from the poorest to the richest.A perfectly equal income distribution would be represented by a 45-degree line, known as the line of equality.The more the Lorenz curve bows away from the line of equality, the greater the inequality.Hence, the correct answer is "Income Distribution."
