In the following question, out of the given four alternatives, select the one which is opposite in meaning of the given word. Quash
aSupport
bRevoke
cAnnul
dCancel
✓ Correct answer: a) Support
ExplanationThe Correct Answer: Option A – Support Quash: To reject or void, especially by legal procedure; to put an end to; suppress.(हिंदी अर्थ: अस्वीकार करना या रद्द करना, विशेष रूप से कानूनी प्रक्रिया द्वाारा; समाप्त करना; दबाना।) Let's learn the meaning of the given options: Option A: Support – To bear all or part of the weight; to hold up; to give assistance, encouragement, or approval to.(हिंदी अर्थ: पूरा या आंशिक वजन उठाना; संभालना; सहायता, प्रोोत्साहन, या अनुमोदन देना।) Option B: Revoke – To officially cancel (a decree, decision, or promise).(हिंदी अर्थ: आधिकारिक तौर पर रद्द करना (एक डिक्री, निर्णय, या वादा)।) Option C: Annul – To declare invalid (an official agreement, decision, or result).(हिंदी अर्थ: अमान्य घोषित करना (एक आधिकारिक समझौता, निर्णय, या परिणाम)।) Option D: Cancel – To decide or announce that (a planned event) will not take place; to revoke or annul.(हिंदी अर्थ: तय करना या घोषणा करना कि (एक नियोजित घटना) नहीं होगी; रद्द करना।) Since Support means to give assistance or approval, it is the correct antonym of Quash (which means to suppress or void).Additional Notes Wor d Meaning (अर्थ) Synonym s (पर्यायवाची) Antonyms (विलोम) Qua sh रद्द करना / दबाना annul, revoke, suppress support, uphold, validate Sup por t समर्थन करना / सहारा देना uphold, back, endorse quash, oppose, reject Rev oke रद्द करना / वापस लेना cancel, annul, repeal confirm, uphold, enforce Ann ul रद्द करना / अमान्य घोषित करना nullify, invalidate, cancel validate, confirm, uphold Can cel रद्द करना call off, revoke, annul confirm, maintain, continue
