Q1 FREE PREVIEW
The World's largest island is
aGreenland
bIceland
cNew Guinea
dMadagascar
✓ Correct answer: a) Greenland
ExplanationGreenland is officially the world’s largest island that is not a continent.Home to 56,000 people, Greenland has its extensive local government, but it is also part of the Realm of Denmark.Greenland was a Danish colony until 1953 when it was redefined as a district of Denmark.In addition to its local government, Greenland has two representatives in the Danish Parliament, the Folketing.Hence, the correct answer is 'Greenland'.
Q2 FREE PREVIEW
The country which tops in the production of cocoa is __________ .
aGhana
bBrazil
cIvory Coast
dNigeria
✓ Correct answer: c) Ivory Coast
ExplanationIvory Coast (Côte d'Ivoire) is the largest producer of cocoa in the world.It contributes around 40% of global cocoa production.Ghana is the second-largest producer.Brazil and Nigeria also produce cocoa, but on a smaller scale compared to Ivory Coast.Hence, the correct answer is "Ivory Coast".
Q3 FREE PREVIEW
Sea water is saltier than rain water because
aSea animals are salt producing
bThe air around the sea is saltish
cRivers wash away salts from earth and pour them into the sea
dSea beds have salt producing mines
✓ Correct answer: c) Rivers wash away salts from earth and pour them into the sea
ExplanationRainwater is pure but when it flows as rivers, it dissolves salts and minerals from rocks and soil.These dissolved salts are carried by rivers into the sea continuously over millions of years.Since water evaporates from the sea but salt does not, the salt concentration keeps increasing.Hence, the correct answer is "Rivers wash away salts from earth and pour them into the sea."
Q4 FREE PREVIEW
Bhutan does not share its border with which Indian state?
aWest Bengal
bSikkim
cMeghalaya
dArunachal Pradesh
✓ Correct answer: c) Meghalaya
ExplanationBhutan shares its border with four Indian states like Arunachal Pradesh, West Bengal, Sikkim, and Assam.The Indo-Bhutan border extends to 699 km.The Indo-Bhutan border was established by the Treaty of Sinchula on November 11, 1865. Only land access to Bhutan is through the border between these two countries in the district of Jaigaon, and Alipurduar in West Bengal.Capital of Bhutan in Thimphu.Hence, the correct answer is “Meghalaya”.
Q5 FREE PREVIEW
In April 2017, diplomatic ties were re- established after 37 years between Cuba and________.
aIndia
bMorocco
cSri Lanka
dUSA
✓ Correct answer: b) Morocco
ExplanationCuba and Morocco in the third week of April 2017 signed a contract to re- establish diplomatic relations after a gap of 37 years.The statement was made by the Cuban Foreign Ministry.Havana, Spanish La Habana, city, capital, major port, and leading commercial center of Cuba, and the Cuban Convertible Peso is the currency of Cuba.It is a country located in the Greater Antilles.Morocco is a Northern African country and is located in the extreme northwest of Africa on the edge of continental Europe.The currency in Morocco is the Moroccan dirham and Rabat is the capital city.Hence, the correct answer is “ Morocco”.
Q6 FREE PREVIEW
Which among the following country is not a part of Scandinavia?
aNorway
bFinland
cSweden
dDenmark
✓ Correct answer: b) Finland
ExplanationScandinavia is a subregion in Northern Europe, That has strong historical, cultural, and linguistic ties between its constituent peoples.In general there are 3 main Scandinavian countries.These countries are Norway, Sweden, and Denmark.Term Scandinavia comes from a region in southern Sweden known as Scania.Term ‘Norden’ refers to five countries: Sweden, Denmark, Finland, Iceland, Norway, and Faroe island.Hence, the correct answer is “Finland”.
Q7 FREE PREVIEW
Masai is a tribe of which of the following country?
aKenya
bGermany
cAustralia
dIndia
✓ Correct answer: a) Kenya
ExplanationMasai are tribes of Northern and Central Kenya and Northern Tanzania.They are a nomadic and pastoralist tribe.Their origin is in the lower Nile valley north of Lake Turkana.Their beadwork culture signifies their beauty, tradition, strength, and social status.They believe in the ‘Enkai’ God.They speak the Massai language.Tribes of Germany are Franks, Angles, Saxons, Alamanni, etc. Tribes of Australia are Koori, Murri, Nunga, and Noongar.Tribes of India are Gond, Khond, Sahariya etc. Hence the correct answer is “Kenya”.
