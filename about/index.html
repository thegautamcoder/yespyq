About YESPYQ

Updated July 2026

YESPYQ is a practice platform for previous year questions (PYQs) across India's major competitive and school exams — UPSC, JEE, NEET, SSC, Defence exams and Boards. We believe the smartest way to crack any exam is to master the questions it has already asked — so we took thousands of authentic past questions, cleaned them up, organised them subject-wise and year-wise, and paired every one with a clear answer and explanation.

Exams we cover

  • UPSC Civil Services (IAS) Prelims — General Studies Paper-I, subject-wise and year-wise.
  • JEE — Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics PYQs.
  • NEET — Physics, Chemistry and Biology PYQs.
  • SSC CGL — Quantitative Aptitude, Reasoning and General Awareness.
  • Defence exams (NDA/CDS) and school Boards, with more exams being added regularly.

YESPYQ by the numbers

  • 12,000+ authentic PYQs across UPSC, JEE, NEET, SSC and more.
  • Multiple subjects per exam — Polity, History, Geography, Economy, Environment, Science & Technology and Current Affairs for UPSC; Physics, Chemistry, Biology and Maths for JEE/NEET; and more.
  • Decades of past papers, browsable year by year.
  • Every question solved — correct answer plus a detailed explanation.

What makes YESPYQ different

  • Only real PYQs. Every question actually appeared in a past exam paper and is tagged with its subject, exam and year — no mock filler.
  • One question at a time. A focused, gamified quiz mode with XP, combos and daily streaks keeps practice engaging without overwhelming you.
  • Answers that teach. Explanations focus on the concept being tested, not just the right option.
  • Free to start, everywhere. A generous free preview needs no login, works offline as a mobile app (PWA), with light and dark modes. PYQ Pass unlocks the full bank across every exam for ₹149/year.

How it works

  • 1. Pick your exam — browse all exams, or jump into a quick 10-question quiz.
  • 2. Solve & learn — answer, see instant feedback, and read the explanation.
  • 3. Build the habit — earn XP and keep your streak going, day after day.

Why previous year questions?

PYQs are the most reliable guide to how an exam actually thinks — the depth it tests, the way it frames options, and the themes it returns to year after year. Practising them is the single highest-return activity in exam preparation, whatever exam you're preparing for. Learn the method in our PYQ strategy guide, or read how to become an IAS officer.

Our commitment

A meaningful set of PYQs on YESPYQ will always stay free, with no login required. We are an independent study tool and are not affiliated with or endorsed by UPSC, NTA, SSC, IBPS or any other examination authority. Each exam referenced here is conducted solely by its respective official body.
