What you can do on YESPYQ

  • Browse the PYQ library. Filter by exam, subject, chapter and year. Every question shows four options; answers and explanations unlock with Pass (with a free preview in every chapter).
  • UPSC quiz mode. Run a focused interactive quiz for Civil Services Prelims GS, one question at a time.
  • PYQ Mock Test. Pick one exam, mix in subjects and years, and build a custom set of 5 to 100 questions from the homepage.
  • Real Exam Mode. Sit the same set as a timed test with a question grid and a full result report.
  • Guides & blog. Short explainers and strategy notes on Blog and Guides to support revision.

Free practice and PYQ Pass

PYQ Pass is a one-time ₹149 payment for 12 months (not a subscription). It unlocks answers and explanations across every exam, plus full practice features including Mock Test and Real Exam Mode. Why previous year questions

PYQs show how an exam actually frames options, which themes it revisits, and how deep it goes. Practising them is one of the highest-return habits in exam prep. YESPYQ exists to make that habit easy: pick an exam, open a subject, solve, read why, move on.
